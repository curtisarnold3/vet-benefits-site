Three ideas that run this whole site

1

Save your date first

An Intent to File (VA Form 21-0966) locks in your start date for one year. Back pay runs from that date, not from the day you finish the paperwork. Deadlines explained.

2

Every condition needs three legs

A current diagnosis, proof of how bad it is, and a doctor's statement that links it to your service (the nexus). Missing one leg is the most common reason claims lose. The three-leg gate.

3

A rating is a key, not a finish line

Ratings unlock health care, education for your kids, property tax breaks, hiring preference, and more. Most veterans never claim half of it. The rating ladder.
