# Mississippi (MS) - disabled veteran benefits research, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Mississippi Veterans Affairs (MSVA), https://www.msva.ms.gov/ (guidebook "State Benefits for Veterans" 2024-2025 PDF; web page https://www.msva.ms.gov/state-benefits, copyright 2026, no last-updated date shown).

## 1. Homestead exemption (property tax)
- Source: https://www.dor.ms.gov/county-services/homestead-exemption (no updated date shown). Quote: "Applicants classified as service-connected, total disability as an American veteran who has been honorably discharged from military service and their unremarried surviving spouses" are in "TIER 3: Total Exemption ... exempt from all property taxes on their qualifying homesteads."
- Filing: "Homestead Exemption Applications are only accepted in the county Tax Assessor's office ... between January 1 and April 1 of each year." Must "own and occupy their home as their primary residence on January 1"; ownership instrument filed with Chancery Clerk "prior to January 7th." Re-apply only if status changes; "change of eligibility status for disability benefits or Veterans Affairs benefits" can trigger re-application.
- Statute text (Miss. Code 27-33-67(2)(a), as reproduced in SB 3120 (2026) as introduced, https://billstatus.ls.state.ms.us/documents/2026/html/SB/3100-3199/SB3120IN.htm): "Service-connected, total disability as an American veteran who has been honorably discharged from military service." Bill died in committee 2/25/2026.
- Rule: 35 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. VI, R. 3.03 (https://www.sos.ms.gov/adminsearch/ACCode/00000161c.pdf, 35.VI.1.x revised effective September 4, 2025): "an applicant will also qualify for the total disability exemption if the applicant is an honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected total disability or is the unremarried surviving spouse of such a veteran"; proof includes "Veteran's Consent of Release (Form 72-042)."
- Rating issue: no statute text names "permanent," "P&T," or "TDIU." DOR FAQ (https://www.dor.ms.gov/county-services/property-tax-frequently-asked-questions) still says disabled persons are exempt on "the first $75,000 of true value" and DOR's own Tier 2 text says "$7,500 of assessed value"; that is the ordinary disability tier, not the veteran Tier 3. The DOR FAQ is internally inconsistent with the main page; I used the main homestead page.
- Disallowance: failure to file/pay Mississippi income tax, nonresident filing, improper vehicle registration, claiming on two properties, and failure to re-apply.
- 2026: HB 420 (https://billstatus.ls.state.ms.us/2026/pdf/history/HB/HB0420.xml): "Approved by Governor" 03/20; effective July 1, 2026; amends 27-33-75. Quote from the signed text: "From and after January 1, 2027, a qualified homeowner who is an American veteran who has been honorably discharged from military service and has reached eighty-five (85) years of age ... shall be allowed an exemption from all ad valorem taxes." Existing 90+ rule continues until then; unremarried surviving spouses covered.
- Varies by county: administration and documentation by county Tax Assessor.

## 2. Disabled American Veteran plates and tax exemption
- Source (statute, official legislature): SB 2052 as sent to governor, 2025, approved 03/12/2025, effective July 1, 2025, https://billstatus.ls.state.ms.us/documents/2025/html/SB/2001-2099/SB2052SG.htm. Quote: "rated as having one hundred percent (100%) permanent service-connected disability or at least seventy percent (70%) nonpermanent service-connected disability ... is privileged to purchase annually under this subsection two (2) motor vehicle license plates ... for the sum of One Dollar ($1.00) in total cost for each plate" plus one motorcycle plate; "Vehicles and motorcycles owned by such person are exempt under this subsection from all ad valorem and privilege taxes"; "(c) This section pertains only to taxes or plates for private passenger motor vehicles or pickup trucks or motorcycles"; 70% nonpermanent must provide "an updated benefits letter" to renew; a third vehicle may get a tag "upon payment of the road and bridge privilege taxes, ad valorem taxes and registration fees." Surviving spouse keeps while unmarried.
- DOR FAQ (https://www.dor.ms.gov/motor-vehicle/motor-vehicle-frequently-asked-questions): verification from the Veterans Affairs Board of "100% permanent service-related disability, or 70% non-permanent"; up to 2 vehicles and 1 motorcycle; "$1.00 fee applies for each plate." Page DOR node 691: Code 27-19-53, tag fee $1.00.
- Conflict: MSVA state-benefits page still says "(100%) ... permanent and total ... or ... individual unemployability and evaluated as permanent and total" (older text). The 2025 statute governs; TDIU is not named in the statute.
- Parking: DOR FAQ says disability parking is a separate program (2 plates and 2 placards for eligible persons); no veteran-specific parking found.
- Surviving-spouse bill SB 2864 (2026) "clarify privilege tax not paid on $1 tags" died in committee.

## 3. Hunting and fishing
- Source: https://www.mdwfp.com/licenses-permits/fishing-license-requirements. Quote: "Residents who are visually impaired, paraplegic, a multiple-amputee, adjudged totally disabled by the Social Security Administration or totally service connected disabled by the Veterans Administration are not required to purchase a HUNTING OR FISHING LICENSE. [MS. Code 49-7-5 and 49-7-9]" and must carry proof.
- Optional card: https://www.mdwfp.com/sites/default/files/2024-07/Exempt%20License%20Application.pdf (Revised 7/2024): "THE FEE FOR AN EXEMPT LICENSE IS $5.79"; "Veterans Administration - 100% service connected"; "must obtain letter from VA stating 100% service connected disability." (Older 2024-05 form said $5.00.)
- Statute text 49-7-5 quoted in SB 2764 (2026 introduced, died 02/03/2026).
- No MDWFP lifetime/discount license for lower ratings found. HB 370 (2024) idea of $1 license for 70% rating not verified as enacted; not included.

## 4. State parks and lakes
- https://www.mdwfp.com/parks-destinations/state-park-fees and 40 Miss. Admin. Code Pt. 6, R. 1.1 (June 2025 version): "15% discount off the camping and lodging rate for 62+/Disabled/Veterans/Active Service Members (excluding Holidays and Special Events)"; "Proof of status required at check in"; disabled proof is "Veterans Administration Letter stating '100% Service Connected Disability.'" Annual entrance pass $50 (no veteran discount found). Cabins/lodging: same 15%, excluding Buccaneer Gulf View sites.
- https://www.mdwfp.com/fishing-boating/state-fishing-lake-permit-fees (Copyright 2023-2026): exempt permits "for persons age 62 or older, 100% disabled, active service members or military veterans": annual boat launch $100 vs $125; fishing permit $35 vs $55; camping 1 day $22 vs $25.

## 5. Education
- Official MSVA page https://www.msva.ms.gov/state-benefits: "Any Veteran, as defined by Title 38 ... or a person entitled to education benefits under Title 38 ... is authorized in-state tuition. Miss. Code Ann., Sec. 37-103-25." Dependents: only "Children of any member of the armed services ... prisoner of war or missing in action" eight-semester scholarship (37-108-1). National Guard tuition via Adjutant General.

- MSVA State Approving Agency: (601) 576-4867 (https://www.msva.ms.gov/educationvocational-services).
- Mississippi State's nonresident waiver page is a university page, not used for the rows.

## 6. Income tax
- https://www.dor.ms.gov/individual/individual-income-tax-frequently-asked-questions: "Mississippi does not tax benefits received from U.S. Social Security, Railroad Retirement Public Welfare assistance, Veterans' Administration payments or workers' compensation." "Generally, retirement income, pensions and annuities are not subject to Mississippi Income tax if the recipient has met the retirement plan requirements. Early distributions ... may be subject to tax." Guard/Reserve: "The first $15,000 of salary ... is excluded from income." Combat-zone pay exempt. Tax is 4.4% above $10,000 per FAQ.
- 2025 Form 80-100-25 instructions (https://www.dor.ms.gov/sites/default/files/tax-forms/individual/80100251%202.pdf, Rev. 12/25): "retirement income from federal, state, and private retirement systems are exempt in total"; HB 1 (eff. July 1, 2025) lowers rate on income over $10,000 to 3.75% (2027), 3.5% (2028), 3.25% (2029), 3% (2030+). "Separation pay is not retirement income and does not qualify for exemption."

## 7. Employment preference
- https://www.mspb.ms.gov/information-veterans: "disabled veterans and veterans are listed at the top of the list with disabled veterans listed before veterans."
- MSPB Policy and Procedures Manual effective 3/1/2025, Sec. 4.6: "Disabled Veteran: A veteran whom the Veterans Administration has certified to have a service-connected disability rating of at least thirty percent (30%) within the last ninety (90) days, or a veteran who was awarded the Purple Heart ... Miss. Code 25-9-301(b)." RIF scoring: "Veterans shall be awarded four (4) points, and disabled veterans shall be awarded eight (8) points." (https://mspb.ms.gov/sites/mspb/files/MSPB_File/Resources%20for%20HR/Policies/FY%202025%20Policies/3.1.25%20Policies%20for%20Newsletter/MSPB%20FY%202025%20Policy%20and%20Procedures%20Manual%20effective%203.1.25%20Final%20Version%20with%20cover.pdf)
