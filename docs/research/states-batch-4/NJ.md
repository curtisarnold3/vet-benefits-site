# New Jersey - disabled veteran benefits research (verified 2026-10-17)

Agency: NJ Department of Veterans Affairs, https://veterans.nj.gov/ (DMAVA split into two departments Jan. 9, 2026; legacy pages at nj.gov/dva/ still live).

## Property tax
- 100% Disabled Veteran exemption. Source: https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/lpt/lpt-disabledvet.shtml (Last Updated 08/12/25). Quote: "100% permanently and totally disabled during active duty service"; must "Provide a U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs ... certification of active duty service-connected disability stating you are 100% permanently and totally disabled." Claim form D.V.S.S.E. (rev. March 2025), https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/other_forms/lpt/dvsse.pdf: box 4C "Service-connected disability rated unemployable with payment of 100% and stated to be totally and permanently disabling." File with municipal assessor any time; appeal Form A-1 to County Board by April 1 (Jan. 15 Burlington/Gloucester/Monmouth). Wartime requirement removed Dec. 4, 2020 (P.L.2019 c.413, https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2018/PL19/413_.HTM). Statute text: "total or 100% permanent disability, and not so evaluated solely because of hospitalization or surgery and recuperation, sustained through enemy action, or accident, or resulting from disease contracted while in such active service."
- Surviving spouse: same pages; unremarried, owns and occupies; also spouse of service member who died on active duty.
- $250 deduction: https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/lpt/lpt-veterans.shtml (Last Updated 08/12/25). "Active duty for training is ineligible." Form V.S.S., Oct. 1 ownership date. Conflict: NJDVA Gold Star page (updated 10/02/2026) still says wartime service required.

## Vehicle
- Free registration: https://www.nj.gov/mvc/drivertopics/veterans.htm. Quote: "Are without sight as a result of war service..." or "qualified for a free automobile from the US Veterans Administration" plus special equipment, NJ insurance, NJ license. Call 609-292-6500 ext. 5076.
- Sales tax: Consumer Automotive Tax Guide Q49, https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/documents/pdf/guides/New-Jersey-Consumer-Automotive-Tax-Guide.pdf: exempt only for "funds ... from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), or the New Jersey Department of Military and Veterans Affairs"; out-of-pocket portion taxed. MVC taxexempt page agrees.
- Placard: https://www.nj.gov/mvc/vehicletopics/disvet.htm (N.J.S.A. 39:4-207.10), meters up to 24 hours, DD-214 + VA Award of Disability letter, renew every 3 years.
- DV plates: https://www.nj.gov/mvc/vehicles/military.htm lists "DV1000-DV9999 Disabled Vet", Disabled American Veterans $15; Form SP-23 says DD-214 plus "Award of Disability percentage letter" and "Certify the applicant is a member." Minimum rating and membership requirement unclear.

## Recreation
- Hunting/fishing: official PDF https://www.nj.gov/dep/fgw/pdf/disvetli.pdf: "service-connected disability of any degree"; six months NJ residency; mail certification. Live page https://dep.nj.gov/njfw/licenses-and-permits/disabled-veteran-license-information/ blocked my direct fetch (Incapsula); content read only through search excerpts (unlimited antlerless permits, no lottery preference, certification invalid if you leave NJ).
- Parks: no veteran pass found. DEP pass page and Island Beach permit page also blocked; read only through search excerpts. Disability Pass is not veteran specific.
- Beaches: N.J.S.A. 40:61-22.20 (P.L.2019 c.398, https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2018/PL19/398_.HTM) lets shore municipalities give free or reduced beach fees to veterans (90 days active duty or service-incurred discharge); local option, left out of JSON.

## Education
- NJTEAM Act, P.L.2015 c.32 and P.L.2019 c.264 (https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2018/PL19/264_.HTM): "shall be regarded as a resident of the State for the purpose of determining tuition."
- Veterans Tuition Credit ($400/yr, 12/31/1960-5/7/1975 service), War Orphan ($500/yr), POW-MIA free tuition: NJ Veterans Benefits Guide (PDF created April 2026) https://www.nj.gov/dva/assets/documents/NJ%20Veterans%20Benefits%20Guide.pdf.

## Income tax
- NJ has an income tax. Military pension and SBP not taxable: https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/military/taxinformation.shtml (Last Updated 04/30/25): "U.S. military pension and survivor's benefit payments are not taxable."
- $6,000 exemption: https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/military/vetexemption.shtml.

## Employment
- Civil service preference: https://www.nj.gov/dva/veterans/services/civil-service-preference/ (Last Updated 10/02/2026). Absolute preference; disabled veteran needs "at least a 10% disability compensation."

## Other
- Catastrophic Entitlement $150/month: https://www.nj.gov/dva/veterans/benefits-resources/ (Last Updated 10/06/2026), https://www.nj.gov/dva/veterans/services/gold-star-families/.
- Memorial Homes (Last Updated 10/13/2026) https://www.nj.gov/dva/veterans/memorial-homes/ and Doyle cemetery https://www.nj.gov/dva/veterans/services/cemetery/index.shtml.
