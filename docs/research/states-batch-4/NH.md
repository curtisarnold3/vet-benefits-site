# New Hampshire - disabled veteran benefits research (verified 2026-10-17)

Statute pages are from gc.nh.gov (official NH General Court RSA text; the pages show source/amendment history, no "last updated" date). The agency page (nhveterans.nh.gov) shows no last-updated date and lists some outdated amounts. DRA (revenue.nh.gov) blocked direct fetch except via search results.

## Property tax (credits set by each town; varies_by_county true)
- RSA 72:35 (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/V/72/72-35.htm; amended 2025, 15:1, eff. July 13, 2025; 2026, 252:7): "shall receive a standard yearly tax credit in the amount of $700 of property taxes on the person's residential property." "The optional tax credit ... shall be an amount from $701 up to $5,000 ... shall replace the tax credits pursuant to RSA 72:28, 72:28-b, 72:28-c, and 72:36-a in its entirety." Proof: "certification from the United States Department of Veterans' Affairs that the applicant is rated totally and permanently disabled from service connection." Principal place of abode only. TDIU is not named.
- RSA 72:36-a (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/V/72/72-36-a.htm): SAH/SHA home owners "exempt from all taxation on said homestead" if "100 percent permanently and totally disabled as prescribed in 38 C.F.R. 3.340, total and permanent total ratings and unemployability", or double amputee/paraplegic, or blind both eyes 5/200. Surviving spouse covered.
- RSA 72:28 (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/V/72/72-28.htm): "The standard veterans' tax credit shall be $50." Optional "from $51 up to $750" until April 1, 2027, then "up to $1,000" (2026, 96:1). Requires 90 days active service in listed war/conflict; resident one year before April 1 (RSA 72:29, I).
- RSA 72:28-b (all veterans' credit, town option): same amount as the town's 72:28 credit; not available if eligible for 72:28 or 72:35.
- RSA 72:28-c (combat credit, town option): $50-$500, to $750 April 1, 2027.
- RSA 72:33 (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/V/72/72-33.htm): "filed with the selectmen or assessors, by April 15 preceding the setting of the tax rate, a permanent application"; move -> amended application "on or before December 1". Late relief for "accident, mistake, or misfortune" only before tax rate approved.
- RSA 72:27-a: town meeting/council vote adopts credits and sets amounts.
- RSA 72:29: surviving spouse excludes remarried (regained after divorce); revocable-trust ownership counts.
- Agency page (https://www.nhveterans.nh.gov/veterans-services/nh-state-benefits-veterans) says optional 72:35 credit "up to $2000" - stale versus the statute.
- 2026 bill HB 1659 (new homestead credit for 100% veterans) was reported killed in the House; not law (secondary report, not confirmed on gc.nh.gov).

## Vehicle
- RSA 261:86 (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXI/261/261-86.htm): plates for "one motor vehicle" for a veteran "evaluated by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs to be permanently and totally disabled from such service-connected disability", with accessibility symbol; "initial set ... issued without charge"; may opt for the RSA 261:87-b veterans plate free but then no "free parking privileges."
- RSA 261:141, VIII: "No fee shall be charged for registering a vehicle owned by any person who ... evaluated by the VA to be permanently and totally disabled".
- RSA 263:42, III (driver's license fee): same P&T standard.
- Free parking (RSA 265:73) and RSA 261:157/159 per agency page only.

## Recreation
- RSA 214:13 (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XVIII/214/214-13.htm; last amended 2019, 161:1): "totally and permanently disabled ... special veteran's license ... perpetual ... A $10 administrative fee." 80%-under-100%: lifetime license "at 50 percent of the cost of a lifetime license."
- RSA 216-A:3-g, IV (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XIX/216-A/216-A-3-g.htm): "No disabled veteran of this state, upon providing satisfactory proof of a service-connected disability, shall be charged a fee for day-use admission"; enterprise activities charged. Para VI adds RSA 261:75-c special-plate vehicles free day use but metered parking charged.

## Education
- RSA 187-A:20, II (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XV/187-A/187-A-20.htm): child of a veteran "totally and permanently disabled" attends a university system institution tuition-free; VA letter "100 percent total, permanent, service connected"; eligible "through the end of the semester in which he or she attains the age of 27"; FAFSA required.
- RSA 193:19-21 and 188-F:15 described only on the agency page.

## Income tax
- DRA: Interest and Dividends Tax repealed for periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025 (https://www.revenue.nh.gov/news-and-media/repeal-nh-interest-and-dividends-tax-now-effect; seen via search snippet). Agency page: no general state income tax.

## Employment
- RSA 283:4, II (https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XXIII/283/283-4.htm; 2026, 252:2): "preference in hiring shall be given to a veteran ... one of multiple candidates who are otherwise equally qualified." No points.

## 2026 law
- SB 448, 2026 ch. 252 (eff. Aug. 31, 2026): replaces "honorable discharge" with RSA 21:50 definition (includes general under honorable conditions) in many statutes. Source line shown on RSA 72:35, 72:36-a, 261:86, 283:4. Text read at bills.nhliberty.org (unofficial; official statutes confirm the amendments).
- HB 1494, 2026 ch. 96: raises optional credit caps April 1, 2027 (shown in RSA 72:28 source line).
