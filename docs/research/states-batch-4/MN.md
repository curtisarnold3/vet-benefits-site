# Minnesota - verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Minnesota Department of Veterans Affairs, https://www.mn.gov/mdva/ (mn.gov pages are behind a bot wall; MDVA pages could not be read directly).

## Property tax - Minn. Stat. 273.13 subd. 34 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/273.13)
- "if the veteran has a service-connected disability of 70 percent or more" (honorably discharged). 70%+: $150,000 excluded; "for a total (100 percent) and permanent disability, $300,000 of market value is excluded."
- Surviving spouse: exclusion "shall carry over to the benefit of the veteran's spouse"; spouse must hold title and live there; ends on remarriage or disposal.
- Caregiver: "the homestead of the veteran's primary family caregiver, if any, is eligible" (veteran must own no MN homestead).
- Deadline (para h): apply "by December 31 of the first assessment year for which the exclusion is sought." Not combinable with the subd. 35 or subd. 22(b). Farm: house, garage, one acre.
- No TDIU language. No 2024/2025 amendment to subd. 34 (last: 2023 c 64). Hint amounts ($150k/$300k) confirmed; no increase enacted (bills SF 185, SF 772, HF 3727 only introduced/engrossed).
- Revenue page (https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/market-value-exclusion-veterans-disability, updated "September 09, 2026"): "Apply to your county assessor's office by December 31 to qualify for taxes payable in the next year"; "Own and occupy a homesteaded property on December 31"; DIC spouse "Veteran's disability status is not a factor." Some county forms show earlier dates (Dec 15).
- Hint "8-year surviving spouse rule": NOT found in the statute text read.

## Vehicle - Minn. Stat. 168.012 subd. 13 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/168.012)
- "A passenger automobile, one-ton pickup truck, motorcycle, or recreational vehicle registered by a veteran with a total service-connected disability"; exempts registration taxes, administrative fees, filing fees/surcharges, plate and sticker fees; "no more than two motor vehicles at the same time"; personalized/special-plate donations not exempt. New 2023 (2023 c 68), amended 2025 (2025 c 20 s 154).
- 171.01 subd. 51: "demonstrates that the veteran has received a 100 percent total and permanent service-connected disability rating."
- Not confirmed: DVS application steps, parking privileges, plate details.

## Hunting/fishing/parks
- DNR https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/licenses/military/print.html (no date shown, (c) 2026): "With proof of a 100% service-related disability": free small game (code 241), one free deer license/yr, free permanent angling license (St. Paul License Center only, no trout stamp); federal duck stamp still required; 100% gets military preference in lotteries. "With any level of service-related disability are eligible to receive a free annual state park vehicle permit."
- Minn. Stat. 97A.441 subds. 5, 6, 6a: "100 percent service-connected disability as defined by the United States Veterans Administration."
- Minn. Stat. 85.053 subd. 10: "The commissioner shall issue an annual park permit for no charge to any veteran with any level of service-connected disability"; subd. 8 Purple Heart and active military.

## Education
- MN GI Bill, Minn. Stat. 197.791 (https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/197.791): "$5,000 per state fiscal year" and "$15,000 in a lifetime"; vets "serving or has served honorably in any branch"; MN resident; surviving spouse/child of member who died from service; subd. 4(a)(1)(iv) spouse/child of member with total and permanent disability. Amended 2025 c 30, 2026 c 49. Hint "$3,000/yr" is wrong.
- Survivors and Dependents Education, 197.75: tuition-free "until the person has attained a bachelor's or equivalent degree" for eligible child/spouse of a deceased veteran; stipend "$750 each year." Amended 2025 c 30 and 2026 c 49 (new text not read).

## Income tax
- 290.0132 subd. 21: "compensation received from a pension or other retirement pay from the federal government for service in the military ... is a subtraction. The subtraction is limited to individuals who do not claim the credit under section 290.0677." No dollar cap; no income limit stated.
- 290.0677: credit "equals $750," "reduced by ten percent of adjusted gross income in excess of $30,000"; qualified: 20+ years, 100% rating, or certain retirement pay.
- Minnesota has a state income tax. VA compensation: no MN page found.

## Employment
- 197.455: 10 points nondisabled veteran; "a credit of 15 points" disabled (open); "a credit of five points" promotional (permanent 50%+). 43A.11: disabled veterans listed ahead of all others; "The top five recently separated veterans must be granted an interview." Hint "5/10" is out of date.

## Veterans homes
- 198.022: "must be without adequate means of support"; spouses at least 55; MN regiment or credited to Minnesota.
