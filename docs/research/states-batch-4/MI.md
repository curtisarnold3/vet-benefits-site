# Michigan veteran benefits research (2026-10-17)

Key finding: the hint that the disabled veterans property tax exemption was replaced by a credit is wrong. MCL 211.7b is current law (amended 2023 PA 150) and still fully exempts the homestead.

## Property tax exemption
- Source: https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-211-7b (MCL 211.7b; history through 2023 Act 150)
- "Real property used and owned as a homestead by either of the following individuals is exempt from the collection of taxes under this act: (a) A disabled veteran."
- "(i) Has been determined by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs to be permanently and totally disabled as a result of military service and entitled to veterans' benefits at the 100% rate. (ii) ... pecuniary assistance due to disability for specially adapted housing. (iii) Has been rated by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs as individually unemployable."
- "An exemption granted under this section as to taxes levied on or after January 1, 2025 remains in effect, without subsequent reapplication, until it is rescinded ... or is denied by the assessor."
- Application filed "with the assessing officer for the local assessing unit after January 1 and before December 31". Surviving spouse: continues "as long as the surviving spouse does not remarry"; title held solely by spouse.
- Form 5107 (Rev. 03-26): https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/treasury/Forms/Local-Government/PSD/STC/5107.pdf ; Treasury page https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/property/exemptions/homeowner/disabled-veteran-exemption (STC memo on Form 5107 approved April 7, 2026). MVAA advises filing in January or February: https://www.michigan.gov/mvaa/quality-of-life/quality-of-life/benefits-disabled-vet
- Pending, not law: HB 5885 (2026).

## MI-1040CR-2 credit
- https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/-/media/Project/Websites/taxes/Forms/IIT/TY2025/MI-1040CR2.pdf : "Veteran with service-connected disability"; "maximum $1,900"; "If greater than $165,400, STOP"; household resources "more than $71,500, STOP". Table 2 allowance by disability percent not retrieved for 2025.

## Plates
- https://www.michigan.gov/sos/-/media/Project/Websites/sos/Vehicle/License-Plates/Application-for-Veteran-License-plate-BDVR-87.pdf : "Permanent disabled veteran (Non-expiring registration. No registration fees apply.)" for "A 100% combined disability rating, or a disability rating of less than 100%, but an unemployable rating of 100%." "Standard disabled veteran (Regular registration fees apply...)" for "a disability rating of 50-90%." "Only one disabled veteran plate may be owned by an applicant." "A disabled veteran plate does not automatically qualify as a disability plate" (MV-110). MVAA says a one-time $5 administrative fee.

## Recreation Passport
- https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/faqs/recreation-passport/rp : "not required for any vehicle registered with the following Michigan license plates: Disabled veteran. Ex-POW or Medal of Honor. The exemption is tied specifically to the plate and registration and not to the person."

## Hunting and fishing
- https://www.michigan.gov/dnr/things-to-do/fishing/license-info : free "any hunting or fishing license that does not require a separate application" if "permanently and totally disabled ... 100% rate, for disabilities other than blindness" or "individually unemployable".

## Children of Veterans Tuition Grant
- https://www.michigan.gov/mistudentaid/programs/children-of-veterans-tuition-grant : "totally and permanently disabled due to a service-connected illness or injury"; age over 16 and under 26; max $2,800 per year, $11,200 / 8 semesters; 2026-27 deadline June 30, 2027.

## Tuition, income tax, hiring, other
- In-state tuition: https://www.michigan.gov/mvaa/quality-of-life/quality-of-life/state-of-michigan-veteran-benefits ("Veterans and active-duty members receive in-state tuition, regardless of their state of residency or duty status.")
- Income tax: https://www.michigan.gov/taxes/questions/iit/accordion/military/are-military-retirement-benefits-exempt-from-michigan-individual-income-tax ("exempt from Michigan individual income tax"). MVAA page: military disability retirement pay "not included in taxable income".
- Hiring: Civil Service Rule 3-8 (PDF dated 2009 on michigan.gov): 5 points veteran, 10 points disabled veteran; 5%/10% agency screening. Veterans_Preference.pdf on michigan.gov confirms 90 days and honorable discharge.
- MVTF: https://www.michigan.gov/mvaa/quality-of-life/emergency-assistance/panel-content ; Veteran Homes: https://www.michigan.gov/mvh/eligibility (from search summaries; pages not read in full).
