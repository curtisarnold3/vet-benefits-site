# Montana (MT) - research narrative, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Montana Veterans Affairs Division, https://veterans.mt.gov/ (hint URL dma.mt.gov/mvad returned 404). Hint "MCA 15-6-311, April 15, DV plates, MUS waiver, HB 252 recent arrivals" checked below; several hints were wrong or unconfirmed.

## Property tax (MDV)
- Source: https://revenue.mt.gov/property/property-tax-help/mdv (no last-updated date; Tax Year 2027). Quote: the veteran "must have 100% disability from an injury related to service." Reduction is 100/80/70/50% of the normal rate. TY2027 single: 100% to $49,654; 80% to $54,620; 70% to $59,584; 50% to $64,551. Married/HoH: $59,584 / $64,551 / $69,515 / $74,482. Unmarried surviving spouse: $41,379 / $46,344 / $51,310 / $56,276. Apply by April 15; late applications count for next year.
- Form MDV (V1 9/2026), https://revenue.mt.gov/files/Forms/Montana_Disabled_Veteran_Property_Tax_Relief_Application_Form_MDV.pdf: "A reduced property tax rate is available for 100% disabled veterans and the unmarried surviving spouses of deceased veterans who were 100% disabled." Home owned (or contract for deed) and lived in "for at least seven months of the year"; submit to "local Department of Revenue field office"; (406) 444-6900. If rating is temporary DOR periodically requests updated VA letter. Spouse income counts.
- Statute 15-6-311 MCA (https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0150/chapter_0060/part_0030/section_0110/0150-0060-0030-0110.html): VA letter that veteran "was rated 100% disabled or was paid at the 100% disabled rate." History through Ch. 583, L. 2023. TDIU treatment not stated.
- Legislative staff summary (Revenue Interim Committee, Nov 2025): 2026 limits single $48,152 (100%) to $62,598 (50%); 3,612 participants, avg benefit $1,913. Note this differs from the 2027 table (annual inflation adjustment).
- Not found: county assessor role (DOR handles it). Not county-varying.

## Vehicles
- 61-3-460 MCA (https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0610/chapter_0030/part_0040/section_0600/0610-0030-0040-0600.html): exempt from "all motor vehicle registration fees" for "two motor vehicles that are not used for commercial purposes"; (2)(b) veteran "currently rated 100% disabled" or paid at the 100% rate, or unremarried surviving spouse; (2)(c) 50%+ with Purple Heart (still pays veterans' cemetery fee). Two sets of special veteran plates. History 2003, 2005, 2019. (The hint's "one vehicle" is the older 61-3-332 language and is not current.)
- 61-3-332: combat-disabled veteran plates replaced when 10+ years old. No parking privilege found tied to veteran plates. MVD page mvdmt.gov blocked (403).

## Recreation
- https://fwp.mt.gov/buyandapply/hunting-licenses/disabled-licenses: "Veterans with certain combat-connected injuries may get half-priced deer and antelope licenses." "Up to 50 licenses will be issued annually." Resident disability conservation license $8 (half-cost deer, elk, fishing, upland bird). No free license for 100% veterans found. No date shown.
- https://fwp.mt.gov/stateparks/fees-and-general-information: resident veterans 50% off camping, excluding electrical; proof DD 214, VA ID or driver's license. References 2020-21 fee schedule; a 2026 fee proposal exists. No free park entry for disabled veterans found.

## Education
- https://www.mus.edu/Prepare/Pay/Waivers/index.html: Honorably Discharged Veteran Waiver, last-dollar up to in-state tuition; "Honorable Under General Conditions will not be accepted"; federal education benefits must be expired or exhausted; apply by third week of first semester. War Orphans Waiver: child 25 or younger of parent killed or died of combat-related injury; tuition only. 20-25-421 MCA (amended 2023, Ch. 455) lets regents waive for resident wartime veterans honorably discharged. No disability-based waiver and no waiver for dependents of living disabled veterans.

## Income tax
- Montana has an income tax. https://revenue.mt.gov/taxes/military: "Treat your income, including your military pension, like other income for tax purposes." Active-duty pay subtractable. Working Military Retirement Exemption (15-30-2120(3)(n)): lesser of Montana wage-type income or 50% of retirement pay, up to 5 consecutive years, new for TY2024; sunset removed by SB 93 (2025) per Revenue Interim Committee, https://archive.legmt.gov/content/Committees/Interim/2025-2026/RIC/Meetings/November_12_13_2025/veteran-tax-benefits.pdf (DOR form may still show 2033).

## Employment
- 39-29-102 MCA: "5 percentage points if the applicant is a veteran; and" "10 percentage points if the applicant is a disabled veteran or an eligible relative." No stacking; initial hiring; guaranteed interview possible by rule. 39-29-101 defines disabled veteran as having an established service-connected disability or Purple Heart.

## Other
- Veterans' homes (Columbia Falls, Butte, Glendive) listed at https://veterans.mt.gov/Veteran-Services/State-Benefits. Columbia Falls page lists 105 skilled/intermediate and 12 domiciliary beds; admission rules not found. $1,000 Purple Heart Scholarship (veterans.mt.gov).
