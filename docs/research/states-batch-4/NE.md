# Nebraska: disabled veteran benefits (verified 2026-10-17)

Agency: Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs, https://veterans.nebraska.gov/

## Property tax: homestead exemption (Neb. Rev. Stat. 77-3506)
URL: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-3506 (amended Laws 2025 LB209; Laws 2026 LB803, operative July 18, 2026)
Quote: "A veteran who was discharged or otherwise separated with a characterization of honorable or general (under honorable conditions), who is drawing compensation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs because of (i) one hundred percent service-connected permanent disability or (ii) assignment of total disability rating for compensation pursuant to 38 C.F.R. 4.16"
Also (2)(c): a veteran drawing compensation for "one hundred percent service-connected temporary disability" qualifies, but "Application ... shall be required annually and shall include certification ... from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs."
Amount: 77-3501.01(3): "exempt amount shall mean the taxable value of the homestead" (full value). 77-3506.03(3): the value-cap reduction "shall not apply to any exemption under section 77-3506." No income test in 77-3506 (income tables are in 77-3507 and 77-3508 only).
Deadline/office, 77-3512: file "with the county assessor ... after February 1 and on or before June 30 of each year", county board may extend to July 20; permanent categories "shall not be required to file an application in any subsequent year." Note the statute says after February 1, not "Feb 2" literally. Statute last amended by LB826 (effective April 15, 2026) and LB803 (July 18, 2026); I did not diff what those changed in 77-3512.
Homestead defined, 77-3502: residence and land "not exceeding one acre" occupied by owner of record "from January 1 through August 15".
Surviving spouse, 77-3506(2)(b),(d)-(f),(4): unremarried, or remarried after age 57; remarry before 57 loses it, notify assessor within 30 days.
Carve-out: veterans eligible for total exemption under 77-3526 to 77-3528 (paraplegic / multiple amputee with VA-contributed home).
Non-service-connected total disability, 77-3508(1)(b)(i): income-based percentage, https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-3508 . 
DOR page https://revenue.nebraska.gov/PAD/homestead-exemption lists Form 458 and Helpline (888) 475-5101; no last-updated date shown (only (c) 2026 State of Nebraska).

## Motor vehicle tax and fee exemption (new 2026)
URLs: https://veterans.nebraska.gov/disabledblind-veteran-motor-vehicle-tax-fee-exemption ; https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=60-3%2C185 (Laws 2025, LB650); definition 77-202.23
Quote (NDVA): "Starting January 1, 2026, all disabled and/or blind veterans in Nebraska can receive an exemption for the Motor Vehicle Tax and Motor Vehicle Fee for ONE vehicle owned and used for their personal transportation." Definition cites 5 U.S.C. 2108 "(covers those who are rated at 0%)".
Quote (statute 60-3,185(2)): "One motor vehicle owned and used for his or her personal transportation by a disabled veteran or blind veteran ... who was discharged or otherwise separated with a characterization of honorable". Note: statute says honorable only.
Apply: be in the NDVA Military and Veterans' Registry; claim at county treasurer within 30 days after purchase or before the final day of the expiration month; automatic on later renewals.

## Disabled Veteran plates and parking
URL: https://dmv.nebraska.gov/dvr/dav-plate
Quote: plates "available to any person who is a veteran ... honorable or general (under honorable conditions) and is 10% service-connected disabled." "There is no limit to the number of motor vehicles or trailers." "Disabled Veteran License Plates do not allow the registered owner to park in spaces designated for handicapped parking." Handicap version needs medical certification. Message plate $40. No page date shown.

## Hunting, fishing, parks
Official Game and Parks pages (outdoornebraska.gov) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so I used NDVA's own guide: https://veterans.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/NDVA%20Guide%20to%20Veterans%20Benefits.pdf (April 2026 Edition, p. 26) and https://veterans.nebraska.gov/recreation
Quote: "Disabled veterans who are legal residents of Nebraska and who have been deemed 50% or more service connected or 100% disabled non-service connected and receive a pension from the VA, are eligible for one (1) perpetual Disabled Veteran's Lifetime Annual Park Entry Permit at no fee." Hunt/fish/fur permit: same threshold, "at no fee. This permit includes all state stamps." Park permit: "An application form and signature from a veterans service officer is required."

## Tuition waiver (dependents)
URLs: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=80-411 ; https://veterans.nebraska.gov/waiver
Quote: waiver for a dependent whose parent, stepparent or spouse "Is permanently and totally disabled as a result of military service. Permanent and total disability does not include total ratings or other temporary ratings except total ratings based on individual unemployability if permanent." Child under 26 (extension to 31). "valid for one degree, diploma, or certificate from a community college and one baccalaureate degree." NDVA: "100% of a student's tuition charges and tuition-related fees"; not available for graduate degrees.

## Income tax
https://revenue.nebraska.gov/about/frequently-asked-questions/military-retirement-benefits-exclusion ; statute 77-2716(15)(b) https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=77-2716
Quote: "For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2022, all military retirees may exclude 100% of military retirement benefits from Nebraska taxable income to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income." Nebraska taxes income (not a no-income-tax state). Also veterans.nebraska.gov/taxes.

## Employment preference
https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=48-227 (Laws 2025 LB144); State Personnel guide (rev. 8/21/2025) https://das.nebraska.gov/personnel/docs/NE_DAS_Personnel_Workday_User_Guides-VeteransPreferenceGuide.pdf
Quote: "five percent added to their passing score ... An additional five percent shall be added ... of any disabled veteran." Guide: "Any disability percentage is acceptable to receive a preference of 10%." Spouse of veteran with 100% permanent disability gets 5%.
