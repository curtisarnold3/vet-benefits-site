# Missouri (MO) research notes, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Missouri Veterans Commission, https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/

## Corrections to the handoff hints
- Military retirement: 100% subtraction applies for tax years 2021 and later (DOR), not "since 2016".
- Veterans preference: not simply 5/10 points by statute. RSMo 36.220 (2018) gives preference and tie-breaking; 5/10 points appear only on the state application form (rev. 08-17). Flagged needs_verification.
- Parks: no 50%/60% veteran discount found; only a flat $2/night military and disability discount.
- Free DV plates: DOR page lists no percentage; statute 301.071 says total SC compensation or VA auto grant. Flagged.
- SB 190 / SB 756 senior freeze: not researched or confirmed; not included.

## Property Tax Credit (circuit breaker)
- Source: https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=135.010 (effective 28 Aug 2025; amended by HB 594 and 508)
- Quote: "the claimant or spouse is a veteran of any branch of the Armed Forces of the United States or this state who became one hundred percent disabled as a result of such service"
- Income: "veterans payments and benefits unless the claimant is a one hundred percent service-connected, disabled veteran" (not counted).
- 135.025: "For all calendar years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, ... up to one thousand fifty-five dollars in rent ... or one thousand five hundred fifty dollars in actual property tax paid." Inflation indexed from 2027.
- 135.030: max income for 2026+: $38,200 single / $42,200 single owner-occupant / $41,000 married / $48,000 married owner-occupant. Pre-2026: $27,500 / $30,000 owners.
- DOR MO-PTC 2025 instructions (https://dor.mo.gov/forms/MO-PTC%20Instructions_2025.pdf): "The 2025 Form MO-PTC is due April 15, 2026, but you may file up to three years from the due date." Qualification B: "100% Disabled Veteran as a result of military service (Attach letter from Department of Veterans Affairs)."
- Military Reference Guide (https://dor.mo.gov/forms/Military%20Reference%20Guide.pdf, "Revised January 2025"; its $750/$1,100 figures are outdated for 2026).
- Not found: surviving spouse rule for veterans (statute only covers age 65 / age 60 with SS survivor benefits and a spouse of an eligible claimant who dies in the year); TDIU treatment.

## Former POW homestead exemption
- https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?constit=y&section=X++6 (effective 02 Dec 2010): "all real property used as a homestead as defined by law of any citizen of this state who is a former prisoner of war, as defined by law, and who has a total service-connected disability, shall be exempt from taxation"
- State Tax Commission FAQ: https://stc.mo.gov/faq/are-veterans-or-former-pows-exempt-from-property-tax-2/ (must own and occupy as primary residence; VA letter plus DD-214 or NARA/VA letter of POW status; applied for at county assessor).
- Pending 2026 proposals (SJR 77, SJR 94) would expand it; not enacted as far as found.

## Disabled Veteran plates
- https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/plates/disabled-veteran.html: "An eligible applicant may obtain one free set of Disabled Veteran license plate(s)... The Disabled Veteran license plates do not allow parking in disabled parking spots unless they display the wheelchair accessibility symbol."
- Proof: "A statement from the United States Department of Veteran Affairs, no more than one year old, stating your disability is service-connected." Form 4601. Wheelchair symbol needs DOR-1776 (valid 90 days).
- RSMo 301.071 (eff. 1977): "...or who is receiving compensation from the Veterans Administration for total service connected disability." 301.072 similar. Conflict with DOR page.
- Former POW free registration and plates: RSMo 301.443 (one set, no fee; POW of WWI, WWII, Korea or Vietnam).

## Permanent disabled placard
- https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/disabled-plates-placards/permanent.html: "A letter from the United States Veterans Administration verifying that you have a permanent disability." "Disabled veterans are not required to submit another statement for renewal." "There is no fee required to obtain a Permanent Disabled Placard." No date shown.

## Military Award Placard
- Military Reference Guide p.26: free parking at meters (with local approval) and public college lots for listed medals incl. Purple Heart and former POW.

## Hunting and fishing
- https://mdc.mo.gov/permits/permit-exemptions: "Honorably discharged veterans or active personnel who: have service-related disability of 60% or greater; were a prisoner of war ... May fish and hunt (except deer and turkey) but not trap without permit." Veteran Benefits page also excludes black bears and elk, and requires migratory bird permit and federal duck stamp. Trout permit required. Nonresidents may buy resident-priced permits. No date shown.
- 2026 bill HB 2202 (free permits for 100% veterans): hearing stage only per news; not confirmed passed.

## State parks
- https://mostateparks.com/activity/camping/camping-fees: "A $2 per night camping discount is available year-round for senior citizens (65 years and older), people with disabilities, current and former military personnel..." Military page: https://mostateparks.com/msp-honors-vets. Veterans Day free night promo is annual and not guaranteed.

## Tuition
- RSMo 173.900: "limit the amount of tuition such institutions charge to combat veterans to fifty dollars per credit hour, as long as the veteran achieves and maintains a cumulative grade point average of at least two and one-half"; graduate: "no more than thirty percent of the cost of tuition and fees"; windows 10 and 20 years. Effective 28 Aug 2019.
- RSMo 173.234 Veteran's Survivors Grant: up to 25 grants a year; 80% disabled in combat after 9/11 or death/injury; ages under 25 for children; certified by Missouri Veterans Commission.

## Income tax
- https://dor.mo.gov/faq/taxation/individual/pension.html: "The taxable military pension is subtracted from federal adjusted gross income on Form MO-A, Part 1, Line 10" (2021 and later). SBP annuity does not qualify for the military subtraction. No date shown.
- VA compensation: no DOR sentence found; relies on federal AGI exclusion (needs_verification).

## Employment
- RSMo 36.220 (eff. 28 Aug 2018): "veterans, disabled veterans, surviving spouses and spouses of disabled veterans shall be given preference."
- State application https://oa.mo.gov/sites/default/files/PaperApplication.pdf (form MO 300-0739, 08-17): "Veterans' preference points are not cumulative and only 5 or 10 points total are allowed"; 10 points for "compensation for a service-related disability"; open competitive recruitment only.

## Veterans Homes
- https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/homes/admissions/: "The 2026 monthly rate for residency is $2,773."; 180 consecutive days Missouri residency; physician documentation of 24-hour skilled nursing care.
