# Nevada (NV) research narrative, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Nevada Department of Veterans Services, https://veterans.nv.gov/

## 1. Disabled veteran property tax exemption (NRS 361.091)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-361.html#NRS361Sec091 (chapter marked "Rev. 4/15/2026")
Quote: "The maximum allowable exemption for total permanent disability is the first $20,000 assessed valuation. A person with a permanent service-connected disability of: (a) Eighty to 99 percent, inclusive, is entitled to an exemption of $15,000 assessed value. (b) Sixty to 79 percent, inclusive, is entitled to an exemption of $10,000 assessed value."
Also: "A bona fide resident of the State of Nevada who has incurred a permanent service-connected disability and has been honorably discharged ... or his or her surviving spouse, is entitled to an exemption." Combined percentages: "the amount of the exemption must be based on the total of those combined percentages, not to exceed 100 percent." Not claimed in any other county; no stacking with NRS 361.090 for the veteran.
Deadline (NRS 361.155): claims "must be filed on or before June 15"; property acquired June 15-30, by July 5. Late/denied: county board of equalization by January 15.
Indexing: amounts adjusted yearly by CPI (July 2003 base). Dept of Taxation FAQ (https://tax.nv.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Veterans-Exemption-FAQs.pdf, file created 2021) repeats $20,000/$15,000/$10,000. Douglas County Assessor (https://www.douglascountynv.gov/government/departments/assessor/assessors-online-services/exemptions/veteran_exemption) lists $18,200 / $27,300 / $36,400 assessed value (about $637 / $956 / $1,274 property tax) and $3,640 for the general veteran exemption for 2026/27. That page labels the disabled amounts "2025/26" but they match the 1.82 index factor of $3,640 vs $2,000; treated as 2026-27. Carson City assessor page returned 403 to me.
Last-updated date on pages: none shown.

## 2. Surviving spouse (NRS 361.091(6))
Quote: affidavit that spouse "was married to and living with the veteran ... for the 5 years preceding his or her death", veteran eligible at death, "has not remarried", and is a bona fide Nevada resident. Same source and deadline as above. NRS 361.091(8)(b): a spouse claiming solely as surviving spouse can still take NRS 361.090 for their own service.

## 3. General veteran exemption (NRS 361.090)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-361.html#NRS361Sec090
Quote: "The property, to the extent of $2,000 assessed valuation, of any actual bona fide resident ... who has served a minimum of 90 continuous days on active duty" in listed periods, honorable discharge or certificate of satisfactory service. CPI-indexed.

## 4. Vehicle governmental services tax exemption (NRS 371.104)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-371.html#NRS371Sec104
Quote: "(a) If he or she has a disability of 100 percent, the first $20,000 of determined valuation. (b) ... 80 to 99 percent ... $15,000 ... (c) ... 60 to 79 percent ... $10,000." Veteran may transfer to current spouse. Annual affidavit. DMV (https://dmv.nv.gov/platesveterans.htm): "Visit your local County Assessor with your DD-214 ... Apply your exemption toward the Governmental Services Tax on your vehicle registration or toward your property taxes ... it cannot be applied after the transaction is finalized." DMV page: standard veteran exemption cannot be combined with disabled one. Taxation FAQ: may split between real and personal property.

## 5. Disabled Veteran plates (NRS 482.377; NRS 482.0962)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-482.html#NRS482Sec377
Quote: "Qualifying service-connected disability means: 1. A service-connected disability rated at 100 percent; 2. More than one service-connected disability, the combined ratings of which add up to at least 100 percent; or 3. A service-connected disability of any rating that constitutes or includes a permanent disability that qualifies a person for a special license plate pursuant to NRS 482.384." Plates: "not more than two sets of plates", private passenger vehicle, noncommercial truck or motor home.
Parking (subsec. 5): "exempt from the payment of any parking fees, including those collected through parking meters, charged by the State or any political subdivision or other public body within the State, other than the United States."
Statute silent on waiving registration fees; DMV Veterans Plates page does not state a disabled-plate fee waiver.

## 6. Hunting and fishing (NRS 502.072, 502.240; NDOW application March 2025)
URL: https://www.ndow.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Veteran-Disability-License-Application-ADA-revised-March-2025.pdf
Quote: "Resident Disabled Veteran Specialty Combination Hunting and Fishing License - $15.00 ... service-connected disability which is considered to be 50 percent or more." NRS 502.240 sets the $15 fee for the NRS 502.072 class. Attach VA Benefit Summary letter; hunter education if born after Jan 1, 1960. Veterans page also states $15 (https://veterans.nv.gov/hunting-fishing-licenses).

## 7. State parks Disabled Veteran permit
URL: https://parks.nv.gov/fees/annual-permits
Quote: "issued to a disabled veteran who resides in Nevada and can show proof of a permanent service-connective disability and an honorable discharge ... unlimited entrance to all parks and use of camping and boat launch facilities in the parks for 12 months ... An annual administrative fee of $30.00 is charged." Authority NRS 407.065(1)(d)(2) (permit "without charge, except" an administrative fee). Renewals not offered. Also a separate "Veteran Annual Entrance" permit exists for Nevada veterans (entrance only; details not read).

## 8. Education (NRS 396.5443, 396.5446, 396.540; NSHE)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-396.html#NRS396Sec5443 ; https://nshe.nevada.edu/system-administration/?p=357
Quote: Board of Regents "shall grant a waiver of the payment of registration fees, laboratory fees and any other mandatory fees" to a Purple Heart veteran or the child (veteran Nevada resident, child graduated a Nevada high school); child uses it "for 10 years after the child attains 18". Reduced by federal education benefits. NRS 396.5446: Regents "may" waive at least half of fees for resident veterans in critical-need graduate programs (2.75 GPA). No general disabled veteran waiver found.

## 9. Income tax
URL: https://tax.nv.gov/about-nevada-department-of-taxation/income-tax-in-nevada/
Quote: "The State of Nevada does not impose a state income tax on individuals." Military retirement and VA compensation not named.

## 10. Employment (NRS 284.260, 284.265; NAC 284.325)
URL: https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-284.html#NRS284Sec260
Quote: "an appointing authority must give a preference to: 1. Veterans." 284.265: must "Interview for the position each veteran with a service-connected disability who is a qualified applicant", and at least 22 percent of those interviewed non-disabled qualified veterans. The State Veteran Hiring Preference guide (nvjobs.nv.gov) defines service-connected as "at least zero percent". Third-party/older text lists 10 points; current official text has no point values.

## 11. State Veterans Homes
URL: https://veterans.nv.gov/state-veterans-homes (Boulder City, 180-bed skilled nursing; Northern Nevada home not read). Exemption can be donated to the Veterans Home gift accounts (NRS 361.0905).
