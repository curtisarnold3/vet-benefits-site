# Massachusetts (MA) - research narrative, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Executive Office of Veterans Services, https://www.mass.gov/orgs/executive-office-of-veterans-services (page lists a 9/23/2026 press release; confirms EOVS runs Chapter 115 and the Veterans Homes in Holyoke and Chelsea).
Note: mass.gov blocks WebFetch/curl (403); pages were read in a browser.

## Property tax (local option; apply to local board of assessors)
- Source: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/local-property-tax-exemptions-for-veterans (no date shown). Quotes: "$400 exemption for: Veterans with a service-connected disability of 10% or more"; "$750 exemption for: ... permanent loss of one foot, hand, or eye ... prisoners of war"; "$1,000 exemption for: Veterans with a 100% service-connected disability"; "$1,250 ... both feet, hands, or eyes"; "$1,500 ... 'specially adapted housing'". Page states eligible "if their municipality has voted to accept the exemptions" and veterans must "Occupy the property as his/her domicile on July 1".
- Statute: https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter59/Section5 . Clause 22E: "a disability rating of one hundred per cent as determined by the Veterans Administration ... to the amount of six thousand dollars of the taxable valuation of real property or the sum of $1,000". Surviving spouse: "shall continue unchanged for the benefit of the surviving spouse ... as long as the surviving spouse ... shall remain an owner and occupant". Clause 22D full exemption for surviving spouses of those whose injury/illness was a proximate cause of death. Clause 22F text lists no dollar amount. The "10 per cent" wording is NOT in the Clause 22 statute text I read; the 10% tier comes from the EOVS page only.
- Deadline: G.L. c. 59, s. 59 https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIX/Chapter59/Section59 : application "on or before April 1 of the year to which the tax relates, or within 3 months after the bill or notice of assessment was sent, whichever is later."
- HERO Act: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/ask-dls-hero-act (published Sept 5, 2024): 22I adds a CPI adjustment, 22J "additional exemption up to 100%"; "there is no additional state reimbursement"; effective for FY2026 once a town votes. DLS article https://www.mass.gov/info-details/reviewing-changes-in-massachusetts-veteran-property-tax-exemptions (Jan 8, 2026): "a veteran under Clause 22E (100% disability) could have their exemption increased from $1,000 to $2,000".
- 2025/2026 changes: H.3836 (disabled veteran homestead exemption) https://malegislature.gov/Bills/194/H3836 history: 5/11/2026 "Accompanied a study order, see H5395". Not law.

## Vehicle (RMV) https://www.mass.gov/info-details/disabled-veteran-fee-and-tax-exemptions (no content date; alert banner Nov. 25, 2025)
- "Veterans will qualify for all these exemptions if the USVA determines they have a 100% disability rating or deems them unemployable due to their service-connected disability." Registration fees exempt for "any/all vehicle registration(s)"; "The disabled veteran MUST be listed as one of the owners"; driver license transaction fees exempt; special plate, reinstatement, title and sales tax fees still required.
- Excise: "Disabled veterans do not need to pay excise tax for one passenger vehicle or pickup truck." Older DLS FAQ (May 2, 2024, https://www.mass.gov/info-details/ask-dls-motor-vehicle-excise-exemptions-for-veterans-and-military-personnel) describes the pre-HERO MAB test and the apply-to-assessors timing (3 years of due date or 1 year of payment); DLS HERO FAQ confirms the VA-based test is effective for calendar 2024 excises and is "fully reimbursed".
- DV plates: "minimum of a 60% service-connected disability rating ... conditions that affect ambulation"; sales tax exemption on one vehicle requires DV plates (G.L. c. 64H s. 6); Purple Heart holders with 60% may use Purple Heart plates.
- Parking: https://www.mass.gov/guides/parking-at-massachusetts-state-parks : "Daily parking fees are waived at all DCR facilities for vehicles with: ... Disabled veteran license plates".

## Recreation
- https://www.mass.gov/info-details/license-types-and-fees (2026 fees): free only for "paraplegic, blind, intellectually disabled" (fishing), "Paraplegic" (hunting/sporting). No disabled-veteran waiver listed.
- No Mass Parks Pass for disabled veterans found beyond the DV-plate parking waiver.

## Education
- https://www.mass.gov/info-details/veteran-educational-benefits (G.L. c. 15A s.19): "eligible on a space-available basis for a waiver of full or partial tuition"; legal resident, not in default on federal loans. Dept. of Higher Education https://www.mass.gov/info-details/categorical-tuition-waiver : "Campus fees are not included in the waivers", resident 1 year, 3+ credits.
- Dependents: only G.L. c. 15A s.16 Public Service Scholarship for "Children of veterans ... who were killed in action or otherwise died as a result of such service" and children of POW/MIA (Southeast Asia).

## Income tax
- https://budget.digital.mass.gov/govbudget/fy26/tax-expenditure-budget/personal-income-tax/exclusions-from-gross-income/1-029/ : "Income from U.S. military pensions is excluded from Massachusetts gross income" (G.L. c. 62 s. 2(a)(2)(E)). Item 1.025: VA compensation exempt under 38 U.S.C. 5301. MA has an income tax.

## Employment
- G.L. c. 31 s. 26 https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleIV/Chapter31/Section26 : eligible lists "(1) disabled veterans ... (2) veterans"; "A disabled veteran shall be retained in employment in preference to all other persons, including veterans." Civil Service page (last updated May 4, 2026) https://www.mass.gov/info-details/civil-service-overview-for-veterans : promotional exams get "two points"; "absolute veteran's preference".

## Other
- Annuity https://www.mass.gov/how-to/how-to-apply-for-veteran-annuity-benefits : "100% service-connected disability or compensated at 100%"; $2,500 once a year; approved by June 30; surviving spouses may remarry effective July 1, 2025.
- Chapter 115 https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massvets-veteran-financial-benefits : "means-tested program"; HERO Act outpatient behavioral health; COLA protection.
- Veterans Homes https://www.mass.gov/info-details/eligibility-requirements-for-veterans-homes-0 (Chelsea 617-887-7146, Holyoke 413-552-4764).
- HERO overview https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hero-act .
