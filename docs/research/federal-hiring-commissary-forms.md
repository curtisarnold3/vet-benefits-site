# Research notes: hiring preference, commissary/exchange, travel pay, parks pass, SSA, forms, fee rules, deadlines, crisis line, COLA
Verified Oct 16, 2026.

## Hiring preference (OPM Vet Guide https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/veterans-services/vet-guide-for-hr-professionals/ ; fedshirevets.gov now redirects to https://www.opm.gov/fedshirevets/)
TP 5 points: qualifying service periods/campaign medal. CP 10 pts: compensable SC 10-29%. CPS 10 pts: compensable SC 30%+. XP 10 pts: SC disability (incl. 0%) or Purple Heart; derived preference for spouses/mothers. SF-15 for 10-point: https://www.opm.gov/forms/pdf_fill/sf15.pdf
Authorities: VRA (noncompetitive to GS-11); VEOA; 30% or More Disabled Veteran (noncompetitive, any grade, VA letter); Schedule A (5 CFR 213.3102(u)). https://www.opm.gov/fedshirevets/hiring-officials/strategic-recruitment-and-hiring/veterans/

## Commissary/Exchange/MWR (since Jan 1, 2020): Purple Heart, former POW, ANY SC rating 0-90% (100%/TDIU already had access), PCAFC primary caregivers. Credential: VHIC marked SERVICE CONNECTED/PURPLE HEART/FORMER POW; or since Nov 1, 2024 REAL ID at visitor center for base access + VA SC letter/H623A at the store. DD214 alone NOT accepted. Small card-fee surcharge at commissary (percent NEEDS VERIFICATION). Online exchange: all honorably discharged vets via VetVerify.org.
Sources: https://news.va.gov/70068/dod-answers-top-10-questions-expanded-commissary-exchange-mwr-access/ ; https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-and-dod-announce-updates-to-improve-veteran-and-caregiver-access-to-installations/ ; https://corp.commissaries.com/Expanding-Access ; https://www.militaryonesource.mil/benefits/veterans-online-shopping-benefit/

## Beneficiary travel https://www.va.gov/health-care/get-reimbursed-for-travel-pay/ (Jul 24, 2026): 30%+ any VA appt; <30% for SC condition care; pension; low income; C&P exams. 41.5 cents/mile; deductible $3 one-way/$6 round trip, max $18/month, waived for pension/low income. File within 30 days via BTSSS or form 10-3542. 38 CFR 70.10, 70.31.

## Parks: Military Lifetime Pass free for veterans and Gold Star families https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/veterans-and-gold-star-families-free-access.htm (Feb 26, 2026); ID: VHIC, VA Veteran ID Card (digital), DD2, state DL veteran designation. Access Pass free for permanent disability https://www.nps.gov/subjects/accessibility/interagency-access-pass.htm

VA "claims predators" https://news.va.gov/133852/beware-claims-predators-want-to-prey-on-your-benefits/ ; report VSAFE.gov 1-833-388-7233. ## Deadlines: 38 CFR 3.155(b) ITF 1 year; 3.2500(a) HLR/Board within 1 year, supplemental any time; (c) continuous pursuit; (h)(2) supplemental >1 yr effective date = receipt date. 38 USC 5103(b)(1) one year to respond to notice. https://www.va.gov/resources/your-intent-to-file-a-va-claim/ ; https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/
