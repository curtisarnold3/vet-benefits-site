# Flag checks, 2026-10-18

First pass at the recurring flags: does VA compensation count as untaxed by the state, and does TDIU qualify for the property tax exemption. Official sources only; a flag clears only if an official page says it explicitly. Absence of a statement does not clear a flag.


## VA compensation and state income tax (DE, MO, MT, NE, NC, OH, OR)

- **DE: VA disability compensation not taxed** - still flagged. Checked 2025 PIT-RES instructions (no VA mention), Delaware Office of Veterans Services federal-benefits PDF (generic 'tax-free', federal only), Finance/Revenue pages. No official page says Delaware excludes VA compensation. Stays flagged.
- **MO: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked DOR Military Reference Guide, 2025 Income Tax Reference Guide (4711_2025; VA only appears in property tax credit income rules), pension FAQ, MO-1040 instructions. No explicit state income tax statement. Stays flagged.
- **MT: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked revenue.mt.gov/taxes/military (silent) and 2025 Form 2 instructions (only lists 'veteran's compensation benefits' as government assistance for the property tax credit, not an income tax statement). Stays flagged.
- **NE: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked revenue.nebraska.gov military retirement FAQ and Info Guide 8-364 (no VA compensation mention), veterans.nebraska.gov/taxes (silent), NDVA benefits guide (says VA compensation/pension is 'tax-free' with no state-tax statement, federal framing only). Stays flagged.
- **NC: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked NCDOR Armed Forces Information page (no VA compensation mention), D-401 instructions, NCDOR/ncleg searches. Only property-tax results found. Stays flagged.
- **OH: State income tax: VA disability compensation** - still flagged. Checked tax.ohio.gov military pages (404/empty), Ohio DVS 2026 resource guide (only calls MIRF payments tax exempt), tax.ohio.gov disability severance alert (severance pay only). No explicit statement on VA compensation. Stays flagged.
- **OR: State income tax on VA disability compensation** - RESOLVED. [Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs : Disability Compensation : Benefits & Programs : State of Oregon](https://www.oregon.gov/odva/benefits/pages/disability-compensation.aspx). Quote: "Compensation is set based on federal law and is not subject to state or federal income tax."

## VA compensation and state income tax (SC, TN, UT, VT, VA, WV, WI)

- **SC: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked 2025 SC1040 instructions (dor.sc.gov IITPacket_2025.pdf), SCDOR policy manual ch.3, SC Code Title 12 Ch. 6, and SCDVA disability-compensation page. SC1040 only says T&P disability retirement, Social Security and railroad retirement 'are not taxed by South Carolina' and never names VA compensation. SCDVA page says compensation is 'tax free' without saying state or federal. No official page explicitly addresses VA compensation for SC income tax, so it stays flagged.
- **TN: No state income tax on wages, military retirement or VA compensation** - still flagged. tn.gov Hall Income Tax page: 'The Hall Income Tax was repealed for tax periods that begin on January 1, 2021, or later.' TDOR help article GEN-34 (revenue.support.tn.gov): 'Tennessee has no state income tax on earned income'. HIT-18: 'Tennessee has no individual income tax on wages, employer sponsored retirement income or most other forms of income.' None of these name military retired pay or VA disability compensation; TDOR military tax guide covers sales/vehicle/business tax only. Wages and Hall repeal are confirmed, but the military retirement and VA compensation parts of the title are not explicitly stated, so it stays flagged (could narrow title to wages/Hall tax).
- **UT: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked 2025 TC-40 instructions (starts from federal AGI, line 4), Utah Tax Commission searches, and veterans.utah.gov FAQ (only says DIC is tax-free). No official Utah page names VA disability compensation for income tax; stays flagged.
- **VT: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - still flagged. Checked tax.vermont.gov/node/604, GB-1099 military tax guide, 2025 income booklet, and veterans.vermont.gov VA Disability page ('For Disability Compensation, this income is tax free', does not say state or federal). No Vermont Dept. of Taxes page states VA compensation treatment; stays flagged.
- **VA: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - RESOLVED. [Disability Claims & Pensions | Virginia Department of Veterans Services](https://www.dvs.virginia.gov/benefits-services/disability-claims-pensions). Quote: "The benefits are not subject to federal or state income tax."
- **WV: VA disability compensation and state income tax** - RESOLVED. [Disability Compensation | WV Department of Veterans Assistance](https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-assistance/benefits/disability-compensation). Quote: "These benefits are not subject to federal or state income tax."
- **WI: VA disability compensation and Wisconsin income tax** - still flagged. Pub 128 (1/26) Part 4.B says 'Wisconsin follows the federal treatment of these disability pensions' (employer disability pensions; does not name VA compensation). WDVA claims page says 'Veteran disability compensation is non-taxable' without saying state or federal. DOR military FAQ covers military retirement only. No explicit official statement for Wisconsin income tax; stays flagged.

## TDIU and property tax (HI, IN, KY, MA, MN)

- **HI: City and County of Honolulu: totally disabled veteran real property tax exemption (ROH 8-10.5)** - still flagged. Checked 2026-10-18: Honolulu RPAD FAQ (realproperty.honolulu.gov/help-resources/faq/) Q7: 'It means you have a 100% disability rating from the U.S Department of Veterans Affairs ("VA") for injuries that occurred while on active duty'; Q10 says P&T 'meets the requirement', so permanence is not required (a 100% rating suffices; Q12 asks for VA award letter showing 100% disability plus a physician certificate). Nothing official addresses TDIU: the FAQ says '100% disability rating', which a TDIU award (paid at the 100% rate but rated below 100%) does not literally state. Ordinance page (amlegal) blocked by bot check. Stays flagged for TDIU only; the 'permanent not required' part could be updated using the FAQ.
- **IN: 100% property tax deduction on principal residence for totally disabled veterans (new 2026 law)** - still flagged. Checked 2026-10-18: DLGF FAQ (Aug 2026) Q10 says section 14 'requires a total disability evidenced by a VA pension certificate or award of compensation, or an IDVA certificate of eligibility'; IDVA page says only 'Have a total disability'. Neither names TDIU or permanence. A search snippet of an older IDVA reference sheet counted 'Individual is Unemployable' as total, but that URL now returns 404 and it predates the 2026 law, so it cannot be cited. Stays flagged.
- **KY: Homestead exemption for totally disabled owners ($49,100 off assessed value, 2025-2026)** - RESOLVED. [Guidelines for Administering the Homestead Exemption, Commonwealth of Kentucky Department of Revenue (2024), Situation #22](https://revenue.ky.gov/PVANetwork/PublishingImages/Pages/Local-Support/Homestead%20Exemption%20Manual%202024.pdf). Quote: "Additionally, the award letter states they have been granted "Individual Unemployability" status. Therefore, an applicant with this type of documentation can be granted a disability homestead exemption."
- **MA: Property tax exemption: Clause 22E (100% service-connected)** - still flagged. Checked 2026-10-18: M.G.L. c.59 s.5 cl.22E (malegislature.gov) requires 'a disability rating of one hundred per cent as determined by the Veterans Administration'; no mention of unemployability or permanence. Mass.gov veterans exemptions page says 'Veterans with a 100% service-connected disability'. DOR guide/Chapter 7 PDFs returned 403 or download-only, so could not read them for TDIU. Silence on TDIU, stays flagged.
- **MN: Homestead market value exclusion, $300,000 (100 percent total and permanent)** - RESOLVED. [Disabled Veterans' Homestead Property Tax Exclusion (Fact Sheet 11), Minnesota Department of Revenue](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/sites/default/files/2018-12/Fact%20Sheet%2011%20(disabled%20vets).pdf). Quote: "veterans who have a service-connected disability rating of 100% permanent and total (including permanent Individual Unemployability)"

## TDIU and property tax (MS, MT, OK, SC, UT)

- **MS: Homestead exemption - TDIU or non-100% schedular rating (unclear)** - still flagged. Found and read the official AG opinion PDF (not just the ezel.ai summary): it says no unemployability requirement and defers to DOR Rule 3.03 for proof, but never mentions TDIU or non-permanent ratings. Rule 3.03 (SOS PDF) says only 'honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected total disability'. No official text names TDIU; stays flagged. The details text can cite the official AG PDF URL instead of 'non-official summary'.
- **MT: MDV: TDIU and non-permanent ratings** - still flagged. Non-permanent half is confirmed by the form (temporary 100% accepted; periodic re-verification), already reflected in current details. TDIU half: form, DOR MDV page, and 15-6-311 MCA say only 'rated or paid at the 100% disabled rate'/'100% disability'; none names TDIU or unemployability. Concluding TDIU pay counts would be inference, so unresolved. Note the form is now Tax Year 2027 and DOR MDV page now shows 2025 FAGI limit Single $64,551 (current details/limits may need refresh).
- **OK: 100% disabled veteran homestead property tax exemption** - still flagged. Form 998 note says the VA letter may certify 100% service-related disability OR show compensation at the 100% rate, which suggests 'permanent' wording on the letter is not the only route; but TDIU/unemployability is not named anywhere I could reach (Form 998, OTC exemptions page, Art. X 8E text). Official OAC 710:10-14-5 text not retrievable (only Justia, non-official). No filing deadline found on official pages. Stays flagged.
- **SC: Disabled veteran home property tax exemption** - still flagged. Searched SCDOR Exempt Property page, SCDOR tax tip on veteran exemptions, and S.C. Code Title 12 Ch. 37 full text for 'TDIU'/'unemployability': zero hits. Statute defines permanently and totally disabled as inability to perform substantial gainful employment, with county service officer or VA certificate; no official text says whether a TDIU award counts. Stays flagged.
- **UT: Disabled veteran property tax exemption (primary residence and personal property)** - RESOLVED (row updated, flag kept: other open question). [Utah Code 59-2a-501, Veteran armed forces exemption amount (effective 1/1/2026)](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title59/Chapter2a/C59-2a-S501_2026010120250507.html). Quote: "A county shall consider a veteran with a disability to have a 100% disability, regardless of the percentage of disability described on the statement of disability, if the United States Department of Veterans Affairs certifies the veteran in the classification of individual unemployability."
