Verified Oct 16, 2026 (va.gov, law.cornell.edu; dfas.mil blocked scripted fetch, so DFAS items are from snippets + 10 USC 1413a/1414 + VA 2026 Federal Benefits guide).

## SMC (rates effective Dec 1, 2025; https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/special-monthly-compensation-rates/)
K $139.87 add-on; S $4,408.53; L $4,900.83; M $5,408.55; N $6,152.64; O/P $6,877.12; R1 $9,826.88; R2/T $11,271.67 (veteran alone).
Triggers 38 CFR 3.350: K = loss/loss of use of hand, foot, creative organ, one-eye blindness, deafness both ears, aphonia, breast tissue. L = both feet, one hand+one foot, blindness 5/200, or bedridden/needs A&A. S = single 100% + independent 60% separate systems, OR permanently housebound in fact. R = max O/P rate + A&A (R2 higher level of care, 3.352(b)). T = TBI needing A&A.
A&A criteria 38 CFR 3.352(a). Forms 21-2680 (exam), 21-0779 (nursing home). Dead link: benefits.va.gov/COMPENSATION/claims-special-smc.asp.

At 100%: spouse adds $219.59; first child $146.85; each addl child <18 $109.11; each addl child 18-23 school $352.45; one parent $176.24; spouse A&A $201.41. Forms 21-686c, 21-674.

## TDIU: 38 CFR 4.16(a) one 60% or 70% combined with one 40%; (b) extraschedular referral. Marginal employment = below Census poverty threshold for one person. https://www.va.gov/disability/eligibility/special-claims/unemployability/ (Apr 27, 2026). Forms 21-8940, 21-4192. Verification now via SSA wage match; 21-4140 within 65 days of notice. https://www.va.gov/disability/verify-individual-unemployability-status/

## CRDP/CRSC
CRDP: 10 USC 1414; 50%+ rating; 20-yr retiree (incl. TERA, Guard/Reserve 20 qualifying yrs; Ch 61 with 20+ yrs subject to offset); automatic, taxable. CRSC: 10 USC 1413a; combat-related disability (Purple Heart, armed conflict, hazardous service, instrumentality of war, simulated war); DFAS says 10%+ rating; Ch 61 retirees with <20 yrs eligible; tax-free; apply to service branch with DD Form 2860. Pick one; Open Season Jan 1-31. VA guide: https://department.va.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Federal-Benefits-for-Veterans-Dependents-and-Survivors_web.pdf
DFAS: https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crdp/ , https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crsc/ (re-verify by hand).
Major Richard Star Act: NOT enacted as of Oct 2026 per non-official sources; NEEDS VERIFICATION.

Oct 1, 2025: $27,074.99 (VA guide; NEEDS VERIFICATION on rates page). Oct 1, 2026: $27,908.90 https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/special-benefit-allowance-rates/ . ## Funding fee https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/ (Oct 5, 2026)
Exempt: receiving SC compensation; eligible but receiving retired/active pay instead; surviving spouse on DIC; pre-discharge memorandum rating; Purple Heart active duty. First use <5% down 2.15%, subsequent 3.3%; IRRRL 0.5%. Form 26-1880. 2026: funding fee now tax-deductible per VA News Feb 18, 2026 (legal basis NEEDS VERIFICATION).

## Caregivers
PCAFC: 70%+ single or combined; 6+ months in-person personal care; any era since Oct 1, 2022; 38 CFR 71.15, 71.20; stipend = GS-4 step 1 locality /12 x 0.625 (Level 1) or x 1.00 (Level 2). Form 10-10CG. Legacy eligibility extended to Sep 30, 2028 (90 FR 46480). Dec 2024 proposed expansion NOT final as of Oct 2026. https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/health-and-disability/comprehensive-assistance-for-family-caregivers/
PGCSS: any era, no rating, veteran enrolled in VA health care; no stipend; contact facility Caregiver Support Coordinator. https://www.caregiver.va.gov/CAREGIVER/Care_Caregivers.asp
