# Puerto Rico (territory) - research narrative, verified 2026-10-18

Agency: Oficina del Procurador del Veterano (OPV), https://opv.pr.gov, tel 787-758-5760. Page last-updated dates are not shown on these sources.

Core source: Ley 203-2007 as amended, OPV compilation "Rev. Noviembre 2025" (includes Ley 26 of 12 June 2025):
https://docs.pr.gov/files/OPV/Leyes/Ley%20203%20segun%20enmendada%20Rev%20NOV%202025.pdf

## Property tax (CRIM)
- Art. 4(C) Segundo (a)(1): exenta "hasta cinco mil (5,000) dólares ... de su valor de tasación" for the "residencia principal" of a veteran or surviving spouse ($50,000 only for FY 2009-10 to 2018-19). Retroactive up to 3 years.
- Art. 4(C) Segundo (c)(1): "Todo veterano que reciba compensación por conducto de la Administración de Veteranos por incapacidad de un cincuenta por ciento (50%) o más tendrá derecho a una exención ... sobre los primeros cincuenta mil (50,000) dólares" ($500,000 only for FY 2009-10 to 2018-19). Land to 1,000 m2 urban / 1 cuerda rural; residence on January 1; claimed annually; permanent rating needs one filing; stacks with the $5,000.
- Art. 4(C) Segundo (b)(1): full exemption of the home of a "veterano incapacitado o impedido". No percent stated.
- CRIM form/guide (BC-89): https://portal.crim360.com/crimpr/CMS/DOCUMENTOS/download/166.pdf - three exemption types; deadlines: regular, by Jan 1 of year after acquisition; incapacitado/impedido, on or before Jan 31 of year before the fiscal year; service-connected, annually at tax payment. Needs no CRIM debt (or payment plan). Online filing exists through the CRIM portal.
- Surviving spouse: Art. 6(a), benefits continue but "cesarán en cuanto al cónyuge supérstite, tan pronto contraiga nuevo matrimonio".

## Vehicles (DTOP / Hacienda)
- Art. 4(C) Tercero (a)-(c): excise tax and license fee exemption for cars "provistos a veteranos impedidos ... por o con la ayuda de la Administración de Veteranos"; DTOP provides a removable parking placard for disabled veterans.
- (e): veterans "declarados incapacitados en un cien por ciento (100%) por la Administración de Veteranos, no pagarán derechos de licencia sobre vehículo de su propiedad" and no driver-license fees.
- (f): veterans over 60: exempt from transfer fees, inspections, license/title requests and driver-license renewal fees.
- (g): "descuento de cincuenta por ciento (50%) en los derechos anuales en la compra del marbete ... un (1) solo vehículo".
- Art. 4(F): at least one reserved parking space for veterans at agency and municipal offices with free public parking.

## Income tax (Hacienda)
- Ley 191-2024 (amends Sec. 1033.18 of Ley 1-2011): full exemption on military pension for retired veteran residents; "podrá reclamarse en la planilla del año contributivo 2025 en adelante"; additional personal exemption $1,500 per veteran ($3,000 if both spouses are veterans on joint return): https://bvirtualogp.pr.gov/ogp/Bvirtual/leyesreferencia/PDF/2020/0191-2024.pdf (scanned OCR text; the enrolled law number is from the file name and bill, confirm with Hacienda).

## Education
- Art. 4(B)(f): 50% discount on tuition, fees, books for spouse, children and dependents at UPR and public postsecondary; total exemption for children/spouse/dependents of service members killed in action or died in active service.
- Art. 4(B)(e): free tuition at UPR for veterans who exhaust federal benefits before finishing a degree.
- OPV Regimiento 65 scholarship for children of veterans 2026-2027: https://docs.pr.gov/files/OPV/Beca65/REQUSITOS%20BECAS%20REGIMIENTO%2065%20INFANTERIA%202026-2027%20PDF.pdf

## Employment
- Art. 4(F)(1),(3): preference when qualifications are equal; "se le sumen diez (10) puntos o el diez por ciento (10%), lo que sea mayor" to the exam score.

## Recreation
- Art. 4(G)(a): 10% discount for veteran, spouse and minor/disabled children at parks, balnearios, zoos, campgrounds.

## Other
- Art. 4(C) Quinto: free government certificates; Art. 4(D): free medical care at public facilities subject to Medicaid-type means test; Ley 313-2000 rent subsidy for qualifying veterans (State Veterans Home, Juana Diaz) - not added as rows.
- 2026 pending bill (not law): P. del S. 1180 (8 Apr 2026) would extend certificate/license fee exemption to veteran-owned businesses.
