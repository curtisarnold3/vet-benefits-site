## College
- URL: https://www.amsamoa.edu/policies/veteranaccess.html (no date). Quote: "charged the in-state rate, or otherwise considered a resident, for tuition and fees purposes" (Section 702). In-state otherwise requires "resided in the Territory of American Samoa for five consecutive years." No tuition waiver found.

## Income tax
- VA News (https://news.va.gov/139592/unlocking-veteran-tax-exemptions-across-states-and-u-s-territories/): "Residents pay taxes to the American Samoa Tax Office, not the IRS ... Military pay and retired pay are taxable, but disability retirement pay for service-related injuries is not." Secondary VA page, not the territorial code; flagged.
- IRS Pub. 570 (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p570): bona fide resident rules; "military service pay during active duty will be sourced based on an individual's state of legal residence." Only first 100,000 of 272,063 characters read; American Samoa chapter not seen.
- IRS Pub. 3 (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p3) lists "Veterans' benefits (including VA rehabilitation payments)" as non-earned income for EIC purposes only.

## Old federal note
- GAO 1992 report (https://www.gao.gov/assets/220/217035.pdf): VA home loans unavailable then; not fetched in full, status unverified.
