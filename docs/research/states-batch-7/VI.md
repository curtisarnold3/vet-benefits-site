# U.S. Virgin Islands (VI) - territory research, verified 2026-10-18

Agency: U.S. Virgin Islands Office of Veterans Affairs, https://veterans.vi.gov/ (St. Croix 340-773-8387; St. Thomas/St. John 340-774-8387).

Sources read: the Office's "Summary of State VA Benefits" (as of March 14, 2025), Policy Letters 1-7 (all dated April 15, 2020), the UVI Tuition Waiver form (as of Jan 29, 2025), and the Office's Dec 2, 2025 legislative testimony. The Office's home page shows no last-updated date.

## Key quoted language
- Definition: "Veterans who were domiciliaries of the Virgin Islands at the time of death, and who entered the armed services in the Virgin Islands or who were domiciliaries of the Virgin Islands but entered the armed services in another jurisdiction while temporarily residing in that jurisdiction."
- Property tax (summary item 7): "Homestead Tax Exemption of Real Property Tax Bill is offered to Veterans or the widowed spouse of a Veteran who occupies their primary home." Item 8: "...compensation for permanent and total disability is exempt from the payment of all real property taxes levied by the Tax Assessor against the disabled Veteran's homestead." Policy Letter 6 (Title 33 V.I. Code): obtain a "Veteran Tax Exemption Eligibility Form signed by the Director." No dollar amount found. The hint "$650, 33 V.I.C. 2305" was NOT confirmed.
- Plates: Policy Letter 1: veterans may apply "for veteran's license plates at no cost" (registration price set by the BMV); "Only two (2) sets of plates are authorized per Veteran" vs summary "Up to 3 FREE sets ... only one disabled licensed plate per eligible Veteran." Disabled plate: "'Permanent and Total' disability designation ... documented on their Benefit Summary Letter."
- Tuition: Policy Letter 2 and form cite section 471, Chapter 33, Title 17 V.I.C. (Act 8147; CELL: Act 6783). Form: veteran certifies "released Under Conditions Other than Dishonorable" and "not entitled to have my tuition paid by any Federal funds made available to Veterans for college tuition." Summary: free tuition at UVI, "all fees are paid by the Veteran."
- Civil service: "10-Points Preference ... 15-Points for Disabled Veterans. A disabled Veteran is a Veteran with a service-connected disability currently rated at 0% or more." (Statute not found.)
- Medical travel: Policy Letter 3: 100% flight reimbursement for non-service-connected appointments within the VA Caribbean network; outside network "no more than $250" with odd wording "no more than 40% service-connected." Act 8991 (signed June 13, 2025, per press reports, not the statute): two round trips a year to mainland VA facilities; Office testified Dec 2, 2025 the benefit is in effect.
- HFA loan: summary "Max amount of $220,000.00 at a low rate of 3.5%" (when available); Title 21 V.I.C. ch. 16.
- Burial: summary "$5,000.00 is reimbursed"; free plot; Title 3 ch. 1 sec. 8b(c).
- Taxi medallion: Title 20 sec. 407(c); min bids $20,000 (STT/STJ), $8,000 (STC).
- Ambulance: "FREE Emergency Ambulance Service to all Veterans residing in the US Virgin Islands."
