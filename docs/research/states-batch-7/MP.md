# Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI) - research narrative, 2026-10-18

Territory/Commonwealth; very few online sources. Verified = read in official legislative text.

## 1. NMC tuition waiver (verified)
Source: https://cnmileg.net/documents/laws/public/22/PL22-16.pdf (Public Law 22-16, signed April 2022; scanned PDF, text extracted).
Quotes: "waive the Northern Marianas College tuition for a veteran with 30 percent or greater service-connected disability"; "'Eligible veteran' means a CNMI resident who was an active member of ... and is certified to have at least 30 percent ... has received an honorable discharge"; "The costs other than tuition - such as for books, student activity and laboratory fees, and other applicable fees- are not waived"; application "not later than the last class date of the semester or term". Waiver reduced by tuition-only federal/local benefits; excludes withdrawn/failed courses; degrees up to master's (Sec. 103). Codified in 3 CMC (cnmilaw.org shows 12500/12501; the site was unreachable, so the section number is unconfirmed).

## 2. Veteran / disabled veteran plates (verified)
Source: https://www.cnmileg.net/documents/files/24TH%20UPDATE/PL%2024-23%20(HB%2024-23%20Veterans%20Identification%20Card).pdf (signed March 11, 2026).
Quotes: "'Disabled Veteran' means a person who served on active duty or in a reserve component of any branch of the United States Armed Forces and who was discharged or released under conditions other than dishonorable and certified as having at least 10% disability due to service-related injury." Proof: DD214, or MVAO certification, or valid VA Veteran ID Card (veteran); MVAO certification of at least 10% (disabled). Fees, parking not in the text.

## 3. Government hiring preference (needs_verification on point tiers)
Source: https://cnmileg.net/documents/laws/public/23/PL23-05.pdf (became law without signature, Aug 2023). "Preference Eligible" defined as in 5 U.S.C. 2108(3)-(4) and 2108a(c). Section 102 (OCR): 2 points for 2108(3)(C)-(G), 1 point for (A)-(B); OPM to issue regulations.
