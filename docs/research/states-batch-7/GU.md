# Guam (GU) - territory research, verified 2026-10-18

Agency: Guam Office of Veterans Affairs, https://gvao.guam.gov/about-us/ (footer 2021). Office 172 S. Marine Corps Dr, Asan; (671) 475-8388; PO Box 2950 Hagatna 96932.

## Property tax (verified from DRT form)
Source: https://www.guamtax.com/forms/ApplicationforVeteranExemption.pdf (DRT Real Property Tax Division; no update date shown).
- "rated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (USDVA) as one hundred percent (100%) disabled or individually unemployable due to injuries received while on duty with the Armed Forces" (11 GCA 24401.1).
- "the exemption shall not be allowed on more than one (1) residence for any one (1) person"; commercial portion not exempt; surviving spouse "is not remarried"; legal guardian "lawfully vested".
- "must be filed with the assessor's office ... on or before the fifteenth (15th) day of March." Needs VA benefit letter from the VA office in Tiyan.
- Gold Star spouses and parents: 11 GCA 24401.2 (eligible per 16 GCA 7120.3(b)). Form cites Public Law 34-106.
- TDIU qualifies; P&T is not required in the text. No percent or dollar cap stated: full residential exemption.

## Education (unverified final text)
17 GCA Chapter 56, added by P.L. 38-99:2 (Feb. 11, 2026) per compiled code history (search snippet; guamcourts PDF returned 404 to me). Read Bill 202-38 as introduced: "Qualifying Veteran" = Guam resident/home of record at entry, honorable discharge, 181 days active duty excluding basic training, current Guam resident, plus wartime service, KIA or died in service etc. Dependent = spouse, or child age 25 or younger claimed on prior federal return. Tuition only, up to 90 undergrad/30 grad credit hours (per reporting), federal benefits exhausted first, GPA 2.5/3.0, space-available. Bill 335-38 proposes amendments. UOG tuition residency for Post-9/11 per Board Resolution 12-16 (not verified further).
