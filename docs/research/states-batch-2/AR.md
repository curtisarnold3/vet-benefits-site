# Arkansas (AR) — State Benefits for Disabled Veterans

Research date: 2026-10-16. Official sources only (Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs, Arkansas General Assembly act PDFs on arkleg.state.ar.us, DFA, AGFC, ADHE/SAMS, Arkansas Dept. of Shared Administrative Services/OPM, county collector .gov pages). Note: the Arkansas Code itself is hosted by LexisNexis and could not be fetched directly; where statute text is quoted it comes from the enacted Act PDFs on arkleg.state.ar.us or from an official agency page quoting the statute. The web-search budget ran out near the end of this session, so a few items (listed under "Could not verify") could not be chased further.

State veterans agency: **Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs (ADVA)** — https://www.veterans.arkansas.gov/ — 501 Woodlane Street, Suite 401N, Little Rock, AR 72201, (501) 683-2382. ADVA's site itself carries very little benefit detail (its main benefit page is the property-tax guide); the department forward-deploys District VSOs who support County Veteran Service Officers.

## 1. Property tax exemption — homestead and personal property (A.C.A. § 26-3-306)

**Source A (ADVA page):** "Guide to AR Property Taxes," https://www.veterans.arkansas.gov/guide-to-ar-property-taxes (no last-updated date shown). Links to the COSL/ADVA "Veterans Guide to Property Taxes in Arkansas" PDF, https://www.veterans.arkansas.gov/s/2024-Veterans-Guide.pdf (marked "Revised 10/2024").

Exact statute language quoted in the 2024 guide:
> "§ 26-3-306. Disabled veterans, surviving spouses, and minor dependent children – Definition. (a)(1)(i) A disabled veteran who has been awarded special monthly compensation by the Department of Veterans Affairs for the loss of, or the loss of use of, one (1) or more limbs, for total blindness in one (1) or both eyes, or for service-connected one hundred percent (100%) total and permanent disability shall be exempt from payment of all state taxes on the homestead and personal property owed by the disabled veteran."

> "Upon the death of a disabled veteran who qualifies for and receives the exemption as provided above, the section also extends this exemption to the surviving spouse and minor dependent children providing that the spouse remains unmarried. Furthermore, such spouse and minor children shall also be exempt if the member is killed while within the scope of their military duties or who is declared missing in action."

> "Please contact your local county collector's office to learn more or to apply for the exemption."

Important dates in the guide: "May 31 of each year - Assess your personal property with your county tax assessor"; "October 15 of each year - Personal and Real Estate taxes are due to your county tax collector."

**Source B (2025 law changes, official Act PDFs on arkleg.state.ar.us):**

*Act 407 of 2025 (HB1129), approved 3/25/25* — https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2025R%2FPublic%2F&file=407.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2025%2F2025R
> "'Homestead' means the: (i) Dwelling that a disabled veteran, surviving spouse of a disabled veteran, or minor dependent child of a disabled veteran occupies as his or her principal place of residence; and (ii) Up to ~~forty (40)~~ one hundred sixty (160) acres of real property contiguous to the dwelling ... if the contiguous property is not being used for a commercial purpose" — "effective for assessment years beginning on or after January 1, 2025."

*Act 876 of 2025 (HB1072), approved 4/17/25* — https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2025R%2FPublic%2F&file=876.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2025%2F2025R
> "(3)(A) A letter from the department required under this subsection is required to be submitted only one (1) time to establish eligibility for the exemption provided under this section. (B) Annual submission of a letter from the department is not required. (C) A taxpayer who has previously submitted a letter under this subsection shall notify the county collector if: (i) There has been a legal change in the status of the taxpayer and the taxpayer no longer qualifies ...; or (ii) There is a change in the property description, ownership, use, or occupancy of the property ... (D) A taxpayer claiming an exemption under this section who changes his or her homestead shall submit a new letter under this section to the county collector of the county in which the new homestead is located."
> New subsection (g): if an exemption "was granted erroneously, the county collector: (1) Shall remove the exemption from the property; and (2) May levy up to three (3) years of the property taxes that should have been paid ... plus any applicable penalties, interest, and costs." Effective for assessment years beginning on or after January 1, 2025.

*Act 880 of 2025 (HB1809), approved 4/17/25* — https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2025R%2FPublic%2F&file=880.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2025%2F2025R
> "'Homestead' includes a dwelling and real property contiguous to the dwelling that ... Is owned by: (1) A revocable trust formed by the disabled veteran who uses the dwelling as his or her principal place of residence; (2) An irrevocable trust of which the disabled veteran who uses the dwelling as his or her principal place of residence is a beneficiary; or (3) A limited liability company, the sole members of which are the disabled veteran ... or the disabled veteran ... and his or her spouse". Also redefines "Personal property" as tangible personal property "Used for other than a commercial or business purpose; and ... Registered in the name of the disabled veteran." Irrevocable-trust claimants must "furnish to the county collector a signed, notarized, and file-marked copy of the irrevocable trust." Effective "for assessment years beginning on or after January 1, 2026."

**Source C (county practice — Saline County Collector, official county site):** https://salinecounty.org/government/collector/tax_info/disabled_veteran_exemption_.php (no revision date shown; affidavit file names reference 2026).
- Eligibility summary on the page: a "100% service-connected disabled veteran, an unemployable veteran, or an unmarried surviving spouse" "may qualify for an exemption towards your homestead and personal property taxes." (Note: "unemployable" is the county's wording; the statute text does not mention TDIU/IU.)
- "The Veteran/Surviving Spouse must be on Personal Property Assessment and Title"; "The Veteran/Surviving Spouse must be listed as owner on the real estate property"; recipients are not eligible for the Amendment 79 Homestead Credit or Over-65 freeze while receiving the DAV exemption.
- "The Surviving Spouse of a Disabled Veteran must remain unmarried or a subsequent marriage terminated to receive benefits."
- VA "Summary of Benefits letter" mailed "in January of each year"; "The Deadline to submit the Summary of Benefits letter is October 15th"; the page still says the letter "must be turned in annually" (this predates / conflicts with Act 876 of 2025).
- "You must be occupying the home to qualify for the tax exemption." "Rental property, special improvement taxes, city liens, sanitation, timber, and land only do not qualify for exemption."
- No refunds "for previous years of DAV eligibility" if the letter was not submitted.

**Threshold summary:** 100% service-connected *total and permanent*; OR VA special monthly compensation for loss/loss of use of one or more limbs; OR total blindness in one or both eyes. TDIU is not named in the statute (one county says "unemployable" qualifies — ask your collector). Surviving spouse keeps it while unmarried; minor dependent children covered. Apply at the **county collector**. County procedures vary (varies_by_county = true).

## 2. Disabled Veteran license plate (free) and parking privileges

**Source A (DFA):** "Disabled Veteran License Plate – Free," https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/specialty-plates/details/disabled-veteran-license-plate-free (no date shown). Exact text:
> "Eligibility: Disabled veterans may qualify for these plates under Act 7 of 1949, or Act 30 of 1979 as certified by the Veterans Administration (VA). An eligible veteran may receive one (1) additional plate at the fee of $4.00. One (1) plate issued to the eligible veteran may be reissued to his or her surviving spouse at the fee of $4.00. Requirements: A letter from the Veterans Administration verifying the disabled veteran's eligibility under Act 7 of 1949 or Act 30 of 1979. A surviving spouse, who is not already joint owner of the vehicle, must also present a marriage license and the death certificate of the disabled veteran. Veteran must be 100% Non-Service Connected Disabled Veteran." Issuance: "Specialty License Plate Office"; Transfers: "Arkansas Revenue Office."

The sentence "Veteran must be 100% Non-Service Connected Disabled Veteran" contradicts the statute below and is almost certainly a DFA page error, but it is what the official page says today.

**Source B (statute text as shown in an official arkleg bill PDF, HB1101 of 2023, which reprinted the then-current A.C.A. § 27-24-203(2) and proposed lowering 30% to 10%):** https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/assembly/2023/2023R/Bills/HB1101.pdf
> "'Disabled veteran' means an American veteran who: (A) Is a citizen and resident of the State of Arkansas; and (B) Has been determined by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs to be a disabled service-connected veteran who either: (i) Is totally and permanently disabled and: (a) Is the owner of a motor vehicle that is used by or for the totally and permanently disabled veteran; or (b) Is issued a motor vehicle by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs under any public law; or (ii) Meets the following criteria: (a) Is at least thirty percent (30%) ... disabled; and (b) Is the owner of a motor vehicle that is used by or for the disabled veteran".
HB1101 "Died in Senate Committee at Sine Die adjournment" (5/1/2023) per https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Bills/Detail?id=HB1101&ddBienniumSession=2023%2F2023R — so the 30% threshold was NOT lowered to 10%.

**Parking (A.C.A. § 27-15-316(b), text as reprinted in HB1086 of 2023, which also died in Senate committee 5/1/2023):** https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/assembly/2023/2023R/Bills/HB1086.pdf
> "A vehicle that meets the following conditions is permitted parking privileges in those areas designated for parking only by a person with a disability under this subchapter: (1) The vehicle must display a disabled veteran special license plate issued to a disabled veteran by the Department of Finance and Administration ... or a valid disabled veteran license plate issued by another state; and (2) The vehicle must be in use for the actual transporting of a disabled veteran."
Because HB1086 failed, the pre-2023 text above should still be current, but I could not confirm the live Code text (Lexis-hosted).

**Fee / count summary:** First DV plate free; one additional plate $4.00; renewal fee not stated on the DFA page. Persons-with-disabilities plates (non-veteran) are "the regular fee" and placards are free: https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/specialty-plates/persons-with-disabilities-license-plate/ .

## 4. Hunting and fishing licenses (Arkansas Game and Fish Commission)

**Source:** AGFC, "Disability, Mobility-impaired, 65-Plus, Military Retiree, Lifetime Licenses," https://www.agfc.com/resources/licensing/disability-mobility-impaired-65-plus-military-retiree-lifetime-licenses/ (no date shown; site navigation references 2026-27 guidebooks).

Disabled military veteran lifetime licenses:
- "Resident Disabled Military Veteran Lifetime Fishing License (VLF)" — $1.50. "Veterans must be 100 percent totally and permanently disabled with certification from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs." Proof of **one year** Arkansas residency required. Trout permit still needed for trout.
- "Resident Disabled Military Veteran Lifetime Hunting License (VLH)" — $1.50, same eligibility; HIP registration and state/federal waterfowl stamps still required.
- "Resident Disabled Military Veteran Lifetime Combination License (VLC2)" — $3.00, same eligibility; optional Lifetime Waterfowl (VLD, $7.00) and Trout (VLT, $10.00) permits.
- "Resident Disabled Military Veteran Lifetime Combination License and Permit (VLL2)" — $52.50. Veterans with a "service connected disability rating of 70% or higher," or "50% or higher with a Purple Heart." Copy of VA disability certification and one year Arkansas residency required. Includes hunting, fishing, trout and state duck stamp privileges. "Sold only at the AGFC Little Rock Office." Created by Act 729 of 2019 (SB397) — AGFC news: https://www.agfc.com/news/governor-signs-bill-creating-resident-lifetime-license-for-disabled-vets/ (April 12, 2019).

General disability licenses (not veteran-specific, but VA certification accepted):
- "Resident 3-Year Disability Fishing License (RDF)" $10.50; "Resident 3-Year Disability Hunting License (RDH)" $25; Resident 3-Year Disability Combination $35.50. Eligibility: "100 percent total and permanent disability certification dated within the last three years from Social Security Administration, Veteran's Affairs or Railroad Retirement and proof of 60 days Arkansas residency are required." Recertification needed at renewal.

So the "60-day residency" rule in the hint applies to the 3-year disability licenses; the $1.50/$3.00 lifetime veteran licenses require one year of residency.

## 6. Education — Military Dependents' Scholarship / Arkansas Heroes Scholarship (dependents and spouses)

**Source A (rules):** Division of Higher Education, "Rules Governing the Military Dependents' Scholarship Program," Effective Date October 31, 2024, https://adhe.edu/File/Military%20Dependents%20Scholarship%20Rules%20Final_Effective%2010.31.24.pdf
> "'Disabled veteran' means a person who: 2.01.4.1 Has been declared by the Federal Department of Veterans Affairs to be one hundred percent (100%) totally and permanently disabled as a result of service-connected injuries or medical conditions; and 2.01.4.2 Is currently receiving special monthly compensation for such, and who was a resident of the State of Arkansas at the time that person entered the service of the U.S. Armed Forces or who is a current resident of Arkansas."
> Also covers spouses/dependent children of persons "declared to be a prisoner of war or missing or killed in action, or killed on ordnance delivery" "during the course of active military duty after January 1, 1960."
> "'Fees waived' means the inclusion of tuition at the in-state rate, room and board when provided in campus facilities, fees or other charges incurred from the approved institution."
> "The applicant must apply for the Dependents' Educational Assistance Program (DEA) Chapter 35 ... with the Department of Veterans Affairs"; scholarship "will pay that portion of charges that exceeds the amount of the DEA educational assistance allowance."
> Renewable up to 8 semesters; 2.0 GPA; 12 hours/term and 24 hours/year; "The applicant must apply for the scholarship prior to the published deadline."

**Source B (application portal):** SAMS lists the program as the "Arkansas Heroes Scholarship" (HEROES), https://sams.adhe.edu/Scholarship/Details/HEROES — covers dependents/spouses of Arkansas residents "killed or missing in action," POWs, Medal of Honor or Purple Heart recipients, or "permanently and totally disabled through military service"; covers in-state tuition, housing and mandatory fees; "Annual Deadline: July 1"; Chapter 35 application deadline June 15; dependent children must be under 26; spouses lose eligibility on remarriage; legal Arkansas resident at least one year before applying. The MDS detail page (https://sams.adhe.edu/Scholarship/Details/MDS) returns an error. I could not find the Act that renamed/merged MDS into the Heroes Scholarship; the eligibility terms are otherwise consistent. Veteran-only tuition waiver: none found in Arkansas (the scholarship is for dependents/spouses).

## 7. State income tax

Arkansas HAS a state income tax.

**Military retirement pay — fully exempt (Act 141 of 2017, A.C.A. § 26-51-307).** Act text: https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2017R%2FPublic%2F&file=141.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2017%2F2017R ("AN ACT ... TO CREATE AN INCOME TAX EXEMPTION FOR MILITARY RETIREMENT AND SURVIVOR BENEFITS"). DFA 2025 AR1000F/AR1000NR Instructions, https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025_AR1000F_and_AR1000NR_Instructions.pdf :
> "Military Retirement Exemption (Act 141 of 2017): Beginning with tax year 2018, retirement benefits received by a member of the uniformed services as defined in this Act are exempted from income tax. Retirees cannot claim the $6,000 exemption for traditional or employer sponsored distributions if their military retirement exemption exceeds $6,000. If the military retirement exemption is less than $6,000, the remaining amount of the exemption may be taken for traditional or employee sponsored distributions."
> Line 17: "Retirement benefits received by a member of the uniformed services are exempt from income tax."

**VA compensation — exempt.** Same instructions, exempt-income list, item 8: "Social Security benefits, VA benefits, Workers' Compensation, Railroad Retirement benefits and related supplemental benefits are exempt from tax." Also item 10: "Disability income MAY BE exempt from tax pursuant to Internal Revenue Code §104." Act 238 of 2009 extends the refund statute of limitations when VA retroactively determines retired pay is for a service-connected disability.

Active-duty pay is also exempt ("Beginning with tax year 2014, U.S. active-duty military compensation is exempt from tax").

## 8. State employment — veterans preference (A.C.A. § 21-3-301 et seq.)

**Source:** Dept. of Shared Administrative Services / Office of Personnel Management, Policy 13 "Veterans Preference and Reemployment," Effective Date May 26, 2026, https://sas.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/13-Veterans-Preference-and-Reemployment-5.26.2026.pdf
> Applies if the applicant "Indicates that he or she is a veteran, disabled veteran, or surviving spouse of a deceased veteran and the spouse is unmarried at the time of application and remains unmarried until hired; Is a citizen and resident of this state; and Has substantially equal qualifications of the other applicants."
> "5 points ... an applicant entitled to a veterans preference receives a passing grade, the applicant shall have 5 points added to his or her final earned rating."
> "10 points: Disabled veterans, veterans over the age of 55 who are disabled and entitled to a pension or compensation under law, or the spouse of a disabled veteran whose disability disqualifies him or her for selection shall have 10 points added to his/her final rating score ... The disability must be established by the United State Department of Veterans Affairs records."
> Veteran = honorably discharged from active duty (not training only) or "served honorably in the National Guard or reserve forces ... for a period of at least 6 years." Also covers promotion and retention (RIF seniority). State jobs: https://arcareers.arkansas.gov/

## 10. Other state items

- **Concealed handgun carry license — no fee for veterans.** Act 888 of 2021 (A.C.A. § 5-73-311(a)(2)(B)): "There is no license fee if the applicant is a United States Armed Forces veteran or is currently serving in the United States Armed Forces. (ii) An applicant shall submit a copy of his or her DD214 or similar document or valid military identification card". https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Acts/FTPDocument?path=%2FACTS%2F2021R%2FPublic%2F&file=888.pdf&ddBienniumSession=2021%2F2021R ; ASP licensing page: https://dps.arkansas.gov/law-enforcement/arkansas-state-police/services-programs/concealed-handgun-carry-licensing/
- **Veteran designation on driver's license/ID.** DFA Driver Services, https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/office/driver-services/licenses-ids-permits/military-veterans/ : "A person may apply to the Office of Driver Services to obtain a veteran designation"; discharge must be "honorable" or "general under honorable conditions"; accepted documents DD214, NGB 22, VA Veteran ID card, DD256/257, DD2 (retired). Requires "Payment of the fee for the driver's license or identification card" — no fee waiver.
- **Act 655 of 2025** requires Arkansas employers with 50+ FTE employees to post an "Arkansas Veterans' Benefits and Services" poster (Arkansas Dept. of Labor and Licensing, https://labor.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/Arkansas-Department-of-Veterans-Affairs.pdf). Informational only.
- Homestead credit (Amendment 79, A.C.A. § 26-26-1118) is a separate $425 (per the 10/2024 guide) credit for all homeowners; Saline County states you cannot receive it while on the DAV exemption.
