# Arizona (AZ) - State Benefits for Disabled Veterans

Research date: October 16, 2026. Official agency: Arizona Department of Veterans' Services (ADVS), https://dvs.az.gov/ (site is behind a Cloudflare bot check; its pages could not be read directly during this review, so ADVS-only programs are flagged below).

Sources used: Arizona Revised Statutes and Constitution (azleg.gov), Laws 2026 session chapters (azleg.gov), Arizona Department of Revenue (azdor.gov), ADOT Motor Vehicle Division (azdot.gov), Arizona Game and Fish Department (azgfd.com), Arizona State Parks and Trails (azstateparks.com), Maricopa County Assessor (mcassessor.maricopa.gov). No page displayed a "last updated" date unless noted.

## 1. Property tax exemption (A.R.S. 42-11111; Ariz. Const. art. IX, sec. 2)

### Constitutional basis (Prop 130, Nov. 2022)
- Source: https://www.azleg.gov/const/9/2.htm
- Quote: "The legislature may exempt the following property by law: ... The property of an honorably discharged veteran of the uniformed services of the United States who is a resident of this state and who has a service or nonservice connected disability as determined by the United States department of veterans affairs, or its successor agency."
- Quote: "A person is not eligible for exemption under more than one category as a widow, widower, person with a total and permanent disability or veteran with a disability..."

### Two 2026 law changes (important: different effective tax years)
**Laws 2026, Chapter 2 (HB 2792)** - https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2R/laws/0002.htm
- "APPROVED BY THE GOVERNOR FEBRUARY 12, 2026." Emergency measure: "operative immediately as provided by law."
- Sec. 2: "This act applies to tax years beginning from and after December 31, 2025." (i.e., tax year 2026)
- Enacted text: the "primary residence of a veteran with a service-connected disability whose disability rating by the United States department of veterans affairs is one hundred percent ... is fully exempt from taxation." Surviving spouse "may continue to claim the full exemption for the surviving spouse's primary residence as long as the surviving spouse uses the property as the surviving spouse's primary residence and the surviving spouse does not remarry."
- TDIU is NOT mentioned in Chapter 2.

**Laws 2026, Chapter 140 (HB 4168, "taxation; omnibus; 2026-2027")** - https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2R/laws/0140.htm
- "APPROVED BY THE GOVERNOR JUNE 13, 2026." No emergency clause.
- Sec. 34(B): "Section 42-11111, Arizona Revised Statutes, as amended by this act, applies to tax years beginning from and after December 31, 2026." (i.e., tax year 2027)
- House summary (https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2R/summary/H.HB4168_061026_CAUCUSCOW.DOCX.htm): "Includes veterans whose disability status is total disability based on individual unemployability to those who qualify for the exemption from property tax on their primary residence." "Removes the income limits for disabled veterans and their surviving spouse." "Provides a full exemption to a widow or widower of a 100% disabled veteran with a service-connected disability."

### Current statute text as displayed on azleg.gov (reflects Chapter 140)
Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/42/11111.htm
- Subsection C: "The primary residence of a veteran with a service-connected disability whose disability rating by the United States department of veterans affairs is one hundred percent or whose disability status is total disability based on individual unemployability is fully exempt from taxation. For the purposes of this subsection, a primary residence that is owned by a veteran who is eligible for the exemption under this subsection and the veteran's spouse shall be treated as if owned solely by the veteran."
- Subsection D: "The property of a veteran with a nonservice-connected disability whose disability rating ... is one hundred percent or less or with a service-connected disability whose disability rating ... is less than one hundred percent is exempt in the amount of $4,188. The limit under this subsection is further limited by multiplying the total exemption amount by the percentage of the veteran's disability, as rated by the United States department of veterans affairs."
- Subsection F: "The primary residence of a widow or widower who is the surviving spouse of a veteran who was eligible for the exemption under subsection C of this section is fully exempt from taxation."
- Subsection G: surviving spouse of a subsection D veteran "is exempt in the amount of $4,188 ... further limited by multiplying the total exemption amount by the percentage of that veteran's disability."
- Subsection J (income limits): "to qualify for the exemption prescribed in subsection E of this section [widows/widowers/persons with total and permanent disability] ... shall not exceed: 1. $34,901 if none of the claimant's children under eighteen years of age resided with the claimant... 2. $41,870 if one or more of the claimant's children residing with the claimant ... (a) Were under eighteen years of age. (b) Had a total and permanent physical or mental disability." (Note: in the Chapter 140 text the income test is tied only to subsection E, i.e. not to veterans, effective TY 2027.)
- Subsection H: amounts are indexed each year by the GDP price deflator; the assessment limit is indexed by the federal house price index beginning tax year 2026.
- Subsection K: "A person shall establish eligibility for exemption under this section by filing an affidavit with the county assessor under section 42-11152 when initially claiming the exemption and, if claiming the exemption under subsection F or G of this section, providing evidence of the veteran spouse's disability rating ... or total disability based on individual unemployability to the county assessor." Disqualifying events: death, remarriage of a widow or widower, income over the limit (subsection E only), conveyance of title.
- Subsection L: "within sixty days after the subsequent primary residence becomes the person's primary residence the person must file with the county assessor ... a fully completed exemption transfer form."
- Subsection M: unused exemption may be applied against vehicle license tax (title 28, chapter 16, article 3).
- Subsection N: "an individual is not entitled to property tax exemptions under more than one subsection."
- Definition of "income from all sources" (per Ch. 140 text) excludes "Veterans disability PAYMENTS due to the disability rating or status of total disability based on individual unemployability."

### Filing deadline (A.R.S. 42-11153) - https://www.azleg.gov/ars/42/11153.htm
- "A failure by a taxpayer who is entitled to an exemption to make an initial affidavit as required by section 42-11152 or furnish evidence required by this article between the first Monday in January and March 1 of the year constitutes a waiver of the exemption."
- Late petitions "may have the waiver redeemed by the county board of supervisors at any regular meeting, except that taxes that were due and payable before the petition was submitted may not be refunded or abated."
- A.R.S. 42-11152: affidavit "signed under penalty of perjury"; assessor may accept electronic affidavits.

### Arizona Department of Revenue FAQ (tax year 2026 application)
Source: https://azdor.gov/business/property-tax/property-tax-faqs (no date shown; says the law was "recently updated in 2025 and again in 2026")
- 100% service-connected: "Full exemption of the qualifying veteran's primary residence." "Includes a continued full exemption of the primary residence for the surviving spouse."
- Less than 100% service-connected and nonservice-connected: "up to a maximum dollar amount that is prorated according to the VA disability rating percentage."
- Income limits: "This limitation still applies to all individual exemptions defined in A.R.S. 42-11111."
- Assessed value limit: "This limitation has been eliminated for the veteran exemptions defined in A.R.S. 42-11111 (C) and (D)."
- Existing recipients: "an additional exemption application is not required." New applicants apply to the "County Assessor during the annual exemption application window" "between the first Monday in January and March 1." Changes "apply to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026."
- ADOR property tax page (https://azdor.gov/businesses-arizona/property-tax) states exemptions are administered by county assessors.

### Maricopa County Assessor (county example; other counties may differ) - varies by county
Source: https://mcassessor.maricopa.gov/page/valuation_relief/ and 2026 Personal Exemption Application (FOR-136 V5), https://mcassessor.maricopa.gov/file/valuation_relief/forms/Personal-Exemption.pdf
- "Application deadline is February 28, or September 1 with an Exemption Deadline Waiver."
- "Qualifying income excludes Social Security, Military Pensions, and Veterans' Disability payments."
- 2026 amounts: "A tax exemption up to $4,873 reduces the Assessed Limited Property Value (LPV)"; income limits "$39,865" (no children under 18) and "$47,826" (minor or disabled children). "Note: Assessed LPV limit does not apply to Veterans' exemption programs."
- "Veteran exemption amount is multiplied by the percentage of disability and applied to the percentage of ownership." Example: $4,873 x 60% = $2,924.
- "Must have an honorable discharge to qualify."
- "Veteran 100% Service-Connected Disability: Fully exempts the applicant's Primary Residence from taxation, if the applicant meets all requirements, including income."
- Rating basis: eligibility "is determined by the combined service-connected disability rating shown on the Veteran's VA Benefits letter." "It is not based on the compensation payment level." (A veteran paid at 100% via Individual Unemployability with a combined rating below 100% does not get the full exemption for 2026.)
- "The current statute does not provide an exclusion from income requirements for 100% Service-Connected Disabled Veterans."
- Surviving spouse: "may continue for the surviving spouse who remains in the home as Primary Residence and does not remarry."
- Primary residence: "the owner's one and only main residence, no matter how many homes are owned within or outside the state of Arizona ... per A.R.S. 42-12053."
- Documents: application, proof of Arizona residency, income documentation (first two pages of AZ Form 140 for 2025), VA letter showing disability percentage, Exemption Deadline Waiver if late. Contact 602-506-3406.

### Who qualifies for the FULL exemption
- 100% schedular service-connected: yes, tax year 2026 onward (income limit applies for 2026 only).
- 100% P&T: yes (P&T status is not required; the test is the 100% rating).
- TDIU: not for tax year 2026; yes beginning tax year 2027 (Laws 2026, Ch. 140).
- Nonservice-connected 100%: no full exemption; prorated $4,188 (indexed) amount only.

## 2. Vehicle license tax (VLT) and registration fee exemption (A.R.S. 28-5802)
Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/05802.htm
- "the registering officer shall not collect a vehicle license tax or registration fee from: ... 2. A veteran for a personally owned vehicle or a veteran and another party owning a vehicle if the veteran is certified by the United States department of veterans affairs as having a one hundred percent disability and drawing compensation on that basis. The exemption provided by this paragraph applies to a surviving spouse of the veteran with a disability until the surviving spouse's remarriage or death."
- "The veteran may claim and be granted an exemption and the surviving spouse may renew the registration ... for only one vehicle or any replacement of the vehicle."
- Also exempt: a vehicle "acquired by financial aid from the United States department of veterans affairs" (A.1) and, for one vehicle, "a bona fide purple heart medal recipient" who "was honorably discharged" (subsection C).

ADOT MVD page: https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/vehicle-services/vehicle-registration/vehicle-license-tax-exemptions (no date shown)
- "VA Disability: You are certified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to be 100-percent disabled and drawing compensation on that basis. The exemption also applies to the surviving spouse, until remarriage. You will need to submit a letter of eligibility from the Department of Veterans Affairs or an U.S. Department of Defense / Uniformed Services Sponsor Identification and Privilege Card with the 100% Disabled American Veteran affiliation."
- "Purple Heart Medal Recipient: A Purple Heart Medal recipient who has been honorably discharged from the military may apply. Proof of honorable discharge and award of a Purple Heart Medal is required. The exemption is available for one vehicle."
- "Widow/Person with a Total Disability/Veterans with a Disability: You receive a widow/widower, person with a total and permanent disability or veteran with a disability property tax exemption from your county assessor. You will need to submit a certified statement from the assessor's office." (This is the A.R.S. 42-11111(M) carry-over of unused property tax exemption to VLT.)

## 3. Disability (ISA) plates and placard; parking (A.R.S. 28-2409, 28-2402, 28-2403, 28-884)
Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/02409.htm
- Application must contain: "If a person with a disability who is a veteran and who has a one hundred percent disability, a copy of the person's certificate of one hundred percent disability issued by the United States department of veterans affairs."
- "The department shall issue a placard pursuant to this section at no additional charge."
- "A request for special plates issued under this section may be combined with a request for an honored military license plate issued under article 13 of this chapter."
- A.R.S. 28-2402: the $25 special plate fee applies "except special plates for hearing impaired persons issued under section 28-2408 and international symbol of access special plates issued under section 28-2409." A.R.S. 28-2403: ISA plate holders "are exempt from the transfer fee."
- A.R.S. 28-884: accessible-space parking requires a vehicle displaying "the valid permanent disability or temporary disability removable windshield placard" or "international symbol of access special plates."
- Veteran special plates (A.R.S. 28-2414, https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/02414.htm) are available to any veteran for the $25 fee ($17 is a donation to the veterans' donations fund). I could not locate an ADOT page describing a separate "Disabled Veteran" plate (see Could not verify).

## 4. Hunting and fishing licenses (A.R.S. 17-333; AZGFD)
Statute: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/17/00333.htm
- "2. A complimentary license to a veteran of the armed forces of the United States who has been a resident of this state for one year or more immediately before applying for the license and who receives compensation from the United States government for a permanent service-connected disability rated as one hundred percent disabling."
- "3. A license for a reduced fee of up to twenty-five percent less than the full license fee to a veteran ... who has been a resident of this state for one year or more ... and who receives compensation from the United States government for a service-connected disability."
- "4. A license for a reduced fee that is one-half of the full license fee to a person who has been a resident of this state for one year or more ... and who submits satisfactory proof ... that the person is a veteran and a bona fide purple heart medal recipient."
- Pioneer license (C.1): "a pioneer who is at least seventy years of age and who has been a resident of this state for twenty-five or more consecutive years"; "valid for the licensee's lifetime." (No separate "disabled veteran pioneer license" exists in the statute; the hint about such a license was not confirmed.)

AZGFD page: https://www.azgfd.com/hunting/hunt-draw-and-licenses/special-licenses-2/disabled-veteran-license/ (no date shown)
- "Bring a copy of your VA benefits letter ... that states 100% disabled, permanent and total, and mail them to: Arizona Game and Fish Department, Attn: Front counter, 5000 W. Carefree Highway, Phoenix, AZ 85086."
- "A paper license will be issued free of charge. A plastic card license may be purchased for $4."
- "Fee: $42 for less than 100% service connected disability. $28 for the Purple Heart recipient." "Fee: None for 100% service connected disability."
- "If the Department of Veterans Affairs certifies that the applicant's disability rate of 100% is permanent and will not be reevaluated, a new certification is not required for renewal. If reevaluated in three years, the disabled veteran's license will expire three years from the date of issuance."
- "This benefit is not available for nonresidents."

## 6. Education
### A.R.S. 15-1808 tuition waiver scholarships - https://www.azleg.gov/ars/15/01808.htm
- Purple Heart (subsection F): ABOR universities and community college districts, "after verification by the Arizona department of veterans' services that a person is a member or former member of the United States armed forces who received a purple heart citation, who was a resident of the state of Arizona or was stationed in Arizona at the time of the injury that resulted in the purple heart citation and whose disability rating determined by the United States department of veterans affairs is fifty percent or more, shall provide the person who otherwise meets the qualifications for admission with a tuition waiver scholarship." "A person who is convicted of a class 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 felony is ineligible."
- Killed in line of duty (subsection A): "a child or the spouse of a ... member of the United States armed forces who was a resident of the state of Arizona or stationed in Arizona and who was killed in the line of duty or who died from injuries suffered in the line of duty."
- PTS suicide (subsections B-C): child or spouse of a veteran/service member who "Was a resident of this state at the time of the person's death," "Suffered a post-traumatic stress injury in the line of duty," and "Died by suicide."
- Limits (subsection D): "limited to children who are thirty years of age or younger or a spouse who has not remarried and ... not more than sixty-four credit hours at Arizona community colleges and a total number of credits ... equal to the number of credits required for a baccalaureate degree."
- National Guard (subsection E): Arizona National Guard member "who received a purple heart citation on or after September 11, 2001" or who "was medically discharged ... due to an injury or disability suffered during status under title 10 ... in weekend training status, in annual training status or in response to a state of emergency."
### In-state tuition (A.R.S. 15-1802) - https://www.azleg.gov/ars/15/01802.htm
- "A person holding an honorable discharge from the uniformed services of the United States from either active duty or reserve or national guard status, or who has retired ... shall be granted immediate classification as an in-state student ... if the person has demonstrated objective evidence of intent to be a resident of Arizona."
- GI Bill users (chapters 30, 31, 33 or 35) who enroll "after the veteran's discharge from active duty service of ninety or more days" also get in-state status; so do service members stationed in Arizona and their spouses/dependents.
### Spouses of Military Veterans Tuition Scholarship Fund (A.R.S. 15-1809) - https://www.azleg.gov/ars/15/01809.htm
- ABOR scholarships "Subject to available monies" for the spouse of an honorably discharged veteran who is an in-state student and Arizona resident. Funding status not verified.

## 7. State income tax
- A.R.S. 43-1022(26) (https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/01022.htm): subtraction for "Benefits, annuities and pensions received as retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services of the United States in amounts as follows: ... (c) For taxable years beginning from and after December 31, 2020, the full amount received."
- A.R.S. 43-1022 also subtracts "compensation received for active service as a member of the reserves, the national guard or the armed forces of the United States, including compensation for service in a combat zone."
- ADOR Military Tax Filing page (https://azdor.gov/individuals/income-tax-filing-assistance/military-tax-filing, no date): lists as an exclusion "benefits, annuities and pensions as retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services of the United States" for "tax year 2021 and forward"; "Beginning with 2006, Arizona does not tax active duty military pay"; the subtraction "includes all pay received by a National Guard member or reservist for services performed."
- VA compensation: A.R.S. 43-1001 (https://www.azleg.gov/ars/43/01001.htm): "'Arizona gross income' of a resident individual means the individual's federal adjusted gross income for the taxable year." Because VA disability compensation is excluded from federal AGI, it is not taxed by Arizona. ADOR's military page does not state this explicitly.
- Arizona does have a state income tax (flat 2.5%; rate not re-verified here).

## 8. State employment preference (A.R.S. 38-492)
Source: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/38/00492.htm
- "A veteran ... who is separated from the armed forces under honorable conditions following more than six months of active duty and who takes an examination for employment by this state or any political subdivision ... shall be given a preference of five points ... but only if the veteran earns a passing grade without preference."
- "An honorably separated veteran who served on active duty in the armed forces at any time and who has a service-connected disability or is receiving compensation or disability retirement benefits under laws administered by the United States department of veterans affairs ... shall be given a ten point preference."
- Spouse/surviving spouse (5 points) of "Any veteran who died of a service-connected disability," certain MIA/POW members, or "A person who has a total, permanent disability resulting from a service-connected disability or any person who died while the disability was in existence."
- "No person eligible for a preference pursuant to this section shall be allowed more than a ten point preference." Where "scores are not given, preference shall be given by granting applicable preference codes."
- The state HR page (https://hr.az.gov/node/2521) was behind a bot check and could not be read; search snippets indicate DD214/VA letter are uploaded once to the azstatejobs.gov profile and preference applies to external candidates.
