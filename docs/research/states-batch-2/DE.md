# Delaware (DE) - State Benefits for Disabled Veterans

Research date: October 16, 2026. Official sources only (state agencies, county .gov pages, the Delaware Code at delcode.delaware.gov, and bill records at legis.delaware.gov). None of the agency pages cited displays a "last updated" date; where a document carries a revision date it is noted.

State veterans agency: Delaware Commission of Veterans Affairs / Office of Veterans Services, Delaware Department of State - https://vets.delaware.gov/ (benefits summary page: https://vets.delaware.gov/veterans-benefits/). Delaware created a cabinet-level Secretary of Veterans Affairs; the Office of Veterans Services remains the operating office (800-344-9900 / 302-739-2792).

---

## 1. Property tax

### 1a. Disabled Veterans School Property Tax Credit (statewide)

- Source: Delaware Department of Finance, https://finance.delaware.gov/disabled-veterans/ (no last-updated date; footer (c) MMXXVI). Statute: 14 Del. C. § 1917(d), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title14/c019/sc01/index.html.
- Rating threshold (statute § 1917(d)(1)c.1.): a qualified person "Receives 100% disability compensation due to a service-connected, permanent and total disability ... based on individual unemployability or a 100% disability rating" from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Finance page: "Receive 100% disability compensation due to service-connected, permanent and total disability" "based on individual unemployability or a 100% disability rating." TDIU qualifies. The statute's wording requires both the 100% payment rate and a permanent-and-total determination.
- Residency (statute § 1917(d)(1)c.2.): "Is legally domiciled in this State for a period of at least 3 consecutive years." Seasonal/temporary residence does not count; a 12-month absence is prima facie evidence of abandoned domicile. Finance page: "have held legal domicile in Delaware for the past 3 years."
- Amount: Finance page: credit "against 100% of non-vocational school district property tax"; "may only be used against property taxes assessed on a primary residence." Statute: "a credit against taxation in the full amount of tax liability imposed by this chapter" on "property owned and occupied as a dwelling by and as the principal residence of a qualified person," where "authorized by majority vote of the whole school board of the local school district." (Per an earlier state release all 16 non-vocational districts opted in; the Finance page lists no non-participating districts.)
- Deadline: Finance page: "Applications for the upcoming tax year are due April 30th." Statute: "no later than April 30 immediately before the beginning of that tax year." The Secretary of Finance may waive the deadline for financially disabled individuals. Prior year's tax bill must be paid in full.
- Where to apply: county offices listed on the Finance page - New Castle (302) 395-5520, Kent (302) 744-2401, Sussex (302) 855-7871. Required documents: "A valid driver's license or official state ID," VA disability documentation, copy of Social Security card.
- Surviving spouse (statute § 1917(d)(1)d.): "the credit must be allowed for a surviving spouse of a deceased qualified person" if the deceased veteran was claiming the credit, the spouse was a titled owner before the death, has not remarried, and occupies the home as principal residence. Lost on remarriage, loss of title, or moving out.
- Cannot combine with the Senior School Property Tax Credit (§ 1917(d)(4)e.; Finance page: "Individuals may not claim both").
- 2025/2026 law changes: NONE enacted. HB 349 (153rd GA), introduced 4/9/26, "AN ACT TO AMEND TITLE 14 OF THE DELAWARE CODE RELATING TO DISABLED VETERANS' SCHOOL TAX CREDIT," would lower the threshold from 100% to "80% or greater." Status on https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/143046: referred to House Appropriations, last status 4/23/26; no votes, not signed. The hint about a 2025 change opening the credit to TDIU is not accurate - TDIU has been in the statute since the credit began in 2022.

### 1b. New Castle County (varies_by_county; needs verification)

- Source: New Castle County Assessment Division, https://www.newcastlede.gov/182/Tax-Exemptions; Disability Exemption fillable form revised January 6, 2026 (https://www.newcastlede.gov/DocumentCenter/View/62373); county "Property Tax Exemptions and Assessment Appeals" presentation (DocumentCenter/View/62792); county news release "New Castle County expands property tax relief for disabled servicemembers and veterans" (CivicAlerts AID=1455).
- What we could confirm (search-engine excerpts of the county documents; the county web server returned a bot-challenge page to all direct fetches): the county presentation lists the Disabled Veteran Exemption as reducing the county tax assessed value "to zero," with New Castle County property tax and Crossing Guard tax "fully reduced to zero, with no residency waiting period." The 2026 form asks whether the applicant "has received compensation due to a service-connected, permanent and total disability based on individual unemployability or a 100% disability rating" and, if yes, to attach the VA rating determination. The 2026 form's filing deadline is June 1, 2026 (prior form: June 1, 2025). The county states the durational residency requirement is "waived for an applicant who is totally and permanently disabled, who incurred their disability as a result of, and while in, the service of the United States armed services, who has been honorably discharged."
- Older county release (2018 ordinance): "$37,000 exemption" of assessed value plus "up to $42,000" more based on severity for income-eligible disabled veterans; superseded in practice by the 2022 county ordinance for 100% VA-rated veterans, per the county presentation.
- Not confirmed: current ordinance text, whether an income test still applies to the 100% category, sewer/dwelling fee reductions, surviving spouse rule. Contact (302) 395-5520 / assessment@newcastlede.gov.

### 1c. Kent County (varies_by_county)

- Source: Kent County Levy Court, https://kentcountyde.gov/Residents/Tax-Credits-Exemptions-Assessment (no last-updated date; footer (c) 2026).
- Eligibility quoted: covers "those entirely disabled (100%) with documented proof" and yields "a reduction in their taxable assessment, reducing or potentially eliminating their property tax liability." Income limits: "Single applicant's adjusted gross annual income not exceeding $18,000;" "combined income for applicant and spouse not exceeding $24,750." "Income excludes social security, railroad retirement tier I and disability-related income from adjusted gross income." The page does not say veterans are exempt from the income limits.
- Residency: "Residency in Delaware for the five years leading up to the tax year sought for exemption," "except for veterans 100% disabled due to military service." "Residency in the property as the primary residence for one year before seeking exemption," "except for qualifying disabled veterans."
- Deadline/process: "submitting them to the Assessment Office by April 30th prior to the tax year." "All taxes, fees, and charges owed to Kent County must be paid in full by the application deadline of April 30th." Applicants "must re-apply each year." Ownership must be in the applicant's, spouse's, or revocable grantor trust's name.
- The county code (eCode360) reportedly sets a $25,000 assessed-value exemption plus $5,000 more for a totally disabled owner whose disability arose from armed-services service, but eCode360 blocked our fetch; those figures are not confirmed.

### 1d. Sussex County (varies_by_county)

- Source: Sussex County, https://sussexcountyde.gov/disabled-veterans-school-tax-credit and https://sussexcountyde.gov/tax-assistance-programs (footer (c) 2026); Ordinance No. 2811 adopted November 16, 2021, https://sussexcountyde.gov/sites/default/files/ordinances/o2811.pdf.
- Ordinance § 103-43: "Every person who shall be a resident of this county, who shall be totally disabled as determined by the State of Delaware Disabled Veterans' School Tax Credit program established under 14 DEL. C. 1917, and who shall reside in a dwelling owned by him or her ... shall be entitled, to a credit against County value-based property tax." § 103-44: "A completed application for the State of Delaware Disabled Veterans' School Tax credit shall serve as application for the Real Property County Tax Credit for Disabled American Veterans." § 103-46: credit "shall remain in effect so long as the real property and the dwelling house thereon are owned by the applicant or his surviving spouse."
- County page eligibility: "Totally disabled;" "Full-time primary resident of Delaware for the preceding 3 years as of July 1 of the current tax year;" benefit: "Up to a full credit towards property taxes, excluding Sussex Tech and non-value-based fees and services;" "Statewide deadline is APRIL 30, for applications to be turned in." "NO APPLICATION SHALL BE APPROVED UNLESS ALL TAXES, USER FEES, SEWER SERVICE CHARGES AND ALL OTHER TAXES AND FEES THEN DUE" are paid; "non-transferable if the property is sold, all credits are removed." Billing Office (302) 855-7871.

## 2. Vehicles

### 2a. Registration fee exemption (one vehicle)

- Source: Delaware DMV Military Services, https://citizen.dmv.de.gov/DriverServices/military-services/; 21 Del. C. § 2164, https://delcode.delaware.gov/title21/c021/sc05/index.html.
- DMV page: "A motor vehicle owned by a veteran with a disability, who is eligible for 'adaptive equipment' benefits" "shall be exempt from the payment of registration fees (limited to one automobile per eligible veteran at any one time)." "The Division of Motor Vehicles will need documentation from the Veterans' Administration Office."
- Statute § 2164: applies to a "veteran with a disability who shall ever have been eligible for certain benefits under 38 U.S.C. § 1901 et seq. or Public Law 538, 93rd Congress"; vehicles "shall be exempt from the payment of registration fees," "limited to 1 automobile per eligible veteran at any 1 time"; "The Secretary shall furnish, without cost, number and registration plates for all such vehicles." Inspection still required. No percentage threshold; eligibility is tied to the VA automobile/adaptive equipment grant.

### 2b. Disabled Veteran plates and parking

- Source: DMV Form MV549 "DAV License Plate Application" (Rev. 4/2025), https://www.dmv.de.gov/forms/veh_serv_forms/pdfs/MV549DAV-Application.pdf; Form MV549HP "DAV-HP License Plate Application" (Rev. 4/2025), https://www.dmv.de.gov/forms/veh_serv_forms/pdfs/MV549DAV-HP-Application.pdf; 21 Del. C. § 2138, https://delcode.delaware.gov/title21/c021/sc02/index.html.
- Eligibility (form, "In accordance with 21 Del.C. §2138"): the applicant "is a veteran with a disability of any war, the Korean conflict, the Vietnam conflict, Operation Desert Storm or other military conflict on foreign soil" OR "It has been determined by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs that the applicant meets the qualifications of being a veteran with a service-connected disability." "A copy of the certification letter from the Veterans Administration Office stating the applicant has a service connected disability must be submitted." No minimum percentage.
- Fee/vehicles: "A one-time fee of $10 must accompany this application." "Plate may be issued only on a private passenger vehicle or a truck with a 26,000 pound or less manufacturer's gross vehicle weight rating" (DAV-HP: 14,000 lb). Statute § 2138(e): "No fee in addition to the annual registration fee required by this title is required for registration under this section."
- Parking: DAV form: "A Disabled Veteran's Plate does not permit special parking in handicapped zones." The DAV-HP plate is "considered in all respects as a special license plate for persons with disabilities" (§ 2138(d)(1)) and displays the wheelchair symbol, but requires a physician certification under § 2134 (cannot walk 200 feet without rest, needs assistive device, portable oxygen, Class III/IV cardiac, etc.).
- Veteran indicator on driver license is available (DMV Military Services page); no fee details given.

## 3. Recreation

### 3a. Free hunting, trapping, fishing license exemption (60%)

- Source: DNREC Division of Fish and Wildlife, https://dnrec.delaware.gov/fish-wildlife/licenses/exemptions/ (footer (c) MMXXVI); 7 Del. C. § 502(h), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title7/c005/sc01/index.html.
- DNREC: "Veterans with at least a 60% service-connected disability rating are eligible for a veteran license exemption." "Qualified applicants must complete the Veterans with Disabilities Exempt Application" and send it to the Division of Fish and Wildlife; "Please allow up to 4 weeks for processing."
- Statute § 502(h): "Persons who have been honorably discharged from the armed forces of the United States" and "certified by the Veterans Administration as having at least a 60% service-connected disability" "may be issued a license exemption to hunt, trap, and fish in this State without charge" "upon receipt by the Department of a written statement from the Veterans Administration" certifying discharge and disability. No residency requirement in the statute (the resident-only limit was removed by HB 156 in 2019).
- Also § 502(l): "Any resident who has served honorably for 90 or more consecutive days on active duty" may, "for the first 12 months following the date the resident was honorably discharged or removed from active status," "be issued a license to hunt and/or fish in this State without charge."

### 3b. State parks

- Source: Delaware State Parks, https://destateparks.com/Know/Passes (fetched via curl; no date shown); 7 Del. C. § 4701(a)(5), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title7/c047/sc01/index.html.
- Annual pass fees: "Annual Pass $50" (Delaware vehicle) / "$100" (out-of-state); "Delaware Military Pass (Active Duty, Reserve, Guard) $25 Plus HON veteran." Statute § 4701(a)(5)(e): a resident "who has honorably served or is honorably serving in the armed forces of the United States," "and who owns a motor vehicle validly registered in Delaware may pay an annual fee of 1/2 of the annual fee." Parks page: eligible if "A permanent resident of the state of Delaware" and "A veteran who was honorably discharged"; bring DD-214, Delaware license, Delaware registration in your name; "one Delaware Military Pass for each vehicle you own." Sold only at park offices.
- Free admission: § 4701(a)(5)(c) "Delaware residents who are disabled, and who hold a valid and current Gold Access Passport Card" "shall be exempted from the annual or daily entrance fees." § 4701(a)(5)(d) OIF/OEF: resident who "served honorably for 90 or more consecutive days on active duty" in Southwest Asia "may, for the first 12 months following the date the resident was honorably discharged or removed from active status, receive a pass granting the resident free admission." Issued only at 89 Kings Highway, Dover.
- There is NO free annual pass for disabled veterans (the "60%+" hint is not supported by the statute or the Parks site). The vets.delaware.gov page confirms only the "50% discount on the annual fee for vehicle entrance."

## 4. Education

### 4a. Educational Benefits for Children of Deceased Veterans and Others

- Source: 14 Del. C. ch. 34, subch. VI (§§ 3451-3455), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title14/c034/sc06/index.html; vets.delaware.gov/veterans-benefits/ ("Delaware Veterans Education Benefits"). Administered by the Delaware Higher Education Office (doe.k12.de.us/Page/1005 now redirects to education.delaware.gov; the DHEO scholarships page blocked our fetch).
- § 3452(a): child of a service member killed on active duty or who died of service-related causes, a POW, or MIA (also state police / DelDOT line-of-duty deaths); "at least 16 years of age, but not more than 24 years of age"; Delaware resident for at least 3 years before applying; enrolled in a post-high-school program of no more than 4 years. § 3452(b): parent must have been a Delaware resident when killed/died, or when declared MIA/POW. § 3455: benefits "may not exceed tuition and fees" per year, limited to 4 years, prorated outside the § 3454 priority order (Delaware public first).
- vets.delaware.gov: "between 16 and 24 years old," resident "for at least three years prior to application."
- Deadline: applications run through the Delaware Higher Education Financial Aid Portal; the cycle opens December 1 and closes in mid-May (official DHEO date for the 2027 cycle not confirmed from an agency page).
- No Delaware tuition waiver exists for disabled veterans or for dependents of living disabled veterans. Delaware National Guard tuition benefits exist for current Guard members only (vets.delaware.gov).

## 5. State income tax

Delaware has a personal income tax (and no general sales tax - stated here as general context; the Division of Revenue FAQ pages we fetched do not contain a quotable sentence on sales tax).

### 5a. Military pension exclusion

- Source: 30 Del. C. § 1106(b)(3), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title30/c011/sc02/index.html (history cites 85 Del. Laws c. 426); 2025 Form PIT-RES Instructions, Line 6, https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2025/PITForms_Instructions/Instructions/PIT-RES_Instructions_2025-01.pdf; SB 219 bill record, https://legis.delaware.gov/BillDetail/142742.
- PIT-RES 2025 instructions: "IF YOU WERE UNDER 60 on December 31, 2025 and retired from the United States military and received pension income during the year, your exclusion equals $12,500 or the amount of your pension, whichever is less." Non-military pension under 60: "$2,000." 60 or over: pension plus eligible retirement income, "Enter Line 3 or $12,500, whichever is less." "Each taxpayer may receive ONLY ONE pension exclusion." Disability pension income paid by an employer before minimum retirement age "would not qualify for the pension exclusion."
- Statute (as amended): under 60, the greater of $2,000 (general) or $12,500 military for 2022-2026; military $15,000 (2027), $20,000 (2028), "not to exceed $25,000, as a United States military pension" (2029+). 60 and older: $12,500 for 2022-2026; from 2027 the greater of $12,500 general or the military amount ($15,000 / $20,000 / $25,000). Residency for the 60+ exclusion: 3 years of domicile if domiciled before January 1, 2027; 5 years if domiciled on or after that date. Limits apply per spouse on a joint return.
- SB 219 (153rd GA) "AN ACT TO AMEND TITLE 30 OF THE DELAWARE CODE RELATING TO EXCLUSION OF MILITARY PENSIONS FROM TAXABLE INCOME": "Signed by the Governor on 8/17/26," session law 85 Del. Laws c. 426. The 2022 law (SB 188, 83 Del. Laws c. 338) raised the under-60 military amount from $2,000 to $12,500 effective tax years beginning 1/1/2022. Delaware's "all ages $12,500" description is accurate for 2022-2026 returns.

### 5b. VA disability compensation

- Delaware taxable income is computed from federal adjusted gross income with statutory modifications (30 Del. C. §§ 1105-1106). VA compensation, DIC and VA pension are excluded from federal gross income, so they are not taxed by Delaware. No Division of Revenue page states this explicitly (the PIT-RES instructions address only military pay, military spouses and the pension exclusion), so this row is flagged needs_verification.

## 6. State employment preference

- Source: Delaware Employment Link, https://statejobs.delaware.gov/explore/veterans-preference.shtml (no date); 29 Del. C. § 5935, https://delcode.delaware.gov/title29/c059/sc03/index.html. (The hint's § 5904 is not the preference section.)
- Statute § 5935(b): credits added to exam ratings, "Disabled veterans receiving no more than 10 points" and "other veterans or members who qualify under subsection (a) of this section no more than 5 points"; preference applies to "original entrance" only, not promotion or reduction in force. "Five preference points may be claimed by the spouse of" a deceased qualifying veteran (if not remarried), a member missing/captured/interned more than 90 days, or "a qualifying veteran or member with a service-connected disability." Guard/Reserve members with 20+ years also qualify.
- Employment Link: veterans "will be granted the appropriate preference points which will be added to their total ranking score"; "Preference shall be confined to original entrance to State Merit employment." Documents: "legible copy of Form DD214," VA letter for disability, NGB-22 for Guard, DD256 for reservists; submit to DHR "by 4:30 p.m. on the business day following the closing date on the posting." vets.delaware.gov confirms "10 points for disabled veterans and 5 for other eligible veterans."

## 7. Other Delaware benefits

- Delaware Veterans Home, Milford - https://vethome.delaware.gov/application-for-admission/: "Honorably discharged from active service (peacetime or wartime) with a minimum of 180 days of service" (Guard/Reserve 180 days active; or eligible for retirement pay at 60; or "Gold Star family member"); "Reside in the State of Delaware for at least one year before applying for admission"; "Have a medically determined need for a long-term level of care." 150 beds with dementia unit (vets.delaware.gov). Admissions: DOSDVH_Admissions@delaware.gov, (302) 424-6000. (A newer August 2026 application packet reportedly says 181 days; the web page says 180.)
- Delaware Veterans Trust Fund - https://vets.delaware.gov/veterans-trust-fund/: "Be a Delaware resident"; "Be a veteran with an Honorable Discharge or General Discharge under Honorable Conditions"; "Have a documented emergency financial need"; "Approval is not guaranteed"; "All funds are paid directly to the vendor or service provider." Documents: DD-214, Delaware ID, bills, two pay stubs, two bank statements; "Incomplete applications will not be processed." Email DOS_OVS@delaware.gov or drop off in Bear, Dover or Millsboro. Application PDF uploaded May 2026.
- Paraplegic Veterans Pension - vets.delaware.gov/veterans-benefits/: "$3,000 per year, payable in equal monthly installments" to veterans who are paraplegic and fully disabled due to wartime service. Statute section and application form not located online.
- Delaware Veterans Memorial Cemetery (Bear and Millsboro) - https://vets.delaware.gov/veterans-memorial-cemetery-interment-eligibility/: "Any Delaware resident who was honorably discharged from the armed forces of the United States"; "The Veteran must have been a legal resident for a period of 3 or more years (does not have to be consecutive)"; spouses and dependent children eligible. Fees not stated on the page.
- Also on vets.delaware.gov/veterans-benefits/: free notary service for veterans and families at OVS headquarters; free certified copy of a veteran's death certificate for next of kin; Veterans Opportunity Credit (employer tax credit, 10% of wages up to $1,500) for hiring veterans who served in a hostile environment; military spouse six-month temporary occupational license.
