# Georgia (GA) — State Benefits for Disabled Veterans

Verified 2026-10-16. Official agency: Georgia Department of Veterans Service (GDVS), https://veterans.georgia.gov/. GDVS's own caveat on its Tax Exemptions page: "The GDVS makes no guarantees regarding the awarding of tax exemptions. The administration of tax exemptions is as interpreted by the tax commissioners of Georgia's 159 counties." None of the GDVS, DOR, DNR, or DDS pages below display a last-updated date unless noted.

Search budget note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out near the end of this research; the last few items (ballot-measure status, parking statute, DNR lifetime page) were attempted by direct fetch only.

---

## 1. Property tax — Disabled Veteran Homestead Exemption (O.C.G.A. 48-5-48)

**Sources**
- GDVS, "Disabled Veteran Homestead Tax Exemption": https://veterans.georgia.gov/disabled-veteran-homestead-tax-exemption (no date shown)
- DOR, "Property Tax Homestead Exemptions": https://dor.georgia.gov/property-tax-homestead-exemptions (no date shown; cites 2025 amount)
- DeKalb County Tax Commissioner, "2026 Homestead Exemption Information" (PDF): https://dekalbtaxga.gov/wp-content/uploads/homestead-exemption-information.pdf
- GDVS O.C.G.A. reference list (cites 48-5-48 and 48-5-380): https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/1631

**Threshold language (GDVS, verbatim)** — "honorably discharged Georgia veterans considered disabled by any of these criteria":
- "VA-rated 100 percent totally disabled"
- "VA-rated less than 100 percent disabled but entitled as 100 percent rate due to unemployability"
- Statutory award for "Loss or permanent loss of use of one or both feet," "Loss or permanent loss of use of one or both hands," "Loss of sight in one or both eyes," "Permanent impairment of both eyes" (footnote: "Central visual acuity of 20/200 or less in the better eye...")

So: 100% schedular qualifies ("100 percent totally disabled"); TDIU qualifies (paid at the 100% rate due to unemployability); P&T is not stated as required for the homestead exemption (contrast the vehicle exemption below). SAH-type losses qualify without a 100% rating.

**Amount (DOR, verbatim):** "Any qualifying disabled veteran who is a citizen and resident of Georgia is granted an exemption of the greater of $32,500.00 or the maximum amount which may be granted to a disabled veteran" under federal law (38 U.S.C. 2102). "The amount for 2025 is $121,812." "The value of the property in excess of this exemption remains taxable." GDVS: "Any qualifying disabled veteran may be granted an exemption of up to $121,812 for 2025."

**2026 amount:** DOR/GDVS still show the 2025 figure. VA's housing-grants page states "you can get up to $126,526 for FY 2026" (https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/disability-housing-grants/). DeKalb County's 2026 sheet reads: "H5 – Disabled Veteran Exemption ... Provides an exemption of $129,026 for school levies and $126,526 for all other levies. The applicant must be an Honorably Discharged Veteran, own and reside at the property, and present a Letter of Adjudication from the U. S. Department of Veterans Affairs as being 100% totally disabled or being less than 100% totally disabled but is compensated at the 100% level due to individual un-employability." (The $129,026 school figure is a DeKalb local add-on.) The statewide 2026 figure of $126,526 is therefore well supported but not yet shown on a DOR page — flagged below.

**Residency / primary residence:** must be a "citizen and resident of Georgia" (DOR) and "own the home and use it as a primary residence" (GDVS). Owner must "have owned the property on January 1" of the tax year.

**Surviving spouse (DOR, verbatim):** "This exemption is extended to the unremarried surviving spouse or minor children ... as long as they continue to occupy the home as a residence."

**Deadline / office (DOR):** Applications go to "the tax commissioner's office, or in some counties the tax assessor's office has been delegated to receive applications." "Taxpayers can now apply for the homestead exemption beyond the historic deadline of April 1st," up to the end of the 45-day window to appeal the notice of assessment; for the current year the homeowner must have filed "by the same date property tax returns are due in the county" (April 1 in most counties; some counties use March 1 per older DOR material).

**Related:** Separate DOR exemption for the unremarried surviving spouse of a service member "killed in or died" as a result of war or armed conflict (O.C.G.A. 48-5-52.1): "$60,000 plus an additional sum" indexed; 2025 amount $121,812; "The surviving spouse will continue to be eligible for the exemption as long as they do not remarry."

**Pending (not law):** 2025-2026 session proposals SB 129 ("Georgia Supporting Those Who Sacrificed for Service Act," statewide expansion incl. partially disabled veterans, effective Jan 1, 2027 if approved Nov 2026), HR 400 (constitutional amendment for a full-value exemption), and HB 52 (surviving spouse extension). Final passage could not be confirmed (search budget exhausted). Varies by county: yes — counties layer local exemptions (e.g., DeKalb H5/H10) on top of the state one.

## 2. Vehicle — Ad valorem / TAVT exemption on one vehicle (O.C.G.A. 48-5-478)

**Sources**
- GDVS, Tax Exemptions (section "Ad Valorem Tax on Vehicles"): https://veterans.georgia.gov/book/export/html/1506
- DOR, Vehicle Taxes – TAVT and Annual Ad Valorem Tax: https://dor.georgia.gov/motor-vehicles/vehicle-taxes-title-ad-valorem-tax-tavt-and-annual-ad-valorem-tax
- DOR Form MV-30 (Rev. 06/2025): https://dor.georgia.gov/mv-30-georgians-veteran-affidavit-relief-state-and-local-title-ad-valorem-tax-fees

**Threshold language:**
- GDVS: "This exemption is for veterans who are verified by VA to be 100 percent totally and permanently service-connected disabled and veterans rated unemployable who receive or are entitled to" certain VA statutory awards (loss/loss of use of hands or feet, loss of sight). "The exemption is granted on ONLY one vehicle the veteran owns and upon which the free Disabled Veteran (DV) license plate is attached." "This exemption applies to either the annual property tax or the title tax, whichever is applicable." Purple Heart and Medal of Honor plate holders are also exempt.
- DOR: "Some Military Veterans are exempt from TAVT (Form MV-30 ... and Military Documentation required)": "Disabled Veterans compensated at 100%," "Prisoners of War," "Purple Heart Recipients," "Medal of Honor Recipients."
- MV-30 (verbatim): "O.C.G.A. § 48-5-478. Constitutional Exemption from ad valorem tax for disabled veterans, unremarried surviving spouse or minor child." Required document: "Letter from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs." "Owner must be a citizen and resident of Georgia." Affirmation: "No vehicle upon which I claim tax exemption as a veteran was conveyed to me for the purpose of obtaining exemption other than from my spouse."

Discrepancy: GDVS says "totally and permanently"; DOR says "compensated at 100%." The 2023 HB 175 (per search summary) removed the plate-display requirement from the statute and limited the exemption to one vehicle; GDVS's page still ties it to the DV plate. Counties' tag offices administer it — varies_by_county true.

## 3. Vehicle — Free Disabled Veteran license plate

**Sources**
- GDVS, License Plates: Disabled Veteran: https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/1556
- GDVS, all veteran plates: https://veterans.georgia.gov/book/export/html/1546
- DOR, Military & Veteran License Plates: https://dor.georgia.gov/military-veteran-license-plates

**Verbatim:** Eligibility — "VA-rated 100 percent totally disabled"; "VA-rated less than 100 percent disabled but paid at the 100 percent rate due to unemployability"; or VA statutory award for loss/loss of use of feet or hands, loss of sight, or permanent impairment of both eyes; also "Surviving, unremarried spouses of qualified deceased veterans" and surviving minor children. "There is no registration fee, manufacturing fee, or annual fee for this plate." "Veterans may receive disabled veteran license plates for up to 2 motorcycles or vehicles." Not issued for motor homes. DOR: "up to two motorcycles or passenger cars for personal use." Apply via DOR Special Tag Unit (404-968-3880) with a VA entitlement letter, or via a GDVS field office with a physician's certified statement.

**Parking:** No GDVS or DOR page found states a parking privilege for the DV plate. A non-official code mirror of O.C.G.A. 40-2-74.1(d) indicates DV-plate vehicles may park in disabled spaces without a permit; not confirmed on an official source — see "Could not verify."

**Other plates:** Veteran's, Woman Veteran's, Retired Military, Commemorative Service, Military Medal Award (incl. Purple Heart, Medal of Honor), Reserve Component, National Guard, Pearl Harbor Survivor, Former POW plates all carry "no registration fee, manufacturing fee, or annual fee" (one plate). Form MV-9W at the county tag office. No GDVS page lists a plate for 10%-rated veterans.

## 4. Vehicle — Sales tax exemption on VA-grant adapted vehicle (O.C.G.A. 48-8-3(30))

MV-30 verbatim: "The sale of a vehicle to a service-connected disabled veteran where the veteran received a grant from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs to purchase and specially adapt the vehicle to his disability." Required: "Grant letter from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs." GDVS: "A disabled veteran who receives a VA grant for the purchase and special adapting of a vehicle is exempt" — applies only to the original grant purchase.

## 5. Free Veteran driver's license / ID

**Sources**
- DDS, "Veterans License Process Simplified" (dated February 01, 2022): https://dds.georgia.gov/node/31701
- GDVS, Veteran Driver's License: https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/1521

DDS verbatim: "Veterans who are Georgia residents, who actively served and were released with any discharge, other than dishonorable" are eligible for "a free Driver's License and/or Identification Card." "DDS will now accept the Armed Forces Separation Papers (DD-214, Mbr copy 4) directly at any Customer Service Center." "DDS continues to accept the GDVS Certificate of Eligibility." GDVS (stricter wording): "Only veterans with an honorable discharge or a medical discharge are entitled to a free veteran driver's license"; "Veterans must be a current resident of Georgia"; National Guard with active duty or 20 years also qualify. The two agencies' discharge wording differs; DDS's 2022 notice is the newer one.

## 6. Hunting & fishing

**Sources**
- DNR WRD, One-Time Veterans License: https://georgiawildlife.com/licenses-permits-passes/veterans
- DNR WRD, Resident Disability License Options: https://georgiawildlife.com/licenses-permits-passes/disability
- GDVS, Hunting & Fishing Licenses: https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/1566

**One-time veterans license (DNR, verbatim):** "available free to Georgia resident military veterans who served on active Federal duty for 90 or more days" and "were honorably discharged on July 1, 2005 or later." "This free license is valid for one year from date of issue," and "may be issued only once." Federal Duck Stamp fee still applies. Attach a photocopy of DD-214 and Georgia license/ID.

**Disability license (DNR fees, verbatim):** "Resident Sportsman's Disability License - Three-Year $15.00 + $3.00 transaction fee = $18.00 - Annual $5.00 + $3.00 transaction fee = $8.00"; hunting-only and fishing-only three-year $12.00 / annual $6.00. "Persons born on or after January 1, 1961 must have hunter education to obtain a disability license that includes hunting." "As long as your disability is permanent and your application is approved, you do not have to resubmit a Disability Application again!" Apply via the WRD Certification of Disability at gooutdoorsgeorgia.com or by mail. DNR's application form lists as an accepted certifier "United States Department of Veterans Affairs (100% disability)." GDVS wording: "Veterans with a permanent and total disability rating from VA (or another government agency)" "are eligible for a 3-year disability sportsman's license at a steep discount from regular license cost"; "Veterans disabled for mental impairment may obtain a discounted disability fishing license."

**Lifetime discount (GDVS, verbatim):** "Honorably discharged resident veterans may purchase a Georgia Adult Lifetime Sportsman's License at a 20% discount" "(ages 16 - 49)"; Older Adult Lifetime at 50, Senior Discount Lifetime at 60. DNR: "Georgia offers a discounted full privilege veterans lifetime license" (no price shown; DNR lifetime page returned 404).

No "Disability Honorary License" by that name was found; the current names are the One-Time Veterans License and the Resident Disability Licenses.

## 7. State parks

**Sources:** https://gastateparks.org/Reservations and https://gastateparks.org/ParkPass (no dates shown)

Verbatim: "Georgia Resident Disabled Veteran discounts are established by Georgia Code 12-3-9.1." "Disabled veterans who are Georgia residents AND present a Certificate of Eligibility card are entitled to a 25% discount" "on individual accommodations, recreation fees such as mini-golf and boat rental," "golf fees, parking fees, and historic site admission." "Limited to one accommodation per person." "When making online reservations, the full fee will be charged to your credit card." "Upon check-in and presenting your Certificate of Eligibility card, you will be reimbursed for your discount." ParkPass: "Parking in Georgia's State Parks is just $10 per vehicle"; "$70 Annual ParkPass"; "Annual ParkPass discounts of 50% are available for seniors 62 and older, and 25% for active military and veterans"; "Discounted passes must be purchased in person." (News reports say the $10/$70 rates took effect January 1, 2026; the official page shows the rates without an effective date.) The VA rating needed for the GDVS Certificate of Eligibility card is not stated on either page.

## 8. Education

**Sources**
- GSFC GAfutures, HERO eligibility: https://gafutures.org/hope-state-aid-programs/scholarships-grants/hero/eligibility/ ; award amounts: https://www.gafutures.org/hope-state-aid-programs/scholarships-grants/hero/award-amounts/
- GDVS Educational Benefits: https://veterans.georgia.gov/services/education-and-training/educational-benefits
- USG, Military Services and Benefits (cites Policy 7.3.4.1): https://www.usg.edu/student_affairs/military/services_and_benefits
- GSW Recently Separated Military Personnel Waiver form (USG institution): https://www.gsw.edu/registrar/_files/waivers/Recently-Separated-Military-Personnel-Waiver.pdf
- GAfutures UNG Military Scholarship Loan: https://www.gafutures.org/hope-state-aid-programs/military/ung-military-program/eligibility/

**HERO (verbatim):** "Georgia Residency requirements for 12 consecutive months immediately prior to the start of the term." Categories: "Be an active member of the Georgia National Guard or U.S. Military Reserves who is a Georgia resident" (deployed to a combat zone); child of such a member (generally 25 or younger during the service); child of a member "killed while serving in such combat zone, or died or became 100 percent disabled as a result of injuries"; "Be the spouse of a former member of the Georgia National Guard or U.S. Military Reserves who was a Georgia resident" killed, died, or 100% disabled from combat-zone injuries. Award: "Eligible recipients may receive up to $2,000 per academic school year." "Students who meet the eligibility requirements of Categories 1, 3, or 4 may receive up to $8,000 for the equivalent of four academic years of study." "The scholarship is awarded on a first-come, first-served basis and as appropriated funds are available."

**In-state tuition:** USG lists "Out-of-state Tuition Waivers for Military Personnel, Recently Separated Military Service Personnel, and National Guard Members" under "Section 7.3.4.1 of the Board of Regents Policy Manual." The policy text itself now lives on usg.policystat.com and could not be retrieved. GSW's form (older wording): "(a) an individual who within thirty-six (36) months of separation from such service enroll in an academic program and demonstrate intent to become domiciled in Georgia. This waiver may also be granted to their spouses and dependent children; or, (b) any separated service member or any student utilizing transferred VA educational benefits, and physically residing in the state, who enrolls within one hundred-twenty (120) months of separation." A USG Chancellor's letter (Aug. 2021) says the 36-month and 120-month limits had to be removed for "covered individuals" under 38 U.S.C. 3679(c). Needs verification.

**UNG Military Scholarship Loan:** "receive a full, four-year scholarship" at University of North Georgia; "Be a legal resident of Georgia, as defined by GSFC, for a minimum of 12 consecutive months"; commission into Georgia Army National Guard. Not a disabled-veteran benefit; omitted from JSON.

**Dependents of disabled veterans:** No Georgia tuition waiver for children/spouses of disabled veterans was found on GDVS, GSFC, or USG (other than HERO's combat-zone 100%-disabled category).

**VECTR:** https://www.gavectr.org/ — "Connecting veterans to careers through education and training." "1001 S. Armed Forces Blvd, Warner Robins, GA 31088." Training at "little to no cost if you are a Georgia resident, are stationed in Georgia, or are transitioning military." Footer credits Central Georgia Technical College; GDVS officers on site.

## 9. Income tax

**Sources**
- DOR, Retirement Income Exclusion: https://dor.georgia.gov/retirement-income-exclusion
- GDVS, Military Retirement Income Tax Exemption: https://veterans.georgia.gov/military-retirement-income-tax-exemption
- HB 266 (2025) as passed House and Senate, Governor's signed-legislation copy: https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2025-signed-legislation/hb-266/download
- OPB fiscal note HB 266 LC 50 1209S (Mar. 20, 2025): https://opb.georgia.gov/document/fiscal-notes-2025/lc-50-1209s/download

Georgia has a state income tax. **DOR (verbatim):** "Beginning January 1, 2022, $17,500 of military retirement income can be excluded for taxpayers under 62 years of age" "and an additional $17,500 can be excluded for taxpayers with more than $17,500 of earned income in Georgia." "The exclusion is available for the taxpayers and his/her spouse; however, each must qualify on a separate basis." General exclusion: "Taxpayers who are 62 or older, or permanently and totally disabled regardless of age" (worksheet in Form IT-511). **GDVS:** "Veterans who retired from the military and are under 62 years of age are eligible for an exemption of up to $17,500"; 62-64 up to $35,000; "Those over the age of 65 are eligible for an exemption of up to $65,000."

**2027 change — HB 266 as passed (verbatim):** "(5.1)(A) Income received by an individual who is less than 65 years of age paid to such individual as retirement benefits from military service in the armed forces of the United States or the reserve components thereof in an amount that shall not exceed $65,000.00. (B) In the case of a married couple filing jointly, each spouse who is otherwise qualified ... shall be individually entitled to exclude retirement income received by that spouse up to the exclusion amount. (C) ... provided, however, that no individual shall be allowed an exclusion provided for in this paragraph in addition to any exclusion provided for in paragraph (5) of this subsection." Section 3(b): "Section 1 of this Act shall become effective on January 1, 2027, and shall be applicable to all taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2027." (The OPB fiscal note describes an earlier phased-in committee version; the final text is a flat $65,000 from TY 2027.) DOR's page has not yet been updated for this.

**VA compensation:** Not addressed on any Georgia page found. Georgia taxable income starts from federal AGI, which excludes VA disability compensation; flagged as not directly confirmed on a Georgia source.

## 10. Employment

**Source:** State Personnel Board Rule 478-1-.18 Veterans' Preference (Revision Date 11/28/2017), DOAS: https://doas.ga.gov/assets/Human%20Resources%20Administration/State%20Personnel%20Board%20Rules/478-1-.18%20Veterans%20Preference%20%2011.28.17.pdf — authority O.C.G.A. 45-20-1; references O.C.G.A. 45-2-20, 45-2-21, 45-2-22 and Ga. Const. Art. IV, § III, Para. II. (The hint's "45-2-1" is not the cited section.)

**Verbatim:** "(2)(a) Veterans, as defined in Section (1)(b), will be given five (5) points in addition to their earned passing ratings on any numerically scored written entrance examination. (b) The following individuals will be given ten (10) points in addition to their earned passing ratings and in lieu of the five (5) points ...: 1. Veterans who establish by official records the present existence of a service-connected disability of at least 10%, as rated and certified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs; 2. Veterans over 55 years of age who because of disability, whether service-connected or not, are entitled to pension or compensation under existing laws; 3. Spouses of disabled veterans ... if the spouses are qualified and the disabled veterans themselves are disqualified for appointment because of the disability; or, 4. Unmarried widows or widowers of deceased veterans of any period of armed conflict." "(c) The point preference is in lieu of and not in addition to any other similar preference accorded under federal or state law." "(3) ... If the hiring agency does not use numerically scored written examination to fill positions, the hiring agency must give appropriate consideration to persons eligible for veterans' preferences." "(4) ... Preference does not apply in cases of promotion, demotion, or transfer to a different job." "A general discharge does not disqualify a person for veterans' preferences." Documentation: "DD 214."

**Business Certificate of Exemption (O.C.G.A. 43-12-1)** — GDVS https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/18001 (verbatim): "discharged under honorable conditions" and "a service-connected disability rating of 10 percent or more"; exempt "for a period of ten years" from local occupation taxes, administrative fees, and regulatory fees for "peddling, conducting business, or practicing a profession or semi-profession." Apply with VS Form 40-025, ID, DD-214, and VA Summary of Benefits letter at the County Probate Court; some counties also require VS Form 40-026. HB 632 (2025-2026) proposed clarifying eligibility; enactment not confirmed.

## 11. Unique to Georgia — State War Veterans Homes

**Source:** GDVS Eligibility Criteria: https://veterans.georgia.gov/node/2076 (verbatim): "The veteran must be a current Georgia resident and meet one of the following conditions: Resided in Georgia two years immediately preceding the date of application; Resided in Georgia five or more of the past 15 years." Wartime periods: "World War II: December 7, 1941 - December 31, 1946; Post-World War II: January 1, 1947 - June 26, 1950; Korea: June 27, 1950 - January 31, 1955; Cold War: January 31, 1955 - August 1, 1990; Vietnam: July 1, 1964 - May 7, 1975; Persian Gulf: August 2, 1990 - present." "The veteran must have a discharge under other than dishonorable conditions." "The veteran must be approved as eligible for skilled nursing care by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)." "A nominal daily fee is charged." "For veterans with a service-connected disability rating of 70 percent or higher" "or whose admission is due to a service-connected condition, the daily fee is paid in full by VA." Homes: Augusta (with Augusta University) and Milledgeville.

6. **SB 129, HR 400, HB 52** (property tax expansions for disabled veterans / surviving spouses, possible November 2026 ballot) — final legislative status not confirmed; none is in effect now.
7. **VA disability compensation not taxed by Georgia** — no Georgia page states it; inferred from Georgia's use of federal AGI.
8. **"Totally and permanently" vs "100%" for the vehicle ad valorem exemption** — GDVS and DOR wording differ; county tag offices decide.
9. **Discounted veterans lifetime license price** — DNR lifetime page returned 404; GDVS states 20% off (ages 16-49) with no price.
