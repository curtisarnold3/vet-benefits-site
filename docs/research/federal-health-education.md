# Research notes: VA health care, CHAMPVA, Ch 35, Ch 31, Ch 33, dental
Verified Oct 16, 2026 against va.gov / benefits.va.gov / law.cornell.edu (eCFR blocked scripted fetches; LII mirrors used).

## Health care
- Priority groups page: https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/priority-groups/ (last updated Apr 3, 2024)
- PG1: 50%+ or unemployable (TDIU) or MOH. PG2: 30-40%. PG3: 10-20%, POW, Purple Heart, discharged for disability, 1151. PG4: A&A/housebound or catastrophically disabled. PG5: 0% non-compensable or NSC with low income, pension, Medicaid. PG6: 0% compensable, PACT Act toxic exposure, combat vet within 10 yrs of discharge, etc. PG7/8 by income.
- Copays: https://www.va.gov/health-care/copay-rates/ (effective Jan 1, 2026). 10%+ no inpatient/outpatient copay. 50%+ no medication copay. Outpatient primary $15, specialty $50. Rx tiers $5/$8/$11 per 30 days, $700 annual cap. Inpatient PG7 $347.20+$2/day; PG8 $1,736+$10/day.
- 38 CFR 17.36, 17.108, 17.110. Combat vet 10-year window: 38 USC 1710(e)(3)(A) (PACT Act).
- Form 10-10EZ: https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-10-10ez/ ; apply https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: content of 91 FR 47143 (July 28, 2026) amendment to 17.108.

## CHAMPVA
- https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/health-and-disability/champva/ (Aug 7, 2026)
- Spouse/child of veteran rated P&T SC; or survivor of veteran who died of SC condition or was P&T at death. Not for TRICARE-eligible. Secondary to Medicare (must have A and B).
- Deductible $50/person, $100/family; 25% cost share; $3,000 cat cap. Form 10-10d. 38 CFR 17.271, 17.274.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: remarriage rules for surviving spouses.

## Chapter 35 DEA
- https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/education-and-careers/dependents-education-assistance/ (Sep 22, 2026); rates https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/education-and-careers/dependents-education-assistance/rates/ (Oct 14, 2026)
- Trigger: veteran "permanently and totally disabled due to a service-connected disability" (38 USC 3501(a)(8), 38 CFR 21.3021(p), 3.340(b)). A 100% not marked permanent does NOT qualify. TDIU marked P&T is a total rating; VA pages do not spell out "TDIU" (NEEDS VERIFICATION wording).
- 36 months (45 if started before Aug 1, 2018). Rates change Oct 1. FY27 (Oct 1, 2026): full-time $1,621/mo; 3/4 $1,281; 1/2 $939; 1/4 or less $405.25. Prior year full-time $1,574.
- Children: eligibility on/after Aug 1, 2023 = any age, no time limit; before = 8 yrs / age 26. Spouses: event on/after Aug 1, 2023 no time limit; before = 10 yrs (20 if active-duty death).
- Form 22-5490. 2026 change: Ch 35 no longer pays for secondary (high school) programs starting on/after Aug 1, 2026 (PL 117-328) NEEDS VERIFICATION of article details.

## Chapter 31 VR&E
- https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/eligibility/ (Nov 7, 2025)
- 20%+ with employment handicap, or 10% with serious employment handicap (38 CFR 21.40, 21.51, 21.52; 38 USC 3102).
- 12-year limit does NOT apply to anyone discharged on/after Jan 1, 2013 (38 USC 3103(g), added by PL 116-315, Jan 5, 2021). Pre-2013: 12 years from later of separation or first SC rating; extendable for SEH.
- 5 tracks: Reemployment; Rapid Access to Employment; Self-Employment; Employment Through Long-Term Services; Independent Living.
- Form 28-1900. Subsistence FY27 (Oct 1, 2026) full-time institutional: $837.23 / $1,038.49 / $1,223.79 (+$89.18 each addl); may elect Post-9/11 BAH rate instead. https://www.benefits.va.gov/vocrehab/subsistence_allowance_rates.asp

## Chapter 33 Post-9/11 GI Bill
- https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/ (Jul 23, 2026); rates https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/ (Aug 1, 2026-Jul 31, 2027)
- 90 days AD after 9/11/01, or Purple Heart, or 30 continuous days + SC disability discharge. Tiers: 36+ mo 100%; 30-35 90%; 24-29 80%; 18-23 70%; 6-17 60%; 90 days-5 mo 50%. Private school cap $30,908.34/yr. Books $1,000/yr. No expiration if service ended on/after Jan 1, 2013; else 15 years.
- Transfer: 6 yrs served + 4 more; via DoD milConnect; child needs 10 yrs served, under 26. Forms 22-1990, 22-1990e.
- Fry Scholarship: https://www.va.gov/education/survivor-dependent-benefits/fry-scholarship/ ; form 22-5490.

## Dental
- https://www.va.gov/health-care/about-va-health-benefits/dental-care/ (Jan 12, 2026); 38 CFR 17.161
- Class I: SC compensable dental. IIA: SC noncompensable from combat/service trauma. IIB: homeless program. IIC: former POW. II: one-time within 180 days of discharge (90+ days Gulf War era service, DD214 lacks dental exam note). III: dental aggravating SC medical condition. IV: 100% schedular OR TDIU at 100% rate (temporary 100% does not count; P&T not required). V: Ch 31 participant. VI: inpatient.
- VADIP: Delta Dental / MetLife; enrolled vets and CHAMPVA beneficiaries; enrollee pays premium. https://www.va.gov/health-care/about-va-health-benefits/dental-care/dental-insurance/ (May 1, 2024)

## Cross-cutting
- P&T (Ch 35, CHAMPVA) != 100% (dental Class IV; PG1 is 50%+). Rate calendars: copays Jan 1; Ch 35 + VR&E Oct 1; Ch 33 Aug 1; compensation Dec 1.
