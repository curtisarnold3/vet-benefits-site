## 1. Property tax

Maryland's state-level veteran exemption is set by statute and uniform statewide; it is filed with SDAT's county Supervisor of Assessments. The partial-rating credit is a local option under state enabling law, so it varies by county and city.

### 1a. Full exemption, 100% permanent service-connected disability (Tax-Property 7-208)
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtp&section=7-208&enactments=false (current text, 2025 Supplement); SDAT exemptions page https://dat.maryland.gov/realproperty/Pages/Property-Tax-Exemptions.aspx (no date shown; forms are "0726-A" files, Rev Feb 2025); DVMF State Property Tax Exemptions page (Updated January 16, 2026).
- Threshold language (7-208(a)(3)): "'Disabled veteran' means an individual who: 1. is honorably discharged or released under honorable circumstances from active military, naval, or air service as defined in 38 U.S.C. § 101; and 2. has been declared by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to have a permanent 100% service connected disability that results from blindness or other disabling cause that: A. is reasonably certain to continue for the life of the veteran; and B. was not caused or incurred by misconduct of the veteran." Also: "'Disabled veteran' includes an individual who qualifies posthumously for a 100% service connected disability."
- TDIU / 100% schedular / 100% P&T: The statute says "permanent 100%" and "reasonably certain to continue for the life of the veteran," so a permanent and total rating qualifies and a temporary (non-permanent) 100% schedular rating does not. The statute does not mention unemployability. However SDAT's page states an Attorney General's Opinion (61 Op. Att'y Gen. 836 (1976)) allows the exemption where the rating is below 100% if the VA finds the veteran permanently unemployable, and SDAT's application form says: "Document from the V.A. must indicate that the veteran's final service-connected disability rating is 100% permanent and total or permanently unemployable. This document must include the disability rating's effective date and date of the V.A. rating's decision." The VA certification block on the form asks "Is the veteran permanently unemployable?" and "receiving disability payments as allowed for reasons of ___% disability, or ___% unemployability." So permanent TDIU is accepted by the administering agency. SDAT: the "VA Summary of Benefit letter is not accepted."
- Dwelling house (7-208(a)(4)): "the legal residence of a disabled active duty service member, disabled veteran, or surviving spouse; and ... occupied by not more than 2 families; and ... includes the lot or curtilage and structures necessary to use the real property as a residence."
- Amount: full exemption from property tax (state, county, municipal) on the dwelling house. 7-208(e): granted "in addition to any other exemption authorized by law," except not together with the 7-207 blind exemption.
- Deadline and office: no deadline; DVMF: "Veterans and surviving spouses may apply at any time and are not bound by the September 1 filing deadline." File SDAT "Application for Exemption for Disabled Veterans" (Rev Feb 2025) with the Supervisor of Assessments in the county where the property is (office list attached to the form). Required: DD-214 or discharge, VA rating decision packet (or VA certification on the form), and Maryland residency proof ("current Maryland driver's license, voter's registration or redacted previous year Maryland income taxes"; a contract of sale if the purchase is pending).
- Proration and settlement (7-208(f)): exemption "is prorated by the supervisor for any part of a taxable year that remains after the date in the year when the ... disabled veteran ... applies." If the dwelling is transferred to a qualifying veteran, "the exemption applies and the property tax is abated from the date of settlement for the purchase of the property, if the transferee applies for the exemption within 30 days after the settlement."
- Pre-purchase application (7-208(d)(5)): SDAT "shall accept, an application ... for a specific dwelling house the individual intends to purchase before the individual purchases" and "within 15 business days" must send a preliminary approval or denial letter; a preliminarily approved applicant gets the exemption "without having to file another application."
- Refunds (7-208(g)-(h)): the State, county, or municipality "shall pay a refund" for years the exemption "was authorized but not granted," but only if the veteran "applies for the exemption during the 3-year period beginning with the calendar year in which the ... disabled veteran initially became eligible." Interest is owed if the refund is not paid within 60 days of the refund application. SDAT: refunds "begin with tax year 2018-2019"; eligibility date is the date of the VA rating decision granting the 100% P&T rating, with the rating's effective date determining the refund start.
- Residency / ownership / income caps: must own the dwelling and use it as legal (principal) residence; must be a Maryland resident (SDAT form). No income cap.
- Disabled active duty service member (7-208(a)(2)): "an individual in active service ... who has a service connected physical disability that: (i) is reasonably certain to continue for the life of the service member; and (ii) was not caused or incurred by misconduct." Certification by a Maryland-licensed physician or the VA; SDAT requires annual "Certification of Active Duty Status."
- County variation: none for the state exemption. 7-208(i): "Each county shall include information on the property tax bill about the availability of the property tax exemption."
- 2025-2026 changes: 2026 Md. Laws ch. 647 (HB 842, approved May 26, 2026, effective June 1, 2026, taxable years after June 30, 2026) amended 7-208(b)(1)(iii) to repeal the requirement that a line-of-duty surviving spouse acquire the home "within 2 years of the individual's death" (see 1b). HB 644 (2026), "Exemption for Dwellings of Surviving Spouses of Disabled Veterans - Application," had a hearing 2/17/2026 and shows no further action (not enacted). No 2025 session chapter amending 7-208 was found.

### 1b. Surviving spouse exemption (Tax-Property 7-208(a)(6), (b)(1)(iii)-(iv), (c))
- Source: statute above; SDAT exemptions page; SDAT "Application for Exemption for Surviving Spouses of a Disabled Veterans" (SDATRP_EX4B, Rev Feb 2025).
- Threshold language: "'Surviving spouse' means an individual who has not remarried and who: (i) is the surviving spouse of a disabled veteran; (ii) is the surviving spouse of an individual who died in the line of duty; or (iii) receives Dependency and Indemnity Compensation from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs."
- Carryover (7-208(c)): after the veteran dies the spouse "shall receive a disabled veteran's property tax exemption: (1) for the dwelling house that was formerly owned by the disabled veteran: (i) if the dwelling house received an exemption under this section; and (ii) if the surviving spouse owns and resides in the dwelling house; (2) for the dwelling house that was formerly occupied by the disabled veteran: (i) if the dwelling house did not receive an exemption under this section; (ii) if the disabled veteran was domiciled in the State at death; and (iii) if the surviving spouse owns and resides in the dwelling house; and (3) for a dwelling house subsequently acquired by the surviving spouse, equal to the exemption for the former dwelling house ..."
- Remarriage: must "not [have] remarried." SDAT: "A spouse who remarries and later divorces does not qualify. An annulment that voids the later marriage from the start may reinstate it."
- Line-of-duty death (7-208(b)(1)(iii), as amended by ch. 647): exempt if "1. the dwelling house was owned by the individual at the time of the individual's death; OR 2. the dwelling house was acquired by the surviving spouse AND the individual or the surviving spouse was domiciled in the State as of the date of the individual's death." The former "within 2 years" limit and the former item 3 were repealed.
- Form requires the veteran's DD-214, VA rating packet showing "100% permanent and total or permanently unemployable," Maryland residency proof, date of death, and answers to "Has the applicant remarried?" and "Was the veteran a Maryland resident at time of death?" Refund window: 3 years from the calendar year the spouse first became eligible (7-208(g)(3)).
- Registration fee tie-in: Transportation 13-903(a)(9) also exempts "A vehicle owned and personally used by an individual who is the surviving spouse of a deceased disabled veteran, as defined under § 7-208."

### 1c. Local-option credit for veterans rated 50% to 99% (Tax-Property 9-265)
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtp&section=9-265&enactments=false ; 2026 Md. Laws ch. 646 PDF https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2026RS/chapters_noln/Ch_646_hb1611T.pdf ; DVMF Local Property Tax Exemptions page (Updated January 16, 2026); Anne Arundel County application (FY2028); Frederick County information sheet; Howard County Finance tax credits page.
- Threshold language (9-265(a)(2)): "'Disabled veteran' means an individual who: (i) is honorably discharged ...; and (ii) 1. has been declared by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to have a permanent service-connected disability of at least 50% that results from blindness or any other disabling cause that: A. is reasonably certain to continue for the life of the veteran; and B. was not caused or incurred by misconduct of the veteran; or 2. has been declared by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to have a nonpermanent service-connected disability of 100% ..." Counties may, under (f)(5), limit the credit to the permanent-rating group only.
- Amount (9-265(c)): "(1) 50% of the county or municipal corporation property tax imposed on the dwelling house if the disabled veteran's service-connected disability rating is at least 75% and the disabled veteran does not qualify for a property tax exemption under § 7-208 of this article; or (2) 25% of the county or municipal corporation property tax imposed on the dwelling house if the disabled veteran's service-connected disability rating is at least 50% but not more than 74%." County/municipal tax only; not state tax.
- TDIU: not mentioned in 9-265. A TDIU veteran whose rating is permanent would qualify for the state exemption instead (per SDAT's reading of 7-208); a non-permanent TDIU veteran would fall under 9-265 only if the county treats the paid-at-100% rate as a "nonpermanent ... disability of 100%" or the scheduler rating is 50%+. Unstated by any official source.
- Income cap: until June 30, 2026, 9-265(b)(2) required that "the disabled veteran's federal adjusted gross income for the immediately preceding taxable year does not exceed $100,000." Ch. 646 (HB 1611, approved May 26, 2026; effective June 1, 2026; "applicable to all taxable years beginning after June 30, 2026") repealed that cap and instead lets the local government set "eligibility criteria for the tax credit that limits the credit based on a disabled veteran's federal adjusted gross income for the immediately preceding taxable year." (An earlier draft would have raised the cap to $150,000 single / $300,000 joint; the enacted text deletes the cap entirely.) Anne Arundel's FY2028 form (due April 1, 2027) still asks whether federal AGI was "$100,000 or less" for 2026, so counties may keep the $100,000 figure in local code.
- Application (9-265(d)): to the county or municipality (not SDAT), with the discharge certificate and "on the form provided by the county or municipal corporation, a certification of the disabled veteran's disability from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs."
- Surviving spouse (9-265(e)): the local government "may, by law, continue to provide the property tax credit ... to the surviving spouse." Frederick: unremarried surviving spouse qualifies if the veteran owned the home at death.
- Residency/ownership: dwelling house must be the veteran's "legal residence" and "occupied by not more than two families."
- County variation: yes, entirely local option. DVMF lists Anne Arundel, Baltimore County, Calvert, Cecil, Frederick, Harford, Howard, Montgomery, and Washington counties, with the caveat that the list may be incomplete and counties may change rules. Examples: Anne Arundel (County Code 4-2-318): annual application due April 1, 50%/25% tiers, $100,000 AGI cap, county tax only, "applied after any mandatory property tax credits." Frederick (County Code 1-8-68, City Code 8-10.3): due October 1 each year, 50%/25% of "net County and Frederick City property tax," $100,000 cap; credit on the July bill if received by April 15. Howard County's page shows a different structure (an "Aging in Place" credit of 20% of county tax on value up to $650,000, for age 65+ veterans rated 75% to 99%, 10-year limit, no income cap, due October 1) and does not cite 9-265.
- 2025-2026 changes: ch. 646 (2026) above. SB 980 (2026, cross-file) passed the House with amendments 4/02/2026 and shows no final action. 2024 ch. 916 (SB 6) had added the nonpermanent-100% tier. No 2025 chapter found.

### 1d. Other local-option credits (Tax-Property 9-258 and new 9-267.1) - not rows, noted only
- 9-258 (https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtp&section=9-258&enactments=false): counties and municipalities may grant a credit for an "eligible individual," including "an individual who: 1. is an active duty, retired, or honorably discharged service member; and 2. has a service-connected disability as defined in a local law enacted under this section" and their unremarried surviving spouse, plus retired service members 65+. Amount, duration, maximum assessed value, and disability criteria are all set locally. (Howard County's age-65 veteran credit appears to be of this type.)
- 9-267.1 (added by 2026 ch. 647, effective June 1, 2026): a county or municipality "may grant, by law, a property tax credit ... on the dwelling house of a surviving spouse if the surviving spouse is ineligible for the property tax exemption under § 7-208." Amount, duration, and eligibility are local. No county adoptions found yet.
- Tax-Property 7-208.1 does not exist (statute lookup returned "File Not Found"). The blind exemption is 7-207 ($40,000 assessed value, per SDAT) and cannot be combined with 7-208.

## 2. Vehicles, driver license, parking

### 2a. Registration fee exemption, Disabled Veteran plates, permanent registration (Transportation 13-903(a)(8), (d); MVA)
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtr&section=13-903&enactments=false ; MVA Military Service Members: License Plates https://mva.maryland.gov/your-mva-guide/military-service-members/license-plates (no date; © 2026); MVA brochure DL-401 (06-22) https://mva.maryland.gov/Documents/DL-401.pdf
- Threshold language (13-903(a)(8)): exempt from registration fees is "A vehicle owned by, or leased to, and personally used by a veteran who: (i) As designated or classified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, has lost the use of a hand, arm, or leg, or is totally disabled; or (ii) Has a permanent impairment of both eyes so that: 1. The central visual acuity is 20/200 or less in the better eye, with corrective glasses; or 2. There is a field defect in which the peripheral field has contracted to such an extent that the widest diameter of visual field subtends an angular distance no greater than 20 degrees in the better eye". (a)(9): surviving spouse of a 7-208 disabled veteran. (d): such a veteran "may, if eligible, receive the special registration number and special registration plates provided under § 13-616, § 13-617, § 13-618, § 13-619, § 13-619.1, or § 13-619.2 ... without payment of the registration fees." 13-619(f): the organizational plate surcharge "is not required for special registration of a vehicle that is exempt under § 13-903."
- MVA page: "If you are a veteran who is rated at 100% disabled, you can receive your license plates free of charge"; no other fees at the appointment; eligible for permanent registration; plates with or without the wheelchair symbol. 50-99% tier: "service-connected disability rating or unemployability rating of 50-99%": plates free, but "you still pay annual registration fees and the surcharge"; no wheelchair symbol; "Disabled Veteran" at the bottom. Vanity/organizational/background plates: pay the plate fee, not eligible for permanent registration, "but registration fees are not charged." DL-401: "MDOT MVA offers permanent vehicle registration cards and license plate stickers to Maryland veterans who qualify as 100 percent disabled ... MDOT MVA also waives registration fees for vehicles owned by qualified disabled veterans."
- TDIU: the statute's phrase is "totally disabled" as "designated or classified by" the VA. MVA's page puts an "unemployability rating of 50-99%" in the pay-fees tier, which suggests MVA does not treat TDIU as "totally disabled." No official page says this directly. 2025 SB 179 / HB 248 and 2026 HB 844 ("Motor Vehicle Registration - Fee Exemption - Unemployable Disabled Veterans," one vehicle) were not enacted (HB 844 passed the House 130-0 and died after a Senate hearing 4/01/2026). Flagged needs_verification.
- Number of vehicles: statute says "A vehicle"; MVA page states no limit. Not confirmed either way.
- How to apply: appointment via myMVA (disabled veteran plates cannot be ordered online), with "a letter on Maryland Department of Veterans and Military Families (DVMF) letterhead showing your disability rating" or VA documentation; by mail with form VR-120 "Application for Military Related License Plates" to the Organizational Plates Unit, 6601 Ritchie Hwy NE, Glen Burnie, MD 21062 (a $25 tag fee applies to ordinary military plates by mail).
- Purple Heart plates: exist among MVA's "Other Military & Veteran plates" but the fee and any waiver were not confirmed on an official page (13-903(d) lists 13-619.1 and 13-619.2 plates as fee-free for exempt veterans; I did not confirm which plates those sections cover).

## 3. Hunting, fishing, parks

### 3a. Complimentary lifetime hunting and fishing licenses (DNR)
- Source: https://dnr.maryland.gov/pages/military-one-stop-shop.aspx (no date shown); DVMF Recreation page (Updated January 11, 2026).
- Threshold language (DNR): "A complimentary lifetime license is available to Maryland residents who are 100% service-connected disabled veterans or former prisoners of war or are deemed unemployable by the US Department of Veterans Affairs." DVMF: "a 100% service-connected disabled American veteran."
- TDIU: explicitly included ("deemed unemployable"). Permanence not stated. Residents only.
- Hunting license includes "bow stamp, muzzleloader stamp, sika deer stamp and furbearer permit"; excludes the Maryland Migratory Game Bird Stamp; Federal Migratory Bird Hunting and Conservation Stamp and Big Game Harvest Record required yearly. Fishing license includes "non-tidal angler's license, trout stamp, Chesapeake Bay sport fishing license and recreational oyster license."
- Apply: "letter of disability or a letter of certification from the Veterans Administration" with the Hunting License Application (H699) or Fishing License Application (DNRF3), scanned/faxed or in person at a DNR Licensing & Registration Service Center ("available only" there). DNR 410-260-3220.
- Also on the page: Purple Heart recipients get a 50% discount on hunting and fishing licenses; Universal Disability Pass (free lifetime; allows hunting from a vehicle); blind residents get complimentary annual licenses. A 2016/2020 law allowing reciprocal issuance to nonresident disabled veterans is described only in legislative fiscal notes; not confirmed on the DNR page.
- Natural Resources statute section was not located (the two sections I guessed, 4-209 and 10-301.1, are unrelated). DNR page is the official source.

### 3b. State parks
- Source: https://dnr.maryland.gov/publiclands/Pages/Veterans-Admission-Waiver.aspx (no date); DVMF Recreation page.
- "All applicable day-use entrance charges are waived for all veterans of the United States Armed Forces." "This waiver applies to both resident and out-of-state veterans." Veteran only, "unless a per-vehicle charge is in effect," in which case the per-vehicle fee is waived. "No other facility use service charges will be waived." No proof document specified; the VETERAN license designation is suggested. Active duty and Maryland National Guard: show military ID at the gate.
- Maryland State Park and Trail Passport: $75/year ($100 nonresident), up to 10 people per vehicle, unlimited boat launching; no veteran discount shown. Golden Age Pass: $10 one-time, age 62+. No free lifetime pass for disabled veterans exists; the "lifetime pass" lead was not supported (the free lifetime pass is the Universal Disability Pass, not veteran-specific).

## 4. Education and tuition

### 4a. Edward T. Conroy Memorial Scholarship (MHEC)
- Source: https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/Pages/FinancialAid/ProgramDescriptions/prog_conroy.aspx (no date; cites 2025-2026 maximum and 2026-2027 process).
- Threshold language: veteran applicants: "a veteran with a service-connected disability of 25% or greater who has exhausted or is no longer eligible for federal veterans' educational benefits." Dependents: "child or stepchild of a service member who died from military service or had a service-connected 100% permanent disability," and an unremarried surviving spouse "if the service member had a service-connected 100% permanent disability." Also 9/11 victims' families, Vietnam POW/MIA (Maryland resident when declared), public safety and school employees killed or 100% disabled in the line of duty.
- TDIU: not mentioned; "100% permanent" is the dependents' standard.
- Amount: "maximum award for 2025-2026 is $13,689," capped at in-state undergraduate tuition and mandatory fees at the highest-cost USM four-year school (excluding UMUC/UMB). Depends on funds.
- Deadline/apply: "Apply to your institution by July 15 each year"; do not send to MHEC. Renews automatically; 5 years full time / 8 part time. Residency: Maryland resident at application (exceptions for some public-safety families). Age 16+. For 2026-2027 MHEC allocates funds to institutions, which may adjust awards.

### 4b. Douglas J.J. Peters Veterans of the Afghanistan and Iraq Conflicts Scholarship (MHEC)
- Source: https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/Pages/FinancialAid/ProgramDescriptions/prog_vaic.aspx (describes 2025-2026 cycle).
- Service in Afghanistan on/after October 24, 2001 or Iraq on/after March 19, 2003; veterans, active duty, activated Reserve/Maryland Guard, and their children, stepchildren, and spouses. Maryland resident (active duty stationed in Maryland counts). Undergraduate or transferable 2-year certificate; 6+ credits. Awards up to $8,699 (on campus), $5,637 (off campus), $5,067 (with parents). Apply via MHEC One-App on MDCAPS; 2025-2026 window April 15, 2025 to April 1, 2026; renewals first, then veterans, then dependents. 4 years full time / 8 part time. No disability requirement.

### 4c. In-state tuition (Education Article 15-106.4)
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=ged&section=15-106.4&enactments=false
- "an individual described in subsection (a) ... who attends a public institution of higher education in the State is exempt from paying nonresident tuition ... if: ... (3) The honorably discharged veteran presents documentation that the individual: (i) Was honorably discharged from the uniformed services; and (ii) 1. Resides in this State; or 2. Is domiciled in this State". ## 5. State income tax

### 5a. Military retirement income subtraction (Tax-General 10-207(q))
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gtg&section=10-207&enactments=false ; DVMF Retirement Pay and Pension Tax Deductions and Exclusion page (Updated January 16, 2026). The Comptroller's own military page could not be fetched (404 after redirect).
- Statute: "'Military retirement income' means retirement income, including death benefits, received as a result of military service." "(2) The subtraction ... includes: (i) if, on the last day of the taxable year, the individual is under the age of 55 years, the first $12,500 of military retirement income received by an individual during the taxable year; and (ii) if, on the last day of the taxable year, the individual is at least 55 years old, the first $20,000 of military retirement income". DVMF: claim on Form 502 per Instruction 13.
- SBP: "including death benefits" covers Survivor Benefit Plan annuities, but no Comptroller page confirms the application to SBP specifically.
- VA disability compensation: no Maryland page addresses it. Maryland AGI starts from federal AGI (10-207(a): amounts "are subtracted from the federal adjusted gross income"), and VA compensation is excluded from federal gross income, so it is not taxed. Stated as inference in the notes.
- Active duty: 10-207(p) subtracts the first $15,000 of military pay for active service outside the U.S. (not a veteran benefit; not a row).
- 2025-2026 changes: HB 857 (2026), to raise the under-55 subtraction to $20,000 for tax years after December 31, 2025, had a hearing 2/19/2026 and was not enacted. No 2025 change found (the $12,500/$20,000 figures date from 2023 legislation per fiscal notes).

## 6. Employment preference

### 6a. Selection test credit (State Personnel and Pensions 7-207)
- Source: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gsp&section=7-207&enactments=false ; DBM https://dbm.maryland.gov/jobseekers/Pages/DetermineEligibilityVeteransCredit.aspx
- Statute (c)(2): "(i) An appointing authority shall apply a credit of 10 points on any selection test for: 1. an eligible spouse [spouse of an active service member]; 2. an eligible veteran; 3. the spouse of an eligible veteran who has a service connected disability; or 4. the surviving spouse of a deceased eligible veteran. (ii) An appointing authority shall apply a credit of two additional points on any selection test for a former prisoner of war." "'Eligible veteran' means a veteran who has received an honorable discharge or a certificate of satisfactory completion of service, including a reserve component." Ineligible: "a current State employee; and ... an eligible veteran who is convicted of a crime after being discharged." (g): 5 points for "an individual with a disability, as defined by the federal Americans with Disabilities Act." (f): 5 points for Maryland residents. (a): credit applies only to applicants with "at least the minimum passing score."
- Note: older versions (2005) gave 2 extra points to a veteran with a service-connected disability; the current statute gives the extra 2 points only to former POWs. The "10 points; spouses" lead is confirmed; a "12 points for disabled veterans" claim is outdated. A claimed noncompetitive hiring route for 30%+ disabled veterans was not found on any official page and is left out.
- DBM VeteransPreference page returned 404; the DBM eligibility page confirms the 10/2/5-point structure and the 7-207 cite.

## 7. Other (homes, cemeteries, grants, bonuses)

### 7a. Charlotte Hall Veterans Home
- Source: https://veterans.maryland.gov/benefits-services/healthcare/charlotte-hall-veterans-home (per diem rates "as of October 1, 2025").
- Eligibility: Maryland resident veteran, "full-time active duty ... other than active duty for training," discharge "under conditions other than dishonorable," "at least 62 years of age or deemed disabled by either Social Security Administration or the US Department of Veterans Affairs"; certain reserve members meeting residence rules; certain non-veteran spouses (pay full assisted living cost). ### 7c. Maryland Veterans Trust Fund
- Source: https://veterans.maryland.gov/benefits-services/maryland-veterans-trust-fund (no date; © 2026).
- One-time grants paid to vendors for "delinquent rent or mortgage payments," "delinquent utilities," "delinquent car and insurance payments," and "reimbursement for emergency car or home repairs." Maryland resident, honorably discharged veteran, Maryland Guard member, or surviving spouse; bills 30 to 60 days past due. Online application; monthly cycle (apply 1st-15th, review 16th-23rd, notify 24th-end). No maximum amount published.

### 7d. Veterans Service Animal Program
- Source: https://veterans.maryland.gov/benefits-services/service-animal-program (Updated January 14, 2026). Grants to Maryland nonprofit service/support dog and equine therapy organizations (FY27 NOFA); veterans, spouses, and dependents (added by 2025 amendment) are served through grantees. "DVMF does not train service animals for placement with veterans." No individual application. Established 2017, amended 2019 and 2025 (no code cite on the page). Not a row.

### 7e. Not found
