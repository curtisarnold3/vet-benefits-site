## 1. Property tax

Kentucky has NO veteran-specific property tax exemption. The only property tax relief is the general homestead exemption in Section 170 of the Kentucky Constitution, implemented by KRS 132.810, for owners who are 65 or older or "totally disabled." Several bills to create a disabled veteran exemption were filed in 2025 and 2026; none passed (Section 8).

### 1a. Homestead / disability exemption (Ky. Const. Sec. 170; KRS 132.810)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=39641 (statute PDF, "Effective: June 8, 2011"; amendments apply to property assessed on or after January 1, 2012)
- Amount: https://revenue.ky.gov/News/Pages/DOR-Sets-2025-2026-Homestead-Exemption.aspx (Nov. 14, 2024): "The Kentucky Department of Revenue (DOR) has set the maximum homestead exemption at $49,100 for the 2025 and 2026 tax periods. By statute, the amount of the homestead exemption is recalculated every two years to adjust for inflation. The 2025-2026 exemption reflects a $2,750 increase over the 2023–2024 exemption of $46,350." Statutory basis, KRS 132.810(2)(e): "The sixty-five hundred dollars ($6,500) exemption provided in Section 170 of the Constitution of Kentucky shall be construed to mean sixty-five hundred dollars ($6,500) in terms of the purchasing power of the dollar in 1972. Every two (2) years thereafter, if the cost of living index of the United States Department of Labor has changed as much as one percent (1%), the maximum exemption shall be adjusted accordingly." This is a reduction in assessed value, not a full exemption. KRS 132.810(3): applies "for state, county, city, or special district purposes."
- Threshold language, KRS 132.810(2)(a): "Every person filing an application for exemption under the homestead exemption provision must be sixty-five (65) years of age or older during the year for which application is made or must have been classified as totally disabled under a program authorized or administered by an agency of the United States government or by any retirement system either within or without the Commonwealth of Kentucky on January 1 of the year in which application is made."
- (2)(c): "Every person filing an application for exemption under the disability provision of the homestead exemption must have received disability payments pursuant to the disability and must maintain the disability classification for the entirety of the particular taxation period."
- Annual reapplication, (2)(d): "Every person filing for the homestead exemption who is totally disabled and is less than sixty-five (65) years of age must apply for the homestead exemption on an annual basis, except as provided by subparagraph 2. ... a. A service-connected totally disabled veteran of the United States Armed Forces; or b. A totally and permanently disabled individual found disabled under: i. The applicable rules of the Social Security Administration; ii. The applicable rules of the Kentucky Retirement Systems; or iii. Any other provision of the Kentucky Revised Statutes; shall document the disability at the time of application for the homestead exemption and shall not be required to apply for the homestead exemption on an annual basis." (Veteran carve-out added by 2008 HB 284, Acts ch. 4.)
- TDIU / 100% schedular / 100% P&T: The statute never uses "100 percent," "permanent and total," or "individual unemployability." It keys on being "classified as totally disabled" under a federal program and receiving "disability payments pursuant to the disability." A 100% schedular VA rating is a total disability rating with compensation payments, so it fits the words. TDIU is a VA total-disability rating for compensation purposes paid at the 100% rate, so it appears to fit the same words, but no Kentucky statute, DOR form, or KDVA page names TDIU, and the Jefferson County PVA page describes the constitutional standard as owners who are "100% disabled." P&T is NOT required by the text; however, the veteran "must maintain the disability classification for the entirety of the particular taxation period," and Form 62A350 says "At any time a Property Valuation Administrator may conduct a review of applications and may require an applicant to re-apply or submit proof of continuing disability and benefits received." Flagged needs_verification for the TDIU question.
- Deadline, office, form: Form 62A350 "Application for Exemption Under the Homestead/Disability Amendment" (DOR, https://revenue.ky.gov/Forms/62A350.pdf, rev. 3-16; a 10-22 fillable version also exists): "This application-affidavit must be submitted by December 31st of the year in which exemption is sought to the property valuation administrator of the county in which the residential unit is located. In addition, the applicant must own, occupy and maintain the subject property as a taxable interest as of January 1 during the tax year." Disability section: "A person must be classified as totally disabled under a program authorized or administered by an agency of the United States government or by any retirement system either within or without the Commonwealth. In addition ... A. The applicant must have maintained the disability classification for the entire year. B. The applicant must have received disability payments under this classification. C. Verification documentation must be submitted to the property valuation administrator by December 31 in the first year of eligibility." DOR press release: application "can be submitted online, by mail or in person."
- Refund/exoneration, KRS 132.810(4)(c)-(d): qualifying before the bill is paid gets "a full or partial exoneration"; qualifying after payment gets "a refund of the property taxes applicable to the value of the homestead exemption."
- Surviving spouse rules: none. There is no carryover; a spouse qualifies only on his or her own age or disability. One exemption per residence: (2)(e)1 "Only one (1) exemption per residential unit shall be allowed even though the resident may be sixty-five (65) years of age and also totally disabled."
- Residency / ownership / income caps: must "own and maintain the property for which the exemption is sought as his personal residence" ((2)(b)); ownership may be "legal or equitable title, by the entireties, jointly, in common, as a condominium," or via stock/membership in a cooperative ((2)(f)); mobile homes, RVs classified as real property, and manufactured homes qualify ((2)(g)). No income cap. No minimum length of Kentucky residency; the test is ownership and occupancy on January 1.
- Reporting duty, (4)(b): "Any totally disabled person granted the homestead exemption under the disability provision shall report any change in disability classification to the property valuation administrator." Form 62A350 warns failure "could result in supplemental bills being issued for the amount of the exemption received for up to a period of five years."
- County variation: none in amount, deadline or eligibility; the PVA administers it locally and chooses the filing method. Jefferson County PVA (https://jeffersonpva.ky.gov/property-assessment/exemptions/disability-exemption/) confirms "The Disability Exemption for 2025-2026 is $49,100" and says "Disability Exemption recipients may be required to apply annually."
- 2025-2026 changes: none enacted. 2025 HB 52 (annual instead of biennial COLA) died in House A&R (last action 02/04/25). 2025 HB 639 (new homestead exemption for veterans with a service-connected disability) died in House A&R (02/21/25). 2026 HB 285 ("a homestead exemption in an amount up to $100,000 for veterans with a 50% or greater service-connected disability") died in House A&R (01/16/26). 2026 constitutional amendments HB 242 (introduced 01/08/26), SB 169 (02/05/26) and HB 587 (02/06/26; "a property tax exemption for the permanent residence of an owner who is a veteran" with a service-connected disability) all died in committee. Because Section 170 caps the exemption, a voter-approved constitutional amendment is required before any larger veteran exemption can exist.

### 1b. Lower-rating tiers, SAH exemption, surviving spouse exemption
- None exist in Kentucky. Veterans rated below "totally disabled" get nothing unless they are 65 or older. There is no specially adapted housing exemption and no surviving spouse exemption.

## 2. Vehicles, driver license, parking

### 2a. Disabled Veteran license plate (KRS 186.162(2)(a) and (2)(d); KRS 186.041; Form TC 96-217)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57951 (version "Effective until January 1, 2027"; history: "Amended 2026 Ky. Acts ch. 161, sec. 99, effective July 15, 2026. -- Amended 2025 Ky. Acts ch. 31, sec. 1, effective June 27, 2025").
- Threshold language, (2)(a): "Disabled veterans who receive assistance to purchase a vehicle from the United States Department of Veterans' Affairs, veterans declared by the United States Department of Veterans' Affairs to be one hundred percent (100%) service-connected disabled, and recipients of the Congressional Medal of Honor: 1. Initial Fee: $0 ($0 SF/$0 CF/$0 EF). 2. Renewal Fee: $0 ($0 SF/$0 CF/$0 EF)."
- (2)(d) (shared category with other military plates): "... and disabled veterans who have been declared to be between fifty percent (50%) and ninety-nine percent (99%) service-connected disabled by the United States Department of Veterans' Affairs: 1. Initial Fee: $23 ($12 SF/$6 CF/$5 EF to the veterans' program trust fund established under KRS 40.460). 2. Renewal Fee: $23."
- TDIU / P&T: the statute says "one hundred percent (100%) service-connected disabled"; it does not say permanent, and does not mention TDIU. The form requires the VA "authorization" letter. Unclear whether a TDIU letter is accepted; not addressed by any official source found.
- Number of plates, KRS 186.041(2) (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57953, "Effective: July 15, 2026"): the eligible person "may purchase an unlimited number of special military-related license plates ... annually for vehicles they own or lease. A disabled veterans license plate shall expire on July 31."
- Form TC 96-217 (Transportation Cabinet, https://transportation.ky.gov/Organizational-Resources/Forms/TC%2096-217.docx, rev. 10/2025 per search listing): "In addition to completing the usual documentation for registering a motor vehicle and before the plate is issued, this form must be completed and a copy of the authorization from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs must be supplied." Applicant certifies "that I am a resident of Kentucky and a disabled veteran who: [ ] Received assistance to purchase a vehicle from the US Department of Veterans' Affairs [ ] Has been declared by the US Department of Veterans' Affairs to be 100% service-connected disabled [ ] Is a recipient of the Congressional Medal of Honor." Notarized. Clerk instruction: "Collect any applicable usage tax or ad valorum taxes. Do not collect a registration fee."
- Residency: Kentucky resident (form certification); applied for at the county clerk of residence.
- Proof (186.041(10)): VA Veteran ID Card or VHIC, DD-2/DD-214/DD-256/DD-257/NGB-22, or Geneva Conventions ID.
- Local fee exemption and parking, KRS 189.4595 (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=6400): "a city, county, town, or other political subdivision of this state shall not require any license or fee from a disabled veteran ... who has been issued a special military-related license plate under KRS 186.041, for the privilege of operating a motor vehicle upon any street or alley," and the vehicle "may be parked for a period of two (2) hours in excess of the legal parking period permitted by local authorities" with fire-lane, rush-hour and traffic-hazard exceptions.
- Sales/usage tax: no exemption. The form says usage tax is still collected. The KDVA summary PDF lists no vehicle tax exemption.
- 2025-2026 changes: 2025 HB 157 (Acts ch. 31) House Committee Substitute would "establish that there are no fees for veteran and Kentucky National Guard special license plates"; the Senate Committee Substitute "revert[ed] back to the bill as introduced" and the enacted act only created a Friends of Kentucky Agriculture plate. 2023 HB 55 (free plate at 50%+) died. 2026 Acts ch. 161 sec. 99 restructures 186.162 effective January 1, 2027: the 100% category becomes "(no CF charged): Initial Fee: $0 ($0 SF/$0 EF). Renewal Fee: $0," and Purple Heart/POW/Pearl Harbor becomes "$17 ($12 SF/$5 EF)" initial and "$5" renewal, with the clerk fee handled separately. I did not capture the post-2027 fee for the 50% to 99% group.

### 2b. Purple Heart plate (KRS 186.162(2)(b); 186.041(7))
- "Former prisoners of war, recipients of the Purple Heart, and survivors of Pearl Harbor: 1. Initial Fee: $23 ($12 SF/$6 CF/$5 EF to the veterans' program trust fund ...). 2. Renewal Fee: $11 ($0 SF/$6 CF/$5 EF ...)."
- 186.041(7): "The surviving spouse of a Purple Heart recipient ... who possessed a vehicle licensed with the Purple Heart recipient special license plate ... may retain the license plate for use on the same vehicle or another vehicle ... The surviving spouse may renew the license plate indefinitely, provided the appropriate registration fee is paid annually."

### 2c. Driver license: "veteran" designation; no fee waiver (KRS 186.416(4); KRS 186.531)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57818 ("Effective: July 15, 2026"; "Amended 2026 Ky. Acts ch. 135, sec. 22"). A veteran may "request that an operator's license or a personal identification card issued under this chapter bear the word 'veteran' on the face or the back of the license or personal identification card," presenting "1. Unexpired Veteran Identification Card or Veteran Health Identification Card issued by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs; 2. DD-2, DD-214, DD-256, DD-257, or NGB-22 form; or 3. Unexpired Geneva Conventions Identification Card."
- KRS 186.531 (fees for licenses and ID cards, https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=55622) contains no veteran or disabled veteran fee waiver (text searched for "veteran," "disab," "exempt"). Kentucky does not offer a free driver license to disabled veterans.

### 2d. Accessible parking placard (KRS 189.456)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=47681 ("Effective: July 14, 2018"). "The county clerk shall issue an applicant one (1) accessible parking placard at no cost." Duplicate $10. "The placard shall be valid for a period of six (6) years." Eligibility is medical (mobility impairment), not veteran status; a VA-rated veteran must still meet the impairment standard.

## 3. Hunting, fishing, parks

### 3a. Resident Disabled Sportsman's License (KRS 150.175(27); 301 KAR 5:022)
- Statute: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=55364: "(27) A disabled sportsman's hunting and fishing license, which authorizes the holder to perform all acts valid under a sport fishing license, a sport hunting license, and a state permit to take deer, turkey, trout, waterfowl, and migratory shore and upland game birds, and which shall be available to a Kentucky resident who is: (a) An American veteran at least fifty percent (50%) disabled as a result of a service-connected disability; or (b) Declared permanently and totally disabled by the federal Social Security Administration, the United States Office of Personnel Management, the Kentucky Teachers' Retirement System, the Department of Workers' Claims or its equivalent from another state, or the United States Railroad Retirement Board. The disabled sportsman's hunting and fishing license shall not be valid unless the holder carries proof of residency."
- Agency page: https://fw.ky.gov/Licenses/Pages/Resident-Senior-and-Disabled-Licenses.aspx (no date shown): "Kentucky resident Veterans at least 50% disabled as the result of a service-connected disability." "Senior, Senior Lifetime and Disabled Sportsman's licenses are available only to Kentucky residents." Package: "include the Combination Hunting and Fishing License, all Deer Permits, Spring Turkey Permit," Resident Fall Turkey Permit, Kentucky Migratory Bird/Waterfowl Permit and Trout Permit. Veterans: "Contact your local VA office and request a letter that verifies you are at least 50 percent disabled." Apply for disability authorization online (My Profile), by mail (KDFWR Disabled License, 1 Sportsman's Lane, Frankfort, KY 40601), fax (502) 564-9845, or in person. "Authorization is valid for three years (check expiration date)." "Disability authorization is not a license."
- Fee: 301 KAR 5:022 (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/301/005/022/, eff. 11-6-2025): resident disabled sportsman's license "twelve (12) dollars," valid March 1 through the last day of February; fees are increased by CPI "every year ending on an even number effective January 1, 2026." fw.ky.gov Fees page (https://fw.ky.gov/Licenses/Pages/Fees.aspx): "Resident Disabled Sportsman's License $12.68"; Resident Sportsman's License $100.42. KDVA summary PDF says "This license costs $12" and calls it a "three year" license; the three years refers to the authorization, the license itself is annual.
- Nothing free: Kentucky does not give free licenses to disabled veterans. Federal duck stamp and HIP survey still required.

### 3b. Kentucky State Parks free stays for 100% disabled veterans
- Source: https://parks.ky.gov/parks/park-listings/deals (no date shown): "Kentucky Disabled Veterans Lodging Guidelines. Benefit is only for veterans who are 100% disabled as a direct result of a service-connected incident and is a current Kentucky resident. Benefit will extend to Kentucky National Guard or Reserve members disabled under the same circumstance. 100% disability is determined by the US Veterans Administration or Department of Defense. National Guard disability is determined as provided in KRS Chapter 342. Those eligible will have a letter from the US Department of Veterans Affairs. Available for a maximum of three overnight stays/calendar year, three nights max for each visit, subject to availability. Pet fee charges will be collected ... During the period Memorial Day-Labor Day, and the month of October, stays must start/end during the 5-day Sunday-Thursday period. ... The free accommodation (hotel/lodge room, cottage, or campsite) will apply only to that occupied by the veteran themselves. Reservations can be made no more than ten (10) days prior to arrival. Reservations are made by calling the park of their choice. Disability letter and proof of Kentucky residency are required upon arrival."
- Other park discounts on the same page: "15% lodging discount" for "Active & retired military personnel with proof of service" (April-October; code USA Nov.-March), 10% on campsites for military, Kentucky POWs camp free, seniors 62+ 10%. No percentage-based or below-100% disabled veteran discount. No state park entrance fee exists in Kentucky, so there is no entrance pass benefit.
- TDIU: the page says "100% disabled ... determined by the US Veterans Administration"; TDIU not addressed.

## 4. Education and tuition

### 4a. Tuition waiver for spouse/children of 100% or P&T veterans, POW/MIA (KRS 164.515)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45836 ("Effective: June 29, 2017")
- Threshold language (1): "The spouse, regardless of age, and any child, stepchild, or orphan, under the age of twenty-six (26), of a permanently and totally disabled member of the Kentucky National Guard or Reserve Component injured while on state active duty, active duty for training, or inactive duty training, or a permanently and totally disabled war veteran, or a one hundred percent (100%) service-connected disabled veteran regardless of wartime service, or prisoner of war or member of the Armed Services declared missing in action shall not be required to pay any matriculation or tuition fees upon his admission to any state-supported institution of higher education or to any state-supported vocational training school for a period not in excess of forty-five (45) months in order to obtain a diploma."
- (2): "the parent or stepparent of the child claiming benefits if living must be rated permanently and totally disabled for pension purposes or one hundred percent (100%) disabled for compensation purposes by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs or the Department of Defense. If the veteran is deceased, the claim to benefits is to be based on the rating held by the veteran at the time of death."
- TDIU / 100% / P&T: the law accepts either "one hundred percent (100%) disabled for compensation purposes" (no permanence needed) or "permanently and totally disabled for pension purposes" (non-service-connected VA pension). TDIU is not named; KDVA's page lists "100% service-connected disabled" and "Totally disabled (non-service-connected) with wartime service." Whether KDVA treats TDIU as 100% for compensation purposes is not stated anywhere official.
- Residency (4): the veteran "must be a resident or, if deceased, have been a resident of the Commonwealth of Kentucky"; discharge "under honorable conditions." KDVA page: "The applicant doesn't need Kentucky residency, but must attend in-person or online classes at a qualifying Kentucky college."
- Coverage (KDVA, https://veterans.ky.gov/Benefits/Pages/Education-for-Veterans-in-Kentucky.aspx, (c) 2022 footer): "The waiver exempts the student from paying tuition ONLY." Not fees, books, room and board. "Private or out-of-state schools do not qualify for the Tuition Waiver." Expires at degree, 45 months, or child's 26th birthday; (5) extends past 26 for the student's own military service. "Allow at least 30 days for processing." Contact: KDVA.TuitionWaiver@ky.gov, 502-503-7911. Documents (KDVA Tuition Waiver Program Summary PDF, modified 2022): birth certificate, marriage certificate, disability award letter, death certificate if deceased, DD-214. "Award from Social Security Administration is not acceptable."
- 2025-2026 changes: 2026 HB 497 (would convert 164.515 and 164.507 to last-dollar scholarships, 128 undergraduate credit hour cap, age-26 rule with service extensions) passed the House 56-36 on 02/25/26 and died in Senate Committee on Committees (last action 02/26/26). 2026 HB 247 (tuition waiver for spouses and children of disabled veterans, 128 hours) was WITHDRAWN 02/27/26. No change enacted; the statute history still ends at 2017.

### 4b. Deceased veterans' dependents (KRS 164.505; KRS 164.507)
- 164.505 (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45833): no fees "if the person's deceased parent or stepparent, or ... deceased spouse if the person has not remarried, was a resident of the Commonwealth of Kentucky upon joining the Kentucky National Guard or upon entering military service and: (a) Was killed while serving ... during a national emergency, or wars declared by Congress, or actions of the United Nations, or was killed by hostile fire ...; or (b) Died as a result of a service-connected disability acquired while serving ... during a national emergency, or wars declared by Congress, or actions of the United Nations." KDVA: no age or time limit; undergraduate and graduate degrees.
- 164.507 (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45834): "The nonremarried spouse, regardless of age, and any child, stepchild, or orphan, under the age of twenty-six (26), of a deceased veteran" who "1. Served in the Armed Forces ... during a national emergency, wars declared by Congress, or actions of the United Nations; or 2. Died while on active duty ... regardless of wartime service; or 3. Died as a result of a service-connected disability ... regardless of wartime service; and (b) 1. Was a resident of the Commonwealth of Kentucky at the time of death; or 2. Was married to a resident of Kentucky at the time of death; and 3. If discharged, was under honorable conditions." 45 months.
- 164.512: tuition waiver for a veteran's child "who has acquired a disability as a direct result of the veteran's service" (VA-compensable, e.g., spina bifida); veteran must be/have been a Kentucky resident.

### 4c. Veteran's own tuition, in-state tuition
- No Kentucky state tuition waiver exists for the veteran himself or herself. In-state tuition for veterans using GI Bill benefits was not researched (search budget); not included.

## 5. State income tax
- Rate: KRS 141.020(2) (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=56339, "Effective: June 27, 2025", amended 2025 Acts ch. 1 = HB 1): "(e) For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2024, but before January 1, 2026, the tax shall be four percent (4%) of net income. (f) For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, the tax shall be three and one-half percent (3.5%) of net income." Further cuts from 2027 depend on revenue triggers in (2)(g).
- Pension exclusion / military retired pay: KRS 141.019(1)(g) (https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57914, "Effective: July 15, 2026"): "For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2018, exclude up to thirty-one thousand one hundred ten dollars ($31,110) of total distributions from pension plans, annuity contracts, profit-sharing plans, retirement plans, or employee savings plans"; "Distributions" includes "any disability pension distribution." (1)(l): "Exclude all military pay received by members of the Armed Forces while on active duty." (1)(k): all income excluded in the year of death and the prior year for members killed in the line of duty.
- DOR Military Tax Issues page (https://revenue.ky.gov/Individual/Pages/Military-Tax-Issues.aspx, undated, references 2018 forms): "Kentucky allows a pension income exclusion of up to $31,110 for all pension income that is reported as taxable"; for more, "complete Kentucky Schedule P Kentucky Pension Income Exclusion."
- Pre-1998 rule: DOR Schedule P 2023 (https://revenue.ky.gov/Forms/Schedule%20P%202023.pdf; 2024 and 2025 URLs returned 404): "Use line 1(a) if retired before January 1, 1998, to report fully exempt pension benefits. Use line 1(b) if retired after December 31, 1997, to compute the amount of pension income attributable to service credits earned before January 1, 1998. Multiply the taxable pension by the exempt percentage." Covers "federal, Kentucky state and Kentucky local government pension income" (military retirement is federal) and "federal or Kentucky disability retirement income attributable to service credit earned before January 1, 1998." KDVA summary PDF: "The military retired pay of Service members who retired before January 1, 1998, is completely exempt from Kentucky income taxes. Service members who retired ... after December 31, 1997, may exclude up to $31,110."
- VA disability compensation, DIC, SBP: no Kentucky page found that names them. Kentucky AGI starts from federal AGI (KRS 141.019), so VA compensation and DIC (excluded federally) are not taxed. SBP annuities are pension income and fall under the $31,110 exclusion. Stated in the row with that reasoning; no official Kentucky quote.
- National Guard $20 credit (DOR individual income tax page).

## 6. Employment preference (KRS 18A.150)
- Source: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=48896 ("Effective: June 27, 2019"; amended 2019 Acts ch. 109 = HB 338).
- Language: "(1) Interview preference to a competitive classified position for which he or she meets the minimum qualifications established for the job classification shall apply to: (a) Any person who has served in the Armed Forces of the United States, Reserves, or National Guard who was discharged or released therefrom with an honorable discharge, discharge under honorable conditions, or a general discharge, or his or her spouse or unmarried widow or widower; (b) The unmarried widow or widower of any military personnel who died while in the Armed Forces ...; and (c) Any current member of the Armed Forces of the United States, Reserves, or National Guard or his or her spouse. (2) A parent totally or partially dependent on a person who ... lost his or her life under honorable conditions while on active duty ... or became permanently and totally disabled as a result of a service-connected disability shall have interview preference ... (3)(b) 1. If the number of individuals identified ... is less than five (5), the employing agency shall offer an interview to all ... 2. If the number ... equals or exceeds five (5), the employing agency shall offer an interview to no fewer than five (5). (4) Interview preference shall only apply to candidates seeking initial appointment to the classified service."
- Personnel Memo 19-07 (https://personnel.ky.gov/Personnel%20Memos/2019-07.pdf, June 27, 2019): HB 338 "Replaces preference points with interview preference"; "Extends interview preference to spouses of current or former members ... rather than the previous limited preference to spouses of disabled veterans"; "Applies only to competitive classified (merit) position vacancies for initial appointments (not internal mobility positions)."
- The old 5-point (veteran) and 10-point (service-connected disabled veteran) system is gone. No disability-based tier remains. Local government preference: not researched. KRS 61.373 (reemployment after military service) was noted in the KDVA summary but not researched.

### 7b. State veterans cemeteries (KRS 40.315)
- Statute: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=56650 ("Effective: January 1, 2026"; "Amended 2025 Ky. Acts ch. 20, sec. 1"): eligibility "(a) Persons who are eligible for interment in a veterans' national cemetery pursuant to 38 U.S.C. sec. 2402 ...; and (b) Any member of a reserve component of the Armed Forces, the Army National Guard, or the Air National Guard not otherwise qualified ... if: 1. The member completed the initial period of service as required by 10 U.S.C. sec. 651(a) and was discharged under conditions other than dishonorable; or 2. The member ... died while on state active duty or while serving under Title 32." Capital-offense bar in (6).
- KDVA Cemeteries page (https://veterans.ky.gov/Cemeteries/Pages/default.aspx, (c) 2022): five cemeteries (West, Central, North, North East, South East); "Kentucky residency is not required"; services free to the veteran; "five hundred dollar spousal fee" payable at death, though the same page also says spouse and dependent benefits are "at no cost to the family" (conflict, flagged). Eligibility application PDF (file name dated Jan 26).

### 7c. Veterans' Program Trust Fund (KRS 40.460; 201 KAR 37:010)
- https://veterans.ky.gov/veterans-trust-fund/Pages/default.aspx: fund "direct[s] funding to projects and programs that help Kentucky's veterans"; grants go to organizations (first eight applications per quarterly meeting; 2026 deadlines Feb 10, May 10, Aug 9, Nov). No individual emergency grant program found. Not a row.

### 7d. Not available
- No state veterans bonus is currently payable (KRS Chapter 40 bonus sections are historic). No state emergency grant to individual veterans found. No housing or home-modification grant (2026 HB 214 "Residential Ease of Access for Disabled Veterans Program" passed the House 95-0 on 01/29/26 and died in Senate Committee on Committees; companion SB 139 died in committee). Indigent Veterans' Burial Program exists (KRS 40.355) but was not researched.
