# Kansas (KS) — State Benefits for Disabled Veterans

Research date: 2026-10-17. Official agency: Kansas Office of Veterans Services (KOVS), https://www.kovs.ks.gov/ (renamed from the Kansas Commission on Veterans Affairs Office by 2024 HB 2760; the old kcva.ks.gov domain still redirects).

Sourcing note: Statutes are quoted from the Kansas Office of Revisor of Statutes (ksrevisor.gov, 2026 statute pages with history lines). Tax items come from the Kansas Department of Revenue (ksrevenue.gov: 2025 homestead instructions, Form K-40SVR, 2025 income tax booklet, Disabled Veteran Tax Exemption page, Notice 26-01). Vehicle plates come from the KDOR Division of Vehicles. Hunting/fishing and parks come from the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks (ksoutdoors.gov, now redirects from ksoutdoors.com). Education comes from the Kansas Board of Regents (kansasregents.gov) and statute. Employment comes from the Kansas Department of Administration (admin.ks.gov) and statute. Homes and cemeteries come from KOVS. The Kansas Legislative Research Department summary "Kansas Benefits for Veterans and Military Personnel" (klrd.gov, dated August 25, 2026) was used as a cross-check and is official (legislative branch). ## 1. Property tax

Kansas has NO property tax exemption for disabled veterans. Property tax relief is delivered as two state refund programs run by the Department of Revenue (not by county appraisers). Both are statewide and do not vary by county. There is also no specially adapted housing exemption. Veterans organizations' property is exempt (K.S.A. 79-201) but that does not help individuals.

### 1a. Senior or Disabled Veteran property tax refund, Form K-40SVR (K.S.A. 79-4508a)
- Source: https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch79/079_045_0008a.html (history "L. 2022, ch. 63, § 17; L. 2025, ch. 123, § 11; July 1"); Form K-40SVR 2025, https://ksrevenue.gov/pdf/k-40svr25.pdf; KDOR 2025 homestead instructions, https://www.ksrevenue.gov/homesteadbook25.html.
- Threshold language (K-40SVR instructions, Line 2, matching K.S.A. 79-4502): "A disabled veteran means a person who is a resident of Kansas and who: (1) served in the active military, naval, air or space service including those groups and individuals under 38 C.F.R. § 3.7; and who was discharged or released therefrom under an honorable discharge or a general discharge under honorable conditions; (2) received a disability that was incurred or aggravated in the line of duty in the active military, naval, air or space service ...; and (3) has a service-connected evaluation percentage equal to or greater than 50%, pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 1101 et seq. or 10 U.S.C. § 1201 et seq."
- Statute eligibility (79-4508a(b)(2)): claimant domiciled in Kansas the entire prior calendar year and either "65 years of age or older" or a "disabled veteran." (c)(1): "The claimant's household income for the year in which the claim is filed is $50,000 or less" (indexed to the federal cost-of-living adjustment; the 2025 form uses $58,041). (c)(2): "the appraised value of the claimant's homestead for the base year is $350,000 or less."
- TDIU / 100% schedular / 100% P&T: the law keys on a "service-connected evaluation percentage equal to or greater than 50%." All three qualify. Permanence is not in the statute, but the K-40H instructions say "The Kansas Department of Revenue must have on file documentation of permanent disability," and KOVS describes the test as "50% or greater service-connected permanent disability rating." A veteran with a TDIU grant whose combined schedular evaluation is below 50% would be a gray area; no official source addresses that case.
- Amount: refund "shall be computed by deducting the claimant's base year ad valorem tax amount for the homestead" from the current year's tax (79-4508a(a)); the form (line 9) is 2025 general property tax paid minus base-year tax, excluding specials. Base year = first year the claimant was 65+ and/or a disabled veteran all year, owned and occupied the homestead all year, and was a Kansas resident all year; anyone eligible before 2021 uses 2021. No dollar cap appears on the form or in the statute. NOTE: the KOVS State Veterans Benefits page says the SVR refund is "not to exceed $700"; that cap is the K-40H cap and does not appear in 79-4508a or on Form K-40SVR. Flagged as a conflict between official pages.
- Deadline/office: "FILE THIS CLAIM AFTER DECEMBER 31, 2025, BUT NO LATER THAN APRIL 15, 2026" with the Kansas Department of Revenue. "Please allow 20 to 24 weeks to process your refund." Late claims for good cause within four years (homestead book). A Refund Advancement Program lets the refund pay the December tax installment.
- Surviving spouse: "For the surviving spouse to qualify, the disabled veteran or person 65 years of age or older must have been receiving benefits under the SVR program (K.S.A. 79-4508a) at the time of their death"; statute: continues "until remarriage." Documentation: "a copy of the original veterans Disability Determination letter or letter from your regional V.A. that includes the disability date and the percentage of disability being 50% or greater."
- Residency/ownership/income: Kansas resident all of 2025; "owned and occupied the same homestead in both 2025 and the base year"; income $58,041 or less for 2025 (KOVS: household income "does not include VA disability, Railroad Retirement, or disability payments from social security or SSI"). "If you filed a Form K-40H or K-40PT for 2025, you cannot claim this refund."
- County variation: none (state program).
- 2025-2026 changes: L. 2025 ch. 123 § 11 amended 79-4508a (household income defined as Kansas AGI of all household members for tax year 2025 and later). No veteran-specific change.

### 1b. Homestead Refund, Form K-40H (K.S.A. 79-4501 et seq.; definitions in 79-4502)
- Source: https://www.ksrevenue.gov/homesteadbook25.html (2025 instructions); K.S.A. 79-4502, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch79/079_045_0002.html (history ends "L. 2025, ch. 97, § 14; July 1").
- Threshold language: 79-4502(i)(3): "has a service-connected evaluation percentage equal to or greater than 50%." K-40H line 2: claimant was "blind or totally and permanently disabled or a disabled veteran all of 2025."
- Amount: partial rebate, "$700 maximum," multiplied by a percentage from 100% down to 0% as household income rises. Household income $43,389 or less. "taxes on property valued at $350,000 or more does not qualify."
- Surviving spouse: mark the box if you are "the surviving spouse (and have not remarried) of a disabled veteran or of a member of the armed forces who died in the line of duty during active service"; statute 79-4502(e): "The surviving spouse of a disabled veteran who was receiving benefits pursuant to subsection (e)(1)(C)" continues until remarriage, and (e)(1)(D) covers "the surviving spouse of a deceased member of the armed forces who died in the line of duty."
- Residency/ownership: Kansas resident all of 2025, must own the home ("contract for deed" counts; "rent to own" does not); renters do not qualify.
- Deadline: April 15, 2026; late claims for good cause within four years. Only one of K-40H / K-40PT / K-40SVR per household.
- SAFESR (K-40PT, "Property Tax Relief Claim for Low Income Seniors"): age 65+ only, income $25,380 or less, refund 75% of property tax paid. Not a veteran benefit; disabled veterans under 65 cannot use it.
- County variation: none.

### 1c. No exemption; 2025-2026 bills that died
- HB 2005 (2025-26), https://kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/hb2005/: "Establishing the veterans' valor property tax relief act." As amended by House Taxation (reports March 14, 2025 and March 9, 2026) it would have created a refundable income tax credit equal to 50% of residential property tax for veterans "totally disabled, permanently and totally disabled, or unemployable" and repealed the July 1, 2026 sales tax exemption. Status: "Died." Never reached a floor vote.
- SB 434 (2026), https://kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/sb434/: passed the Senate (floor amendment Feb. 11, 2026); the House Substitute (March 9, 2026) carried the Veterans' Valor act and the sales tax repeal. Status: "Died."
- Earlier: HB 2036 (2023) graduated homestead exemption and HB 2635 (2024) died in committee.
- Result: for tax years 2025 and 2026 there is still no exemption and the sales tax exemption (section 3 below) took effect on schedule.

## 2. Vehicles, driver license, parking

### 2a. Disabled Veteran plate, free registration, parking (K.S.A. 8-160, 8-161)
- Sources: KDOR Division of Vehicles, https://ksrevenue.gov/dov-disabledvet.html (no page date); K.S.A. 8-160, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_001_0060.html (history ends "L. 2024, ch. 84, § 6; L. 2025, ch. 97, § 1; July 1"); K.S.A. 8-161, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_001_0061.html (last amended L. 2022 ch. 57).
- Threshold language (8-160): "'disabled veteran' means a person who: (a) Served in the active military, naval, air or space service, including those groups and individuals listed under 38 C.F.R. § 3.7, and who was discharged or released therefrom under an honorable discharge or a general discharge under honorable conditions; (b) received a disability that was incurred or aggravated in the line of duty ...; and (c) has a service-connected evaluation percentage equal to or greater than 50%, pursuant to 38 U.S.C. § 1101 et seq. or 10 U.S.C. § 1201 et seq."
- 8-161(a): for a passenger vehicle, truck "not more than 20,000 pounds," or motorcycle owned or leased by a Kansas-resident disabled veteran, "such registration shall be made and such license plates issued free of charge to the disabled veteran." 8-161(b): a resident who transports a disabled veteran, or the veteran for a vehicle not owned/leased, may get the plate with "no fee for such license plates in addition to the regular registration fee."
- KDOR: "There is no registration fee for the initial Disabled Veteran plate" at application or renewal; "Property tax is still owed on the vehicle." Additional plates pay standard registration and property tax. Form TR-103 "must be completed and signed by both the disabled veteran and the regional director for the Veterans Administration"; the wheelchair-emblem version (effective 7/1/2022) also needs a medical professional's signature (K.S.A. 8-1,124 standard). Apply at the county treasurer with proof of insurance.
- Parking (8-161, as summarized by KOVS): with the plate the veteran may "(A) Park without charge in any metered zone. Such motor vehicle shall be exempt from any time limitation ...; or (B) park without charge in any parking space in a public parking facility or public parking lot if such parking space is clearly marked as being reserved for the use of persons with a disability ... and such public parking facility or public parking lot employs persons who are parking attendants." Plates with the international symbol may also use accessible spaces generally. The director of vehicles issues an ID card the veteran must carry when parked in an accessible space. False claims: unclassified misdemeanor, fine up to $250.
- Caveat: the KDOR plate page still lists alternative criteria ("loss, or permanent loss of use, of one or both feet or one or both hands" or blindness) that are no longer in the current 8-160 definition. The statute controls.
- Personal property tax on vehicles: NOT exempt. K.S.A. 79-5107(e) exempts up to two vehicles only for current full-time military, deployed members, members stationed in Kansas, and Guard/reserve members. "The text does not mention veterans or disabled veterans." KDOR confirms property tax is still owed on the DV-plated vehicle.

### 2b. Purple Heart and other military plates (K.S.A. 8-1,140 et seq.)
- Source: https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_001_0140.html. Purple Heart for wounds "in military or naval combat against an armed enemy of the United States"; "payment of the regular license fee as provided in K.S.A. 8-143"; "one distinctive license plate for each such passenger vehicle, truck or motorcycle." No surviving spouse provision. KOVS lists the full military plate menu (Medal of Honor, POW, Gold Star Mother, Korea, Vietnam, Desert Storm, Iraqi Freedom, Enduring Freedom, U.S. Veteran, Families of the Fallen, branches, Bronze Star, Silver Star, 1st Infantry Division, Armed Service Occupation Medal). None is free except the DV plate.

### 2c. Driver's license: VETERAN designation; no fee waiver
- K.S.A. 8-243(e), https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch08/008_002_0043.html (history ends "L. 2025, ch. 97, § 3"): applicant "shall provide a copy of the veteran's DD form 214, NGB form 22 or equivalent discharge document" showing "an honorable discharge or a general discharge under honorable conditions." No fee for the designation is stated; regular license fees apply. KOVS: "Any person who is a Veteran may request that the DMV issue ... the designation 'VETERAN' displayed on the front" (K.S.A. 8-1234 for nondriver ID).
- No disabled veteran driver's license fee waiver exists in Kansas law that I could find.
- 2026 HB 2274 (per KLRD): homeless veterans may get a non-expiring nondriver ID card without a residence address.

## 3. Hunting, fishing, parks

### 3a. Free permanent hunting and fishing license, 30% (K.S.A. 32-934)
- Source: https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch32/032_009_0034.html (history "L. 2023, ch. 43, § 1; L. 2024, ch. 84, § 10; L. 2024, ch. 104, § 5; L. 2025, ch. 97, § 5; July 1"); KDWP Special Licenses and Permits page, https://www.ksoutdoors.gov/licenses-permits-fees/special-licenses-permits (read via browser; the old URL /License-Permits/Disabled-Veteran-Hunting-and-Fishing-Licenses now returns "Not found").
- Statute: issued "to any person residing in the state who submits to the secretary satisfactory proof that the person is a disabled veteran," free, as "a permanent hunting and fishing license"; disabled veteran = honorable or general under honorable conditions discharge, disability incurred or aggravated in line of duty, service-connected evaluation of at least 30%.
- KDWP page: "Kansas provides free, permanent hunting and fishing licenses to qualifying disabled veterans. The military service member must be honorably discharged, reside in the state of Kansas, and have service-connected disabilities of at least 30%. (K.S.A. 32-934) Please allow 2 weeks for processing." Apply via gooutdoorskansas.com / GoOutdoorsKS app (Customer Applications) or mail to KDWP Licensing, 512 SE 25th Ave, Pratt, KS 67124-8174. Required: "Applicant's current Kansas driver's license or KS ID showing proof of residency; Applicant's current disabled veterans entitlement papers showing proof of at least 30% disability; If applying for a hunting license, a copy of the applicant's Hunter's Education Card (if born on or after July 1, 1957)."
- KOVS: "Free hunting and fishing licenses are made available and issued based on state funding." Older KDWP releases (2009, 2011) said the benefit was "not applicable for park permits." Neither the statute nor the KDWP page says big game permits, stamps or park permits are included.
- Before 2024 the license was annual (365 days) and funded year to year; the 2024 amendments made it permanent.

### 3b. State park vehicle permits: no veteran discount
- Source: https://www.ksoutdoors.gov/licenses-permits-fees/kansas-state-park-entrance-and-camping-fees (browser-verified). 2025 fees: Annual Vehicle Permit $25.00; "Annual Vehicle Permit – Senior/Disabled (2025)" $13.75; Daily $5.00; Daily Senior/Disabled $3.25; Kansas State Park Passport (bought with vehicle registration at the county treasurer) $15.50. "Beginning November 1, 2026 several Kansas State Park fees will change": $39.00, $20.25, $7.00, $4.25 and $30.50 respectively.
- Who qualifies for Senior/Disabled: search snippets of the same page describe residents 65 or older or persons with disabilities who display a disability plate or placard issued under state law. I did not capture that sentence in the browser read, and nothing says whether a DV plate without the wheelchair symbol counts. Flagged needs_verification.
- Free park permits exist only for "active members of the Kansas National Guard, as annual funding allows" (KDWP Special Licenses page). No disabled veteran park pass exists.

## 4. Education and tuition

### 4a. Kansas Hero's Scholarship (K.S.A. 75-4364)
- Sources: Kansas Board of Regents 2026-2027 information sheet, https://www.kansasregents.gov/resources/PDF/Students/Student_Financial_Aid/Heros_2026-2027.pdf; statute https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch75/075_043_0064.html (history ends "L. 2024, ch. 82, § 24; May 2").
- Threshold language (statute (c)(1)(B)): servicemember "Died while serving in military service"; or "sustained a service-connected injury or disability that rendered the servicemember incapable" of continuing service; or "is entitled to compensation from the United States department of veterans affairs for a service-connected disability of at least 80%." Regents sheet: "Dependents or spouses of a current or former military servicemember, provided that the servicemember is a current resident of the State of Kansas (or was a resident of the State of Kansas at time of death)."
- Benefit: enrollment "without charge of tuition or fees" at "community colleges, the municipal university, state educational institutions," Washburn Institute of Technology and technical colleges; "not to exceed 10 semesters of undergraduate instruction." Not available at private or out-of-state schools. Required fees exclude program/lab/course fees, books and living costs.
- Spouse = "has not remarried." Dependent includes birth, adopted, step and other actually dependent children. No age limit in statute or sheet.
- Funding: "awarded on a first come, first served basis and funding is limited. ... once funding is exhausted, the application will close for the school year." Deadlines: "Summer 2026: July 1, 2026 Fall 2026: September 1, 2026 Spring 2027: February 1, 2027." Apply at sfa.kansasregents.gov; renew yearly; not retroactive.
- Documentation: DD-214, VA letter with combined rating of 80% or more, proof of Kansas residency (LES or state return, or Kansas ID/voter registration plus utility bill), marriage or birth certificate.
- Post-9/11 service requirement: present in 2024 guidance, absent from the 2026-27 sheet and current statute (2024 ch. 82 amendments).
- Also in statute: POW/MIA dependents (K.S.A. 73-1217, 10 semesters) and Vietnam-era service-connected deaths (73-1218, 12 semesters), per KLRD.

### 4b. In-state tuition (K.S.A. 48-3601; 76-729(c))
- Source: https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch48/048_036_0001.html (history ends "L. 2025, ch. 97, § 7; July 1").
- (a) Current armed forces members and their spouses/dependent children enrolled at a Kansas postsecondary institution "shall be deemed to be a resident of the state for the purpose of tuition and fees." (b) In-state rates regardless of length of residence for: (1) a person who files a letter of intent to establish residence, lives in Kansas while attending, and is eligible for federal veterans education benefits (e.g., Post-9/11 GI Bill); (2) a veteran "stationed in Kansas for at least 11 months during active service" or who "had established residency in Kansas prior to active service" and lives in Kansas at enrollment; (3) the spouse or dependent of such a veteran living in Kansas at enrollment. "Veteran" = active service including 38 C.F.R. § 3.7 groups, discharged "under an honorable discharge or a general discharge under honorable conditions." No time-since-discharge limit.
- KOVS restates the same rule. Regents military page (https://www.kansasregents.gov/students/military) says contact the registrar.

### 4c. Kansas Military Service Scholarship
- Source: KOVS State Veterans Benefits page, https://www.kovs.ks.gov/veteran-services/state-veterans-benefits-guide. Kansas resident who "received hostile fire pay for at least 90 days after September 11, 2001, or served less than such 90 days because of injuries"; honorable or general discharge or still serving; DD-214 or orders showing the deployment; FAFSA required ("financial need is a priority"); pays "undergraduate tuition and required fees for up to 8 semesters"; not for graduate students; "Priority Date: May 1." Not disability-based.

## 5. State income tax
- Source: KDOR 2025 Individual Income Tax Booklet, https://ksrevenue.gov/incomebook25.html; K.S.A. 79-32,121, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch79/079_032_0121.html (history ends "L. 2024, ch. 1, § 20 (Special Session); L. 2025, ch. 112, § 6; July 1"); KOVS page.
- Military retired pay: Schedule S line A14 "Retirement benefits specifically exempt from Kansas Income Tax," covering "Federal Civil Service Retirement or Disability Fund payments and any other amounts received as retirement benefits" from federal employment or the Armed Forces (K.S.A. 79-32,117(c)(vii)). No age or income limit.
- VA disability pay: KOVS lists "Department of Veterans Affairs Disability Payments" among items "not taxed in Kansas." (They are excluded from federal AGI, which is the Kansas starting point.)
- Additional exemption (79-32,121(b)): "any individual who has been honorably discharged from active service in any branch of the armed forces of the United States and who is certified by the United States department of veterans affairs or its successor to be in receipt of disability compensation at the 100% rate, if the disability is permanent and was sustained through military action or accident or resulted from disease contracted while in such active service, such individual shall be allowed an additional Kansas exemption of $2,250 for tax year 2023" ($2,320 for 2025 and later per the booklet and KOVS). KOVS calls it "100% Permanent and Total." TDIU is not mentioned; a TDIU veteran is "in receipt of disability compensation at the 100% rate," but permanence is still required. Standard 2025 exemptions: $18,320 joint, $9,160 other, $2,320 per dependent.
- Bonuses: line A17 subtracts recruitment/retention bonuses and certain military student loan repayments (79-32,117(c)(xvi)).
- SBP/DIC: no official Kansas source found that names them. DIC is excluded from federal AGI. SBP annuities are federal retirement benefits and appear to fall within the line A14 subtraction, but KDOR does not say so by name.
- 2024-2025 changes: the 2024 Special Session (SB 1, L. 2024 ch. 1) and L. 2025 ch. 112 reset exemption amounts; nothing veteran-specific beyond the exemption dollar figure.

## 6. Employment preference
- Sources: K.S.A. 73-201, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch73/073_002_0001.html (history ends "L. 2025, ch. 97, § 9"); Department of Administration eligibility page, https://admin.ks.gov/offices/personnel-services/jobs/veterans-preference/how-do-i-determine-if-i-am-eligible and "When is Veterans' Preference Given?"; KOVS page.
- Nature of preference: not points. Veterans are "preferred for initial employment and first promotion" if "competent" (good-faith determination that the applicant is likely to meet performance standards, documented before selection). Applies to the state and to Kansas counties and cities. Dept. of Administration: "Each veteran who meets the requirements for a vacant position shall be offered an interview for that vacancy" when the vacancy is a regular classified "Veterans Preference" Eligible (VPE) position and no Kansas Employee Preference Program applicant applied. "The veterans' preference laws do not guarantee the veteran a job."
- Eligibility (admin.ks.gov): honorable separation plus wartime/campaign service (dates listed), or "a disability certified by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs as being service connected, were issued the Purple Heart ... or were released from active service with a service-connected disability," or voluntary retirement at O-3 or below (O-4+ only if retired for combat wounds or disabled), or "a spouse of a veteran who has a 100% service-connected disability or 100% unemployable as determined by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs," or the unremarried spouse of a member who died in service, or a POW spouse. Statute exclusion: retirees at "pay grade 04 or above, unless the retirement resulted from combat wounds or the person has a service-connected disability rating of at least 10%."
- Process: notice within 30 days by certified mail to eligible veterans not hired; administrative appeal, then district court. DD-214 required (Bulletin 08-01).
- Disabled veteran business (K.S.A. 73-230, 75-3740; KOVS): "'Disabled Veteran' means a person who served ... and has a service-connected evaluation percentage equal to or greater than 30%"; business at least 51% owned/controlled; "goal of awarding at least 3% of all such contracts"; award to a disabled veteran business "whose total bid cost is not more than 10% higher than the lowest competitive bid."
- Military spouse licensing (K.S.A. 48-3406, effective July 1, 2025; KOVS): military spouses "shall be exempt from all fees assessed by any licensing body to obtain an occupational credential in Kansas." "Military servicemember" includes "a former member with an honorable discharge."
- KANSASWORKS: federal priority of service for veterans at workforce centers (Jobs for Veterans Act); not a state law benefit.
- Private employers may adopt written veteran preference policies (K.S.A. 73-231).

## 7. Other (homes, cemeteries, grants, bonuses)
- Veterans homes (KOVS Eligibility and Admissions, https://www.kovs.ks.gov/veteran-homes/eligibility-admissions; K.S.A. 76-1954, https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch76/076_019_0054.html): Kansas Soldiers' Home, Fort Dodge (domiciliary, long-term care, dementia unit, cottages) and Kansas Veterans' Home, Winfield; Northeast Kansas Veterans' Home (Topeka) appropriated in 2023 but federal match not released as of the 2026 session (KLRD). Eligibility: "Meet criteria established by the Department of Veteran Affairs for Veterans status," discharge "under conditions other than dishonorable," "Require 24-hour skilled nursing health care services," no sex offender registry or felony record; "On a space available basis, spouses, surviving spouses and Gold Star Parents may qualify." "While Kansas residency is not required for admission, preference will be given to Kansas residents." Priority "first given to Veterans who do not have adequate means of support." (Statute 76-1954(b) still says two years' Kansas residency; the KOVS page says residency is not required. Flagged.) Fees: KLRD says charges depend on ability to pay and "Veterans with a service-connected rating of 70 percent or more pay no resident fees"; not found on KOVS or in K.S.A. 76-1952. Flagged.
- Cemeteries (KOVS, https://www.kovs.ks.gov/veteran-cemeteries/program-information): four cemeteries (Fort Dodge, Fort Riley/Manhattan, WaKeeney, Winfield). NCA eligibility: discharge "under other than dishonorable conditions," death on active duty, or Guard/Reserve with 20 qualifying years entitled to retired pay. "State of Kansas residency requirement: None." Spouses, surviving spouses (including those remarried to a non-veteran if death on or after January 1, 2000), children under 21 (23 if full-time students), and permanently disabled adult children. KLRD: free burial space, grave liner, opening/closing, perpetual care, headstone or marker. Pre-register with KVCP Form 200. 2026 HB 2331 allows unclaimed cremated remains of veterans to go to KOVS or a national cemetery.
- Vital records: certified birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates "at no charge for Veterans and/or eligible Family members when requested through the KOVS for VA purposes only."
- Vietnam War Era Medallion (K.S.A. 73-1239) and Operation Recognition honorary diplomas (WWII, Korea, Vietnam).
- No state veteran bonus, emergency grant or housing grant program for individuals was found. The Military Emergency Relief Fund (income tax checkoff) helps active duty families; VCAP ($1.0 million per year, SFY 2024-2025) funds service organizations, not individuals.
- Guard: K.S.A. 48-284 death and disability benefits (CPI adjusted from July 1, 2025) apply to Kansas National Guard members on state duty only.

## 8. 2025-2026 law changes checked
- L. 2024 ch. 94 (HB 2098, enacted April 2024 by veto override): K.S.A. 79-3606h sales tax exemption, effective July 1, 2026. KDOR Notice 26-01 (May 15, 2026) and the KDOR application page confirm it took effect. KOVS says the exemption year runs July 1 to December 2026, then calendar years.
- L. 2025 ch. 97: amended 8-160, 8-243, 32-934, 48-3601, 73-201, 79-4502 and others, inserting the "38 C.F.R. § 3.7" groups language in the veteran and disabled veteran definitions (the content of HB 2273, whose own page shows "Died"; the enacting bill number could not be confirmed because the search budget ran out).
- L. 2025 ch. 123 § 11: 79-4508a household income definition (Kansas AGI) for tax year 2025+.
- L. 2025 ch. 112 § 6: 79-32,121 exemption amounts ($2,320 disabled veteran exemption for 2025+).
- K.S.A. 48-3406, effective July 1, 2025: military spouse occupational license fee exemption.
- 2026: HB 2274 (homeless veteran nondriver ID), HB 2331 (unclaimed cremated remains), HB 2533 (compact fee exemptions for active duty and spouses) per KLRD.
- Died: HB 2005 (2025-26 Veterans' Valor property tax credit), SB 434 House Substitute (2026, same plus repeal of the sales tax exemption), HB 2273 (2025 definitions bill as a standalone).
- KDWP state park fee increase effective November 1, 2026 (administrative, not legislative).
