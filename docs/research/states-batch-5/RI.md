# Rhode Island - narrative (verified 2026-10-17)

Hint corrections: military retirement exemption confirmed (tax year 2023+). "Gold Star parents" is NOT in 44-3-4 as read (the statute text has no Gold Star item; the 2022 municipal report lists a Gold Star Parent line for some towns). Free hunting/fishing is for 100% rated veterans (DEM form), and state parks free for 100% (DEM rule). Tuition waiver is a disabled-veteran waiver (10%-100%), not just Guard. Tuition for dependents not found.

## Property tax - R.I. Gen. Laws 44-3-4 (read in full at https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE44/44-3/44-3-4.htm; history shows amendments through P.L. 2025 ch. 189 and ch. 220, eff. July 1, 2025)
- (a)(1): service-period list incl. Cold War "between 1947 through 1991"; "exempted from taxation to the amount of one thousand dollars ($1,000)" with town exceptions (e.g., Burrillville $4,000, Newport $4,000, Lincoln "shall not exceed ten thousand dollars", Glocester $30,000, Westerly up to $40,500).
- (a)(2): "the exemption is not allowed in favor of any person who is not a legal resident of the state, or unless the person entitled to the exemption has presented to the assessors, on or before the last day on which sworn statements may be filed" ... "which evidence shall stand so long as the person's legal residence remains unchanged"; South Kingstown "at least five (5) days prior to the certification of the tax roll."
- (b): "ten-thousand dollar ($10,000) exemption from local taxation on real property" for a veteran or unmarried widow/widower "determined... to be totally disabled through service-connected disability" who received "specially adapted housing"; domicile only; evidence to assessors.
- (c): "totally disabled through service-connected disability may, by ordinance... receive a ten thousand dollar ($10,000) exemption" real or personal; North Kingstown $11,000, Westerly $39,000, Cumberland up to $47,544, Narragansett $20,000 real / $12,000 vehicle, Cranston up to $250,000 "and be extended to the unmarried widow or widower."
- (d): remarriage no bar if "void, has been terminated by death, or has been annulled or dissolved."
- (e): POW additional $15,000 by ordinance. (h): Bristol may exempt partially disabled. (i): "totally and permanently disabled" owning an adapted homestead (VA SAH grant, ADA guidelines): "exempt from all taxation on the homestead", surviving spouse too; Westerly $46,500. (k): Scituate may give a credit to 100% service-connected.
- Local examples (self-reported, assessment date 12/31/21, released Sept 2022): https://municipalfinance.ri.gov/documents/data/exemptions/Veterans-Senior-Exemptions-Report.pdf Providence totally disabled $17,247 (deadline March 15), Warwick $346 credit (March 15), West Warwick $200 credit. Report disclaimer: figures self-reported, ask the local assessor. Motor vehicle column N/A.
- Pending (not enacted as far as found): 2026 S 2916 / 2025 H 5309A to raise minimum to $6,000; H 7084. Not confirmed passed.

## Vehicles - R.I. Gen. Laws 31-6-8 (https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE31/31-6/31-6-8.htm; amended P.L. 2023 ch. 53/54 eff. June 12, 2023)
- "(1) Has a combined service connected disability rating of one hundred percent (100%); or (2) Is considered 'individually unemployable' due to the veteran's service-connected disability, shall be exempt from the payment of any fee for the annual registration of and a license to operate that motor vehicle."
- "registration plates designated 'Disabled Veteran'" for an automobile or commercial vehicle up to 12,000 lbs; transferable to surviving spouse "for the spouse's lifetime or until the spouse remarries"; "Only one set... shall be issued."
- DMV page claims (veteran designation free, disabled-vet license free for life) seen only in search summary, DMV pages blocked: listed under Could not verify.

## Recreation
- DEM form (read): https://dem.ri.gov/programs/bpoladm/manserv/hfb/fishing/pdfs/fishdis.pdf "Veterans: ... applicants are required to present the ORIGINAL DOCUMENT OF THE FINAL DECISION(S), on Agency letterhead from the Department of Veteran Affairs for a fishing license." "THIS LICENSE ONCE ISSUED IS PERMANENT." "NO FEE REQUIRED FOR THE FISHING AND COMBINATION DISABILITY LICENSES." The form's headline is "100% Permanent Disability"; the veteran paragraph does not state the 100% number itself (the headline does). Permits/stamps carry fees. No date on form.
- State parks: 250-RICR-100-00-3 section 3.3(B)(3) and 3.5(A): https://rules.sos.ri.gov/Regulations/part/250-100-00-3?reg_id=8531 "Veterans' benefits and who shall be determined under applicable federal law by the federal Department of Veterans' Affairs to be one hundred percent (100%) disabled through a service-connected disability." "No fee shall be charged to any person with a disability regardless of age"; fees = parking, admittance, golf; excludes camping, picnic table, equestrian etc. Statute 32-1-17 (via fetch summary): https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE32/32-1/32-1-17.htm
- Pass logistics (RI State Parks HQ, 1100 Tower Hill Rd, North Kingstown; yearly VA letter) came from search summary of riparks.ri.gov (blocked): unverified.

## Education - R.I. Gen. Laws 30-30.1-1 (https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE30/30-30.1/30-30.1-1.htm; last amended P.L. 2016)
- "Any veteran who is a permanent resident of this state who submits proof sufficient to establish a veterans' rated ten percent (10%) to one hundred percent (100%) disability ... shall be entitled to take courses at any public institution of higher education in the state without the payment of tuition, exclusive of other fees and charges". Must apply for financial aid; aid applied to tuition. No dependent benefit in text.

## Employment - R.I. Gen. Laws 36-4-19 (https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE36/36-4/36-4-19.htm; last amended 2012)
- "service credits amounting to five (5) points for veterans or service credits amounting to ten (10) points for disabled veterans" on entrance tests, "but not for promotion", after meeting the minimum earned rating. "Disabled veteran" defined in chapter 3 of title 36 (not read).
