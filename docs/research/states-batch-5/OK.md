# Oklahoma (OK) - verified 2026-10-17

Agency: Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs, https://oklahoma.gov/veterans (benefits page published 01/12/2026).

## Property tax
- OTC Form 998 (Tax Year 2026, revised 11-2025): https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/ad-valorem/current/998.pdf . Quoted: "Is the applicant certified by the U.S.D.V.A. to have 100% permanent disability sustained through military action or accident?" "Is the applicant receiving benefit compensation at the 100% rate?" "Is the applicant the surviving spouse of such 100% compensated veteran?" "The 100% disabled veterans cannot be approved if you do not own and occupy the homestead property as your place of residence on January 1 each year." Deed must be recorded with County Clerk "no later than February 1." Applicant gives assessor "a current U.S.D.V.A. benefits award letter." No filing deadline stated. TDIU not addressed.
- Const. Art. 10 Sec. 8E (via https://50constitutions.org/ok/constitution/section-id-85860): head of household honorably discharged, VA-certified "a one hundred percent (100%) permanent disability"; exemption for "the full amount of the fair cash value of the homestead"; also "the surviving spouse"; must prove Oklahoma residency and have qualified/be eligible for homestead exemption; replacement homestead carries the exemption.
- ODVA page (https://oklahoma.gov/veterans/benefits): "The exemption would be for the full fair cash value of the homestead."
- Pending only: HJR 1009 / HJR 1082 (2025-26 introduced resolutions on partial exemptions); not law.

## Sales tax / excise
- OTC Help Center Exemptions https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/exemptions.html (no date; footer 2019): "Sales qualifying for the exemption are limited to $25,000.00 per year." Surviving spouse not remarried: "$1000.00 per year." "exempt from sales and use tax ONLY." Apply via OkTAP with VA Muskogee letter; ODVA 1-888-655-2838. Not exempt from 7% medical marijuana excise tax.
- Service Oklahoma https://oklahoma.gov/service/all-services/auto-vehicle/fees.html: "The exemption only applies to 1 vehicle purchased every 3 years from the date of purchase."

## Plates / license
- Form 751-J (rev. 2/2025): "Applicant must be at least 50% service connected disabled to qualify." Fee $5.00 + $1.50 insurance = $6.50 picked up. OAC 260:135-7-152 (via law.cornell.edu): 50% option of DAV plate; regular tag for two vehicles of one ton or less. Accessibility-symbol version requires parking placard number.
- Free driver license (47 O.S. 6-101 P, quoted on ODVA page) for 100% rate permanent disability.

## Recreation
- Wildlife Dept Lifetime License Info Sheet (2024-06): "Resident Disabled Veteran Combo (0-59% $200 or 60-100% $25)"; VA Award Letter required.
- ODVA page: free permit and Wildlife Land Stamp exemption at 60%; 100% exempt from deer gun/archery/primitive/bonus deer license fees (quoted from statute on ODVA page; not checked against statute).
- ODVA page: honorably discharged resident veterans get free admission to state parks and museums with Oklahoma ID and DD 214.

## Income tax
- OTC https://oklahoma.gov/tax/helpcenter/income-tax.html: "Beginning in Tax Year 2022, each individual may exclude 100% of their retirement benefits." Schedule 511-A. ODVA page still says greater of 75% or $10,000 (stale).

## Employment
- OMES Civil Service FAQ https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/omes/documents/CivilServiceFAQ.pdf: "Veterans Preference and points no longer apply to the hiring of state employees." Office of Veterans Placement gives a guaranteed interview; DD 214 required. Special disabled veterans may use 72 O.S. 401-404 instead (text not confirmed).
