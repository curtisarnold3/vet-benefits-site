# North Dakota research (verified 2026-10-17)

Sources are official: ndlegis.gov Century Code PDFs (code PDFs created 2025-06 to 2026-05), tax.nd.gov SFN 24770 (2026), veterans.nd.gov, parkrec.nd.gov, gf.nd.gov.

## Property tax credit - N.D.C.C. 57-02-08.8 (https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t57c02.pdf)
Quote: "eligible for a credit applied against the first nine thousand dollars of taxable valuation of the homestead owned and occupied ... equal to the percentage of the disabled veteran's disability compensation rating". Threshold: "service-connected disability of fifty percent or greater" or "extra-schedular rating to include individual unemployability which results in the veteran being paid at the one hundred percent rate". Surviving spouse receiving DIC "receives a one hundred percent credit". Credit "terminates at the end of the taxable year of the death of the applicant." Application: SFN 24770 (2026) "must be filed with the county auditor by April 1 of the first year for which the credit is claimed" with DD-214 and VA certificate (https://www.tax.nd.gov/sites/www/files/documents/forms/property-tax/property-tax-general/application-for-disabled-veterans-property-tax-credit.pdf). Hint of "$8,100"/$180,000 was off: current is $9,000 taxable value. 2025 HB 1266 amending this section failed in the Senate (secondary search summary).

## Registration fee waiver / DAV plate - 39-04-18(2)(j); https://www.veterans.nd.gov/dav-plates
Quote: "100 percent service related disabled" via VA certification letter; "There is no annual license fee"; "two excise (sales) tax exempt vehicles ... at a time"; plates barred on 26,000 lb+ vehicles; may park "in designated handicap parking spots in North Dakota" (39-01-15). Statute text (https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t39c04.pdf): no more than two vehicles, extra-schedular/unemployability at 100% rate counts. 39-04-19(3): sales/use tax exemption, two vehicles. 2019 HB 1397 (lower to 50%) failed.

## Hunting/fishing - 20.1-03-12 (https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t20-1c03.pdf); https://gf.nd.gov/licensing/resident
Quote subsection 46: combined general game, habitat stamp, small game, fur-bearer license for "a resident disabled veteran who has at least a fifty percent service-connected disability ... ten dollars." Subsection 6: fishing license $25, "ten dollars" for disabled veteran at least 50%. GF page: Veteran Combined Hunting $10 + $2 certificate; fishing $10 + $2. 2025 HB 1147 (secondary search) lowered to 50% at $3; later fee bill raised to $10. Page shows only "Copyright 2025".

## State parks - https://www.parkrec.nd.gov/dailyannual-permits
Quote: "50 percent or greater service-related disability" and former POWs get free lifetime pass; "All other ND veterans with service-related disabilities may purchase an annual permit for $28." No update date shown.

## Dependent tuition waiver - https://www.veterans.nd.gov/nd-dependent-tuition-waiver (15-10-18.2, 15-10-18.3)
Quote: "one hundred percent service-connected disability"; extra-schedular/unemployability counts; free tuition and fees, undergraduate degree or certificate; excludes "aviation flight charges". Five-year residency rule for child via other parent. No update date. NDDVA guidance PDF effective 2026-05-27 exists (not read).

## Income tax - 57-38-01.2(1)(r) (https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t57c38.pdf)
Quote: "Reduced by the amount received by a taxpayer as retired military personnel benefits, including ... surviving spouse ... only to the extent the amount was included in federal taxable income." ND has an income tax.

## Employment - ch. 37-19.1 (https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t37c19-1.pdf)
Quote: "add five points for a veteran and ten points for a disabled veteran"; disabled veteran "first entitled to the position" for unscored hiring; VA letter "less than one year old".
