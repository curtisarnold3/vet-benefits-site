# South Carolina - verified 2026-10-17 (details are in SC.json)

Sources are official only: scstatehouse.gov Code of Laws, dor.sc.gov, dmv.sc.gov, dnr.sc.gov, scdva.sc.gov, southcarolinaparks.com. No page showed a last-updated date unless noted.

## Property tax (S.C. Code 12-37-220(B)) - https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c037.php
- Home: "'eligible owner' means: (A) a veteran of the Armed Forces of the United States who is permanently and totally disabled as a result of a service-connected disability and who files with the Department of Revenue a certificate signed by the county service officer certifying this disability". "'permanently and totally disabled' means the inability to perform substantial gainful employment by reason of a medically determinable impairment ... twelve months or more or result in death". House = dwelling and lot classified under 12-43-220(c).
- Surviving spouse: "while remaining unmarried, who resides in the house, and who owns the house in fee or for life"; also spouse of a member killed in action.
- Vehicles (B)(3): "two private passenger vehicles owned or leased by any disabled veteran, or the spouse ... designated by the veteran for which special license tags have been issued ... or, in lieu of the license, if the veteran has a certificate signed by the county service officer or the Veterans Administration of the total and permanent disability". Surviving spouse: one vehicle.
- SCDOR https://dor.sc.gov/property/exempt-property: "Certificate from VA or Local County Service Officer certifying total and permanent service-connected disability, with the effective date"; "VA Rating decision letters do not meet requirements of law." Apply via MyDORWAY or Form PT-401I; processing "10-12 weeks"; no deadline listed. Property.Exemptions@dor.sc.gov, 803-898-5700. TDIU not mentioned.
- 2024 change (SCDVA news 2024-06-06; SCDOR tax tip dated 2025-11-04 https://dor.sc.gov/tax-tips/veterans-learn-more-about-sc-property-tax-exemptions): claim from year disabled; "retroactive to the previous two years" if taxes paid on time (SCDVA article says "retroactive to 2022" - conflict).
- Homestead 12-37-250: "first fifty thousand dollars of the fair market value"; apply to county auditor "before July sixteenth".
- Pending, not law: H.4700 (partial exemption by rating %, last action 2026-01-21, Ways and Means).

## Plates - SCDMV MV-37 (02/2026) https://dmv.sc.gov/sites/scdmv/files/2026-02/MV-37%20Application%20for%20Military%20License%20Plate_sub.pdf, FAQ (2024-03-25), https://dmv.sc.gov/node/1522
- Disabled Veteran: registration fee "Free", no free metered parking, no handicap parking; "Letter from the County or US Department of Veterans Affairs Office showing the disability as the loss of or use of a leg, arm, or vision or classified as totally and permanently disabled due to service-connected disabilities." Wheelchair version: Free, free metered parking, handicap parking, needs RG-007A. Surviving spouse: only if plate held before May 6, 2022. Old plate statute 56-3-1110 repealed (2021 Act 38, eff May 6, 2022).

## Hunting/fishing - https://www.dnr.sc.gov/disabled.html; Code 50-9-525
- "A resident who is determined to be disabled and receiving benefits under ... the Veterans Administration ... may obtain a three year disability combination license ... at no cost." No VA percentage. The old 50-9-820 certificate I was hinted about was not found in Chapter 9.

## State parks - Code 51-3-60 https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t51c003.php
- "Any South Carolina resident who is a veteran and who has been classified by the Veterans Administration as permanently and totally disabled may also enter any state park without charge upon presentation ... of an identification card from the county veterans affairs officer". Discounts not less than 35% (51-3-65). SCPRT FAQ: 100% disabled residents 35% off, all-parks passport 50% off. Free-campsite bill H.4811 pending.

## Tuition - Code 59-111-20 https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c111.php; Act 135 of 2026 (H.3453, signed 2026-05-15) https://www.scstatehouse.gov/sess126_2025-2026/bills/3453.htm; https://scdva.sc.gov/education
- "(5) is permanently and totally disabled, as determined by the Veterans Administration from any cause". Child 26 or younger, undergraduate. I could not read the full amended Act 135 text; only that it adds residency "since birth".
- Not found: tuition waiver for the disabled veteran personally.

## Income tax - SCDOR RR 22-11 https://dor.sc.gov/resources-site/lawandpolicy/Advisory%20Opinions/RR22-11.pdf
- "An individual taxpayer may deduct all military retirement income" (12-6-1171), effective tax year 2022, any age; surviving spouse also.
