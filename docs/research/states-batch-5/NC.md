# North Carolina (NC) - verified 2026-10-17

Agency: NC Department of Military and Veterans Affairs, https://www.milvets.nc.gov/

## Property tax
- G.S. 105-277.1C (https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_105/GS_105-277.1C.html): "The first forty-five thousand dollars ($45,000) of appraised value of the residence is excluded from taxation." Qualifies by VA certification of "a service-connected, permanent, and total disability" or benefits under 38 U.S.C. 2101, as of January 1. Applications "must be accepted at any time up to and through June 1." Surviving spouse "who has not remarried." No income test. No date shown; last session-law cites 2008-2010.
- NCDMVA page (https://www.milvets.nc.gov/benefits-services/veterans-property-tax-relief): NCDVA-9 + AV-9 to county tax office, "June 1 of the current tax year." Co-owners capped at $45,000. No updated date.
- HB 118 (2025) would raise to $61,000; last action 9/23/2025 re-referred to House Rules (https://www.ncleg.gov/BillLookUp/2025/H118). Not law. SB 660 (Honoring Sacrifice) stuck in Senate Rules since 3/26/2025.
## Vehicle
- G.S. 20-79.4(b)(64): "Issuable to a veteran ... who suffered a 100% service-connected disability." (190) Partially Disabled: "less than 100%."
- NCDMVA plates page: "combined rating of 100% or receive Individual Unemployability (I.U.)"; license plate fee waived; one MVR-33A per vehicle. MVR-33A rev 5/2025: Disabled Veteran under "One Free Plate Per Person Per Year." Plate guidebook: special fee "$0.00" "in addition to regular license fee"; personalization $30.
- G.S. 20-87: $1.55 fee for vehicle given by federal government for war disability. G.S. 105-187.6 (HUT) has no veteran exemption; no parking privilege found.
## Recreation
- NCWRC https://www.ncwildlife.gov/outdoors/accessible-opportunities/disabled-licenses and form NCDMVA-50 (Rev 4/2025): "fifty percent or more disabled veteran as determined by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs"; fees $15 / $131 / $144 + $5 transaction fee; mail/in person only.
- State Parks free Annual Pass: https://ncparks.gov/about-us/fees/annual-passes/press-release/free-annual-pass-veterans-disabilities (dated 9/20/2024), S.L. 2024-45.
## Education
- Scholarship synopsis (Rev 8-2021) https://scholarships.milvets.nc.gov/Pages/NCDMVAScholarshipSynopsis.pdf: Class I-B "rated by USDVA as 100% disabled due to wartime service"; Class II 20% to under 100% or Purple Heart. Under 25; NC resident. Dated 2021, so amounts may have changed (flagged).
- G.S. 116-143.3A: in-state tuition without 12-month wait for veterans/dependents (covered individuals).
## Income tax
- 2025 D-401 instructions: 4.25% rate; Line 21 uniformed services retirement for "at least 20 years" or medically retired; Bailey Line 20. DOR notice (posted 5/2/2022, updated 8/5/2022): deduction for tax years beginning on or after 1/1/2021.
## Employment
- G.S. 126-81/126-82: experience credit; eligible veteran includes "A veteran who suffered a service-connected disability during peacetime" and spouses. Amended 2025-25 s.17(a).
