# Pennsylvania (PA) - research narrative, verified 2026-10-17

Full structured data: PA.json (13 benefit rows, 12 notes). No page showed a last-updated date unless stated.

## Property tax - Disabled Veterans' Real Estate Tax Exemption
URL: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmva/pennsylvania-veterans/pa-vetconnect/state-veterans-programs/financial-assistance/retx (old URL dmva.pa.gov/.../RETX.aspx now 404s)
- "100 percent permanent service-connected disability, total disability individual unemployability, or service-connected blindness, paraplegia, or loss of two or more limbs as rated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs"
- "Applicants with an annual income of $114,637 or less are given a presumption of need... Applicants whose gross annual income exceeds $114,637 will be considered to have a financial need for the exemption when their allowable monthly expenses exceed monthly household income."
- "On January 1, 2025, the State Veterans' Commission adjusted the presumptive need level... upward to $114,637". Hint's $114,637 confirmed (the older figures $92,594 and $108,046 are outdated). No 2026 figure found.
- Also: PA resident; honorable/under honorable conditions; wartime service; principal dwelling; owned solely, with spouse, or as estate by entirety. Mandatory criteria apply to "disabled veterans and their surviving spouses."
- Apply: "contact your County Director of Veterans Affairs in the county you reside." Questions: Ra-retx@pa.gov. Legislative link: 51 Pa.C.S. Ch. 89 (hint cited 8902), PA 43 Subchapter C.
- TDIU: listed explicitly. 100% schedular: only if permanent. No deadline found.

## Vehicle
- MV-145V (1-26): https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/penndot/documents/public/dvspubsforms/bmv/bmv-forms/mv-145v.pdf. "A Severely Disabled Veteran who has a 100% service-connected disability that is certified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Regional Office Administrator may have free registration for one qualifying vehicle"; same for "being paid at 100% as a result of unemployability." Qualifying for free: registered gross weight 9,000 lbs or less. SDV plate benefit: parking in disability spaces and 60 minutes beyond legal period. Disabled Veteran plate (12): "FEE REQUIRED (NOTE: No Special Parking Privileges.)". Motorcycle decal no fee.
- MV-371DV (4-25): https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/penndot/documents/public/dvspubsforms/bmv/bmv-forms/mv-371dv.pdf. "may have free registration for ONE qualifying vehicle"; also limb/eye/paralysis, POW, Purple Heart, Medal of Honor.
- Bulletin 25-05 (Feb 2025) introduced MV-371DV (binary PDF; read via search summary).
- Hint said "vehicle registration fee exemption": confirmed, but limited to 100% (or TDIU paid at 100%) and one vehicle.

## Hunting (Game Commission)
URL: 2026-2027 Instructions for Issuing Agents Manual, https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/pgc/documents/hunttrap/licensesandpermits/2026%20instructions%20for%20issuing%20agents%20manual.pdf
- "Eligible veteran's disability must be service-related and incurred during a war or armed conflict, AND meet one of the following three criteria: 1. The loss of one or more limbs 2. The loss of use of one or more limbs 3. Veterans Affairs certification of 100% physical disability or documentation that the veteran is receiving 100% or total compensation."
- 315 Resident DV Hunting (100%): no charge, annual. 316 Lifetime: county treasurers only. 317 Reduced (60-99%): $2.97, replacement $6.97.
- Not confirmed on an official page: SB 1020 (would drop the war requirement) was only described by legislators' sites; status unknown.

## Fishing (Fish and Boat Commission)
URL: https://www.pa.gov/services/fishandboat/apply-for-fishing-license-disabled-veteran
- "Be 100% disabled as certified by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)." Free license; Combination Trout/Lake Erie Permit "issued free of charge." 60-99%: reduced license (no price listed). Apply at county treasurer, Harrisburg office, or regional office. Page shows only "Copyright 2026".
- FAQ page still says "Have a disability incurred in any war or armed conflict consisting of loss or use of one or more limbs or total blindness. OR are 100% disabled" (older wording). Claim of Act 21 of 2025 removing the war requirement appears in search summary of a secondary source; not confirmed on an official page.

## State parks
https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dcnr/recreation/where-to-go/state-parks: "Pennsylvania state parks do not charge an entrance fee." Camping page: "People with a disability can receive a $4.50 discount from the base campsite price upon request." No veteran pass found.

## Education
https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmva/pennsylvania-veterans/pa-vetconnect/state-veterans-programs/financial-assistance/educational-gratuity-program: "A 100% permanent and total service-connected disability rating"; children 16-23, five years PA residency, approved PA school, financial need, "Payment will not exceed $500 per term or semester per qualified child... no longer than four scholastic years (eight payments)." Apply via County Director. No tuition waiver for the veteran found. National Guard EAP (hint) not researched (not a disabled-veteran benefit).

## Income tax
https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/forms-and-publications/pa-personal-income-tax-guide/gross-compensation: "Retired or retainer pay of a member or former member of a uniform service calculated under Chapter 71 of Title 10, U.S. Code... is also exempt from PA PIT." Disability pension "for physical injuries or sickness resulting from active service in the armed forces" exempt. Military Pay page: https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/personal-income-tax/military-pay (active duty outside PA not taxable; inside PA taxable). PA has a flat income tax (not a no-tax state).

## Employment
https://www.pa.gov/agencies/employment/veterans/veterans--preference: "All eligible Veterans will receive an additional 10 points added to their final examination score."
