# Oregon research (verified 2026-10-17)

Agency: Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs, https://www.oregon.gov/odva/

## Property tax (ORS 307.250-307.283)
- Source: https://www.oregon.gov/dor/forms/FormsPubs/veteran-spouse-exemption_310-676.pdf (DOR 150-310-676, Rev. 11-07-25; cover dated January 2026).
- "you may be entitled to exempt $27,092 or $32,512 of your homestead property's assessed value ... The exemption amount increases by 3 percent each year."
- $32,512: "service-connected disabilities of 40 percent or more." $27,092: "disabilities of 40 percent or more" or physician-certified each year, with income "can't be more than 185 percent of the annual Federal Poverty Level Guidelines" (includes disability compensation and retirement pay).
- "Standard filing: ... on or before April 1 preceding the tax year"; acquired March 1 to July 1: "within 30 days after the date of acquisition." Late physician-certified: by May 1 with $10 fee. Claim form 150-303-086 to county assessor. VA/armed forces certificate must be dated within three years.
- Surviving spouse: unmarried; higher amount if veteran died of service-connected cause or received "at least one year of the maximum exemption".
- Residency: "own and live on your homestead property"; Oregon resident.
- 2025 bills: HB 2361 (threshold to 10%) and SB 387 (higher amounts) show "In House Committee"/"In Senate Committee" at olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Measures/Overview/HB2361 and SB387. ODVA's July 4, 2025 digest of signed bills listed no property tax bill (via search summary; not read in full). DOR Nov 2025 guide still 40%.
- ODVA page https://www.oregon.gov/odva/benefits/pages/taxes.aspx: "veterans must be certified ... as having disabilities of 40 percent or more". No date shown.
- TDIU / 100% schedular / P&T not addressed separately; threshold is the certified percentage.

## Vehicle
- ORS 805.100 (https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors805.html): honorable discharge, 90 consecutive days or service-connected discharge, VA letter; fee "is the one-time registration fee established by ORS 803.420"; no commercial vehicles; DMV may issue registrations equal to the number of qualifying disabled veterans in the household.
- DMV Form 6736 / Chapter K (06/05/26) via search: $15 registration + plate fee, about $41 new plate; one set per disabled veteran; not valid for disabled parking. I did not open these PDFs directly, only search summaries, so flagged needs_verification.
- ODVA https://www.oregon.gov/odva/benefits/pages/licenses.aspx: plates for "a veteran who has a service-connected disability".

## Recreation
- ODFW https://myodfw.com/articles/exclusive-benefits-uniformed-services-and-disabled-veterans (dated July 8, 2026): "at least a 25 percent disabled rating"; six consecutive months residency; free hunting, fishing, shellfish incl. Columbia Basin and Ocean endorsements; elk tag $29. ODVA recreation page says $25 elk tag (conflict). 65%+ may qualify for the Oregon Disabilities Hunting and Fishing Permit.
- State parks https://stateparks.oregon.gov/index.cfm?do=v.page&id=19 (fetched live, no date): "Oregon veterans with any level of service-connected disability"; four nights at a time at one park or four per month; "The pass is valid for 10 years." ODVA says four years and 26 parks. OPRD Division 15 rules "ADOPTED, effective April 6, 2026" with changes to special access pass; proposal text said Oregon residents only and 10 nights/year, adopted summary says 4 nights/month. Adopted OAR text not found. Needs verification.

## Education
- ORS 350.285 https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_350.285 (third-party mirror of statute; used for text, verify at oregonlegislature.gov): 100% disabled service member, Purple Heart children, tuition only, 4 yrs undergrad / 2 yrs grad, child 23 or younger.
- ODVA education https://www.oregon.gov/odva/benefits/pages/education.aspx: nonresident veterans pay "no greater than the Oregon resident rate", undergraduate; not graduate or active duty. No veteran tuition waiver for the disabled veteran himself found.
- 2025 bill digest mentioned a proposal for 80%+ disabled tuition waiver; no evidence it passed.

## Income tax
- 2025 OR-17 (Rev. 01-29-26) https://www.oregon.gov/dor/forms/FormsPubs/publication-or-17_101-431_2025.pdf: federal pension subtraction code 307 "If all of your months of federal service occurred or points were earned before October 1, 1991, subtract 100 percent"; none if no pre-1991 service; proportion otherwise. Military pay: other taxable military pay subtraction "up to $6,000". Combat pay not taxed.
- VA compensation: ODVA says "tax free"; DOR publication does not name it. Flagged.

## Employment
- ORS 408.230 https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors408.html: 5 points veteran, 10 disabled veteran; disabled veteran has a VA rating (no minimum percent); Oregon National Guard added (2025 c.137). DAS guide https://www.oregon.gov/das/HR/Documents/Veteran%20Preference%20Guide.pdf (search summary only).
