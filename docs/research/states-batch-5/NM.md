# New Mexico (NM) - research narrative, verified 2026-10-17

Agency: New Mexico Department of Veterans' Services, https://www.dvs.nm.gov/ (HANDOFF hint said nmdvs.org; the current agency site is dvs.nm.gov, and nmdvs.org is an older domain.)

## Property tax
- Source: HB 285 (2026) final text, https://www.nmlegis.gov/Sessions/26%20Regular/final/HB0285.PDF (amends NMSA 7-37-5.1, 7-38-17, 7-38-21, 7-38-24). Quote: "exempt from property taxation in an amount equal to the percentage of the veteran's disability as determined by federal law multiplied by the taxable value of the property after the amount that may be exempted pursuant to Section 7-37-5 NMSA 1978 is deducted; provided that the disabled veteran occupies the property continuously as the veteran's principal place of residence". Definition: "has been determined pursuant to federal law to have a permanent service-connected disability". Claim: "no later than thirty days after the mailing of the county assessor's notices of valuation". Highest percentage used if several disabled veteran owners. Effective Feb 25, 2026 per Bloomberg Tax summary (secondary; not relied on).
- HB 47 (2025) final: https://www.nmlegis.gov/Sessions/25%20Regular/final/HB0047.pdf. Quote: "for tax year 2025, ten thousand dollars ($10,000)"; 2026 onward inflation-adjusted. "The provisions of Section 2 of this act apply to the 2026 and subsequent property tax years." Surviving spouse: "the surviving spouse and the disabled veteran were married at the time of the disabled veteran's death" and "continues to occupy the property continuously".
- Joint DVS/TRD release, Aug 13, 2026: https://www.dvs.nm.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/7.21.26_Disabled_Tax_Exemption_Joint_Press_Release-FINAL.pdf. Quote: "allow partially disabled veterans to receive prorated property tax exemptions based on service-connected disability rating, effective 2026." Process: get certificate from NMDVS, file with county assessor. 7,096 partially disabled veterans got it March-June 2026.
- DVS Form 1 (rev. March 2025): https://www.dvs.nm.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/100023_DVS_DVS1_VeteransExemption_Fillable.pdf. Quote: "Up to Ten thousand dollars ($10,000) of the taxable value"; standard needs 90 days continuous active duty, honorable (not dishonorable or misconduct), NM resident. Disabled needs "determined pursuant to federal law as a service-connected disabled Veteran by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs". Residency proof limited to NM license/ID, voter registration, or latest NM income tax return. Surviving spouse of disabled veteran "is entitled to disabled exemption, even if the deceased veteran did not apply".
- DVS benefits page (no date shown): https://www.dvs.nm.gov/benefits/
- Not found: any official statement on TDIU, or on non-permanent ratings. Hint "2024 Amendment raised exemption to $10,000" confirmed via HB 47 title; hint "100% only" is out of date.

## Vehicle
- MVD special plates, https://www.mvd.newmexico.gov/chapter-7-special-plates/ (no date). Quote: "The 50 percent disabled veteran plate is available for up to two vehicles, including motorcycles... No fee, including the regular registration fee, shall be collected". Form MVD10353. Wheelchair symbol option for HP-eligible.
- DVS page: reduced registration at "two-thirds the rates", motor vehicle excise tax exemption for loss/total loss of use of legs at or above the ankle or arms at or above the wrist.

## Recreation
- State Parks, https://www.emnrd.nm.gov/spd/fees-permits/for-veterans/ : HB 161 pass from July 1, 2025 for resident veterans; excludes reservation fees $3-$10, group shelters, cabins, yurts. HB 161 final text defines veteran (not dishonorable discharge; Guard/reserve need six continuous years).
- Wildlife, https://www.wildlife.state.nm.us/fishing/licenses-permits/discounts-exceptions (page shows 2026 updates). Quote: "New Mexico residents who are 100% disabled as a result of having served in the armed services are eligible for a free lifetime game-hunting & fishing license." Others: "$10 combination game-hunting & fishing license".
- Monuments and Museums pass 50%+: DVS benefits page.

## Education
- HED, https://hed.nm.gov/financial-aid/scholarships/vietnam-veterans (Wartime and Vietnam scholarships; tuition, required fees and books; residents; DVS certifies).
- DVS page: immediate in-state tuition for veterans, spouses, children; Children of Deceased Veterans scholarship (not disability-based).

## Income tax
- 2025 PIT-ADJ instructions, https://realfile.tax.newmexico.gov/2025pit-adj-ins.pdf, line 24: "$30,000 of armed forces retirement pay". Agency analysis (SB 116 fiscal report, 1/30/2026) says tax years 2024-2026 are $30,000 and statute sunsets; SB 116 (remove cap) reported postponed indefinitely (tracker, not official page).

## Employment
- DVS page: 5 points veterans/Guard, 10 points service-connected disabled. Statute NMSA 10-9-13.2 not fetched from an official page.
