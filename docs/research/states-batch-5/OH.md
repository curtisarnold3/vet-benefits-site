# Ohio verification narrative (verified 2026-10-17)

Agency: Ohio Department of Veterans Services, https://dvs.ohio.gov/ (DVS site pages for benefits were mostly 404/empty; benefits were verified from statutes and agency pages below).

## Property tax - enhanced homestead (R.C. 323.151, 323.152)
- 323.151(F): "'Disabled veteran' means a person who is a veteran of the armed forces ... who has been discharged or released from active duty ... under honorable conditions, and who has received a total disability rating or a total disability rating for compensation based on individual unemployability for a service-connected disability ..." https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-323.151 (effective Oct 27, 2023)
- 323.152(A)(2)(a): "The reduction shall equal the product obtained by multiplying fifty thousand dollars of the true value of the property in money, as adjusted under division (A)(1)(d)". "The reduction applies to only one homestead owned and occupied by a disabled veteran." https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-323.152 (page shows effective Sept 23, 2026, HB 479; content change unknown).
- Dollar figures (Ohio Dept of Taxation FAQ): disabled veteran $58,000 (TY2025), $59,400 (TY2026). "There are no income requirements for those applying under the enhanced homestead exemption for service-connected disabled veterans and their surviving spouses..." https://tax.ohio.gov/help-center/faqs/real-property-tax-homestead-means-testing (page dated May 15, 2020 on its face but contains 2025-26 figures).
- Form DTE 105I Rev. 10/19: "File with the county auditor on or before December 31." "The applicant must be 100% disabled by or be receiving 100% compensation for service-connected injuries on January 1 of the year for which the exemption is sought." Attach DD-214 and award letter showing 100%; TDIU also needs IU approval document. https://tax.ohio.gov/static/forms/real_property/dte_105i_fi.pdf
- Surviving spouse: 323.152(A)(2)(b), "the reduction shall continue through the tax year in which the surviving spouse dies or remarries."
- Local option (HB 96, 2025): FAQ question 16; county-by-county.
- Correction to hint: the figure is indexed ($58,000/$59,400), not $50,000; TDIU qualifies by statute text.

## Vehicles
- R.C. 4503.41 (last updated July 21, 2025): "any disabled veteran having a service-connected disability either rated or compensated at one hundred per cent by the veterans' administration" ... "not required to pay any registration fee and service fee" ; plates with "veteran" and "international wheelchair symbol"; extra vehicles pay normal fees. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4503.41
- BMV: documents - "Letter from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs stating ... rated or compensated at 100%, dated within the past year"; plates "permit vehicles to use parking spaces marked with the international symbol of access". https://bmv.ohio.gov/military-veterans-with-disabilities-plates.aspx
- Placard: "documented service-connected disability that limits or impairs the ability to walk." https://www.bmv.ohio.gov/military-veteran-removable-windshield-placard.aspx
- R.C. 4507.23(I): 100% rated or compensated veteran gets permit, license, endorsement without fee. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4507.23 (eff. Mar 20, 2026). State ID fee waiver under 4507.50: page text could not be extracted; search snippet only.

## Recreation
- R.C. 1533.12(B)(1) (eff. Mar 20, 2026): "disability that has been determined by the veterans administration to be permanently and totally disabling, who receives a pension or compensation ... honorable discharge ... and every veteran to whom the registrar of motor vehicles has issued a set of license plates under section 4503.41 ... free of charge on an annual, multi-year, or lifetime basis". Hint "100% P&T" is approximately right but the text says permanently and totally disabling, or the plate holder route. Form DNR 9032-B (5-year approval, certified by DVS Cleveland, email liaisonoffice@dvs.ohio.gov).
- ODNR camping page: "Honorably discharged veteran who has Ohio residency, and ... Former Prisoner of War [or] Permanently and totally disabled veteran who is receiving compensation from the Department of Veterans of Affairs"; apply at reserveohio.com. Also 10% military discount (listed for active/retired military). https://ohiodnr.gov/discover-and-learn/safety-conservation/about-ODNR/division-parks-watercraft/stay-overnight/state-parks-camping-discounts
- No general state park entry fee found; free camping pass is the park benefit.

## Education
- WOS application (2027-28): "'Disabled' means having a sixty per cent or greater service-connected disability or receiving benefits for permanent and total nonservice-connected disability"; child "sixteenth but not ... twenty-fifth birthday"; deadline "May 15, 2027"; FAFSA required. R.C. 5910.04: "exemption from the payment of one hundred per cent of the general and instructional fees" (reducible if funds short). https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/highered.ohio.gov/sgs/wos/WOS-Application.pdf ; https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5910.04

## Income tax
- R.C. 5747.01(A)(23): "Deduct ... amounts received by the taxpayer as retired personnel pay for service in the uniformed services or reserve components thereof, or the national guard, or received by the surviving spouse or former spouse ... under the survivor benefit plan". (A)(21): military pay for active duty. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5747.01 (eff. Mar 5, 2026)
- VA compensation: inferred from federal-AGI starting point; no explicit state page found (needs_verification true).

## Employment
- R.C. 124.23(C)(1): "additional credit of twenty per cent of the person's total grade given in the examination" for honorably discharged uniformed-service veterans who file a certificate. (C)(2) 15% for reservists. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-124.23 (eff. Mar 20, 2025)
