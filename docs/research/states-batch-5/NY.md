# New York (NY) verified 2026-10-17

Handoff hints corrected: Lifetime Liberty Pass no longer needs any disability rating (parks.ny.gov); VTA is for 4+ years active duty or combat service, no combat requirement since 2025; MERIT scholarship not found on official pages (replaced by Regents Award, 40%, $450/yr); DEC reduced-fee needs 40%; new 458-a(11) permanent and total exemption added.

- Alt Vets exemption: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPT/458-A "an additional amount equal to 50% of the disability rating"; caps $12,000 / $8,000 / $40,000 x equalization rate; local laws may lower or raise; filed "on or before the appropriate taxable status date"; NYC March 15. Revised 2026-06-05 per page.
- P&T exemption: https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/property/exemption/altvetoverview.htm "based on taxable status dates occurring on or after October 1, 2026"; "Updated: October 9, 2026". Statute 458-a(11): "permanently and totally disabled as a result of military service". Pending bills (A10839, S8958A) not confirmed as law.
- Cold War: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPT/458-B 10%/$8,000 or 15%/$12,000; "fifty percent of the Cold War veteran disability rating" cap $40,000.
- Fee-exempt registration: https://dmv.ny.gov/more-info/information-for-military-and-veterans "This exemption can only be used for one vehicle." No update date.
- Disabled Veteran plate: https://dmv.ny.gov/plates/disabled-veteran $28.75, $0 renewal, CP-30 by mail.
- Thruway E-ZPass: https://www.thruway.ny.gov/ezpass/veterans.html
- Hunting/fishing: https://veterans.ny.gov/node/616 "Veterans with service-related disability of 40 percent or more"; DEC page (dec.ny.gov/places-to-go/outdoor-recreation-discounts) "Resident veterans with a service-related disability of 40% or more can get a Sportsman license."
- Liberty Pass: https://parks.ny.gov/visit/passes/lifetime-liberty-pass "NY State residents who are Veterans or Gold Star parents, spouses, or children"
- VTA: https://hesc.ny.gov/vta ; Regents Award: https://hesc.ny.gov/regents (40%, $450, open year-round)
- Income tax: https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/file/military_page.htm "Military pension payments ... are totally exempt." Updated June 4, 2026.
- Civil Service: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CVS/85 10 points original / 5 promotion, "rated at ten per centum or more"; https://veterans.ny.gov/additional-civil-service-credits
