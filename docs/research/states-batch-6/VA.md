# Virginia (VA) verified research, 2026-10-18

Agency: Virginia Department of Veterans Services, https://www.dvs.virginia.gov/ (site blocked automated fetch of its tax-exemption page; statutes used instead).

## Benefits (all quotes from official Code of Virginia pages, law.lis.virginia.gov, current as of 2026-10-18 unless noted)
- Real estate: 58.1-3219.5: "100 percent service-connected, permanent, and total disability, and who occupies the real property as his principal place of residence." Surviving spouse: "so long as the death of the veteran occurs on or after January 1, 2011, and the surviving spouse does not remarry." Land up to one acre. 58.1-3219.6: affidavit to the commissioner of the revenue; refile only if residence changes.
- Const. Art. X Sec. 6-A(b): General Assembly "may exempt" surviving spouse of a member who "died in the line of duty with a Line of Duty determination" (2024 referendum). Implementing statute not confirmed.
- Vehicle personal property tax 58.1-3668: "one motor vehicle ... rated ... 100 percent service-connected, permanent, and total"; "shall not be available for his surviving spouse"; since Jan 1, 2021.
- Sales and use tax (DMV page): 100% service-connected, permanent and total; one vehicle; SUT3 and VA letter.
- 46.2-739 (2026 updated, amended by 2026 c. 39): DV plates for veterans "certified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to have a service-connected disability"; subsection C waives annual registration fee for one vehicle for "any disabled veteran ... who has provided certification that such disability is service related". DMV VLIC-4.211 revised 07/01/2026: first plate set no fee. DMV public plate page (https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/vehicles/license-plates/search/disabled-veteran) still shows older $10 fee / limb-loss-blind-P&T free categories: conflict.
- DV disabled parking plates: 46.2-739(B), physician-type certification.
- 29.1-302: lifetime license: P&T no cost; 70%+ $50; 50%+ $75; 30%+ $100; no trout-stocked waters.
- 10.1-202.2: passport for "service-connected disability rating of 100 percent": free entry, 50% off camping etc.
- 23.1-608 (last amended 2025 c. 384): spouse or child 16-29 of veteran "totally and permanently disabled or at least 90 percent permanently disabled"; tuition and mandatory fees waived; domicile/5-year rule.
- Income tax 58.1-322.02: military benefits subtraction up to $40,000 tax year 2025 and later; "any age" for later years (age 55 applied to earlier). https://www.tax.virginia.gov/military-benefits-faq
- Employment 2.2-2903: 5% exam bonus; disabled veteran 10%.
