# Texas (verified 2026-10-18)

None of the pages below showed a "last updated" date unless noted.

## Property tax
- Partial exemption, Tax Code 11.22 (https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TX/htm/TX.11.htm). Statute schedule: $5,000 at 10% but less than 30%; $7,500 at 30% to <50%; $10,000 at 50% to <70%; $12,000 at 70% and over. Subsec (b): $12,000 if "65 years of age or older and has a disability rating of at least 10 percent", "totally blind in one or both eyes", or "has lost the use of one or more limbs." (f): one property only. (g): must be a Texas resident. Surviving spouse (c): amount at death while unmarried. Comptroller FAQ (https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/exemptions/disabledvet-faq.php): "The deadline to apply for an exemption is April 30"; up to five years after the delinquency date; Form 50-135 to the appraisal district.
- 100% total homestead, 11.131: "awarded ... 100 percent disability compensation due to a service-connected disability and a rating of 100 percent disabled or of individual unemployability is entitled to an exemption from taxation of the total appraised value of the veteran's residence homestead." No "permanent and total" language. Comptroller 100% FAQ (https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/exemptions/disabledvet-100-faq.php): "either 100 percent disabled or is unemployable"; Form 50-114; April 30; five years after delinquency; spouse "does not qualify if the surviving spouse has remarried." Tax Code 11.43(c) text (HB 2508 enrolled): once allowed, need not be claimed again, but chief appraiser may require new application.
- Donated homestead 11.132: exemption percent equals rating, veteran under 100%, charity-donated at no cost or at most 50% of market value; spouse keeps if unmarried.
- NEW 2025: HJR 133 (Prop 7) listed "Adopted" for 11/4/2025 by the Legislative Reference Library (https://lrl.texas.gov/legis/ConstAmends/results.cfm?electionDate=2025-11-04). HB 2508 adds Tax Code 11.136: total residence homestead exemption for unmarried surviving spouse of a veteran who died from a PACT Act presumptive condition "regardless of the veteran's disability rating at the time of the veteran's death" (https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB02508F.htm). Comptroller exemptions page now lists 11.136 (https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/exemptions/).
- Bills adding a homestead break for 10-99% ratings (HB 2032, SB 1126, HJR 19) appeared only as filed/introduced in my searches. I found no evidence they passed.

## Vehicle
- Transportation Code 504.202 (https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TN/htm/TN.504.htm): "at least a 50 percent service-connected disability" or "a 40 percent service-connected disability because of the amputation of a lower extremity"; receives compensation; may register "one vehicle without payment of any fee ... except the fee for the license plates"; vehicle owned by the person, 18,000 lbs or less; "The fee for the first set of license plates is $3. There is no fee for each additional set"; (g) a person who gets ISA-symbol plates under (b-1) may get a placard under 681.004 for each set.
- TxDMV page (https://www.txdmv.gov/motorists/disabled-parking-placards-plates): "Disabled veteran license plates that do not include the ISA do not authorize the owner to park in a disabled parking space." "Not all disabilities that qualify a veteran for disabled veteran license plates will qualify a veteran for a disabled veteran license plate featuring the ISA." Form VTR-615 to the county tax office. The page lists no fee.
- Driver license, Transportation Code 521.426 (https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/TN/htm/TN.521.htm): exempt from fees if "honorably discharged", "service-related disability of at least 60 percent", and "receives compensation".

## Recreation
- TPWD Super Combo (https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/licenses/combination-hunting-fishing/disabled-veteran-super-combo-hunting-and-all-water-fishing-package): "loss of the use of a foot or leg, or a disability rating of 50% or more, and who is receiving compensation"; resident or non-resident; proof within the last 12 months; in person at a retailer; fee Free; Duck Stamp not included.
- TPWD Parklands Passports (https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/park-information/passes/texas-parklands-passports): Disabled Veterans: "60 percent or more service-connected disability or loss of lower extremity. A companion gets free entry." Veterans passport: any "honorably discharged" veteran, pass holder only.

## Education
- TVC Hazlewood page (https://tvc.texas.gov/education/hazlewood-act/): "up to 150 hours of tuition exemption, including most fee charges"; 181 days; honorable or general; Texas at entry; current Texas residence; Legacy child 25 or younger, veteran must reside in Texas. Spouse/dependent category: "100%, total and permanent, service-connected disabled", "rating for individual unemployability"; "A veteran who is totally and permanently disabled and individually unemployable is not required to maintain Texas residency".

## Income tax
- Texas Constitution Art. VIII Sec. 24-a (added Nov. 5, 2019): "The legislature may not impose a tax on the net incomes of individuals..." (https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/docviewer/?docName=CN.8.24-a.htm).

## Employment
- Government Code 657.003 (https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.657.htm): disabled veteran first priority, then veteran; "service credit of 10 points" and "A veteran with a disability is entitled to ... five additional points." 657.002(5): spouse of a veteran with a 70% or IU rating, if the spouse is the primary household income source, also qualifies. 657.0047 interview requirement.

## Other
- State Veterans Homes (https://www.glo.texas.gov/veterans/texas-state-veterans-homes): "Veterans with a 70% or higher service-related disability can generally live in our homes for free."
- VLB land loan (https://www.glo.texas.gov/veterans/land-sale/land-loans): up to $200,000, 5% down (older TVC PDF said $150,000, now stale).
