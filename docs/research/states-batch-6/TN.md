# Tennessee research narrative (verified 2026-10-18)

Agency: Tennessee Department of Veterans Services, https://www.tn.gov/veteran.html

## Property tax relief (disabled veterans)
- Comptroller 2026 brochure (https://comptroller.tn.gov/content/dam/cot/pa/documents/tax-relief/TaxReliefBrochure.pdf): "$175,000 Maximum market value on which tax relief is calculated"; "You must own your home and use it as your primary residence"; "You must complete a 2026 F-16"; qualifying: paraplegia/paralysis/loss or loss of use of two or more limbs/legal blindness, 100% permanent total from POW service, or "service-connected permanent and total disability or disabilities, as determined by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs." Apply: "Contact your county trustee... If your property is within city limits, you may also contact your city collecting official." Deadline: "35 days after the delinquency date. Taxes must also be paid by this date." Reimbursement program. No income limit shown for veteran categories.
- tn.gov veterans page (same $175,000 cap): https://www.tn.gov/veteran/veteran-benefits/tn-state-benefits/homeowners/property-tax-relief-for-disabled-veterans.html
- Comptroller OREA March 2026 report: bills to raise cap to $200,000/$250,000 not funded.
## Surviving spouse
- Same brochure: F-16S, death certificate, not remarried, veteran met disability requirements or death was service-connected combat-related. $175,000. The tn.gov spouse page still says $100,000 (stale): https://www.tn.gov/veteran/veteran-benefits/tn-state-benefits/homeowners/property-tax-relief-for-surviving-spouses.html
## County motor vehicle privilege tax
- "Any disabled veteran who has a 100 percent permanent, total disability from a service connected cause, or any former prisoner-of-war... shall be exempt" TCA 5-8-102. https://www.tn.gov/veteran/veteran-benefits/tn-state-benefits/motor-services/county-motor-vehicle-privilege-tax.html
## DV plate
- OREA 2026 report (https://comptroller.tn.gov/content/dam/cot/orea/advanced-search/2026/VeteransReport.pdf): loss/permanent loss of use of one or both feet or hands, permanent impairment of vision of both eyes, or 100% permanent and total service-connected; first plate only free. TCA 55-4-256 (statute text only seen on Justia mirror; not cited).
## Placard / decal / parking
- DOR form RV-F1310301 Rev. 07/26: "Effective July 1, 2026... Veterans eligible for a free Disabled Veteran (DV) plate under T.C.A. 55-4-256 may choose a Disabled Veteran placard instead, at no cost." Decal: https://www.tn.gov/veteran/veteran-benefits/tn-state-benefits/motor-services/free-disabled-decal.html
## TWRA license
- tn.gov: "one-time $10 fee to residents of Tennessee who are 100% permanent and totally disabled from service connected cause, or by reason of service in any war are 30% or more disabled." https://www.tn.gov/veteran/veteran-benefits/tn-state-benefits/state-parks/hunting-and-fishing-licenses.html . OREA agrees. "Type 198" appears in the tn.gov SC benefits matrix (Mar 2025). TWRA application page could not be opened (404 on PDF).
## State parks
- https://tnstateparks.com/about/discounts : "Tennessee resident veterans with 100% service-connected disability receive a 50% year-round discount on cabin rentals and campsites"; 25% lodge, 20% golf, 15% gift shops; Veterans Day free night and golf. tn.gov discounted-rates page is older (off-season only, Dec-Feb).
## Education
- OREA: Helping Heroes ($1,000/semester, campaign medals); dependents tuition waiver (POW/MIA/KIA/died on duty, under 23); out-of-state tuition exemption. Veterans' tuition waiver bill not heard in committee.
## Income tax
- DOR Hall tax page: "repealed for tax periods that begin on January 1, 2021, or later." https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/hall-income-tax.html
## Employment
- DOHR Policy 12-007 (Rev. 10/01/2025), TCA 8-30-307: interview guarantee and tie-breaker preference; spouse rule. https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/hr/policy/DOHRPolicy12-007.pdf
