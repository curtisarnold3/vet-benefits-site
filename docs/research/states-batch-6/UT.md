# Utah research narrative (verified 2026-10-18)

Agency: Utah Department of Veterans and Military Affairs, https://veterans.utah.gov/

## Property tax (Veteran Armed Forces Exemption)
- Statute, Utah Code 59-2a-501 (eff. 1/1/2026), https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title59/Chapter2a/C59-2a-S501_2026010120250507.html: "the amount of taxable value of eligible property that is exempt under Subsection (1) is equal to the percentage of disability described in the statement of disability multiplied by the adjusted taxable value limit." Also: "A county shall consider a veteran with a disability to have a 100% disability ... if the United States Department of Veterans Affairs certifies the veteran in the classification of individual unemployability." And "A county may not allow an exemption ... if the percentage of disability listed on the statement of disability is less than 10%." Subsection (3): full taxable value for "the unmarried surviving spouse of a veteran who was killed in action or died in the line of duty" or a minor orphan, and pre-1921 veteran survivors with 10%+.
- Application, 59-2a-502 (eff. 1/1/2026): "shall file, on or before September 1 ... with the county in which the veteran claimant resides on September 1." Needs discharge certificate and a statement of disability; no re-filing unless rating/ownership/occupancy changes (6). Pre-purchase filing allowed for a "qualifying disabled veteran claimant" (5). Deadline extensions in (3). URL: https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title59/Chapter2a/C59-2a-S502_2026010120250507.html. No page date shown.
- Definitions, 59-2a-101 (eff. 5/6/2026, amended Ch. 299, 2026 GS): "Eligible property" = primary residence (including one not occupied because of inpatient admission) or personal-use tangible personal property not used in a business. "Veteran with a disability" = acquired disability "in the line of duty" in an active or reserve component "as determined by a military entity" (VA counts). "Qualifying disabled veteran claimant" = "100% service-connected disability rating ... that is permanent and total." Adjusted taxable value limit was $479,504 in 2023, indexed by CPI each year. https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title59/Chapter2a/C59-2a-S101_2026050620260506.html
- Dollar cap conflict: Tax Commission Pub 36, Rev. 9/26 (watermarked DRAFT pending public meeting): "The exemption is up to $535,459 of taxable value of a residence ..." https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/pubs/pub-36.pdf. UDVMA benefit booklet (PDF dated Aug 2026): "up to $505,548" (stale; 10% = $50,554). Search snippets of other (non-official) pages gave $521,620 for 2025; not used. A Duchesne County .gov page was reported as showing $535,459 for 2026 but I could not open it.
- H.B. 124 (2026 GS, Veteran Armed Forces Property Tax Exemption Amendments) would change reapplication rules. Could not confirm outcome; le.utah.gov still lists only the 1/1/2026 version of 59-2a-502.

## Vehicle
- DMV Disabled Veteran plate, https://dmv.utah.gov/plates/license-plates/disabledveteran/ (no date): "This license plate recognizes Utah resident disabled veterans for their military service." Requires "Letter from Utah Department of Veterans' and Military Affairs or other United States military entity." Fees $0 initial/annual contribution; standard plate $8.25; special group $5.00; certificate $4.00. "It does not provide handicapped parking space privileges, a disabled windshield placard must be obtained separately." No threshold or plate count stated.
- DMV registration fee page: no veteran fee exception. Purple Heart plates are exempt from the $2.50 driver education fee and $1.00 uninsured motorist fee.
- Personal vehicles can receive the property tax exemption (see above).

## Recreation
- DWR, https://wildlife.utah.gov/accessibility: "Discounted hunting, fishing or combination license for resident veterans with disabilities: Service-connected disability of 20 percent or more." R657-12-10(1)(a): "A resident who has a service-connected disability of 20% or more may purchase a discounted 365-day ... license." Fee page https://wildlife.utah.gov/fees (updated Oct 8, 2026): fishing $12.00, hunting $25.50, combination $28.50 vs $40/$40/$44 resident.
- State Parks, https://stateparks.utah.gov/park-passes/: "Must be a Utah resident, honorably discharged from military service, and have a documented service-connected disability." Free; veteran plus up to seven guests in the same vehicle; system under digital upgrade in 2026. The department's Aug 2026 booklet says a lifetime pass is pending federal approval; benefits letter remains valid.

## Education
- Purple Heart waiver, Utah Code 53H-11-303 (eff. 5/6/2026): "The total amount of all graduate tuition waived for a Purple Heart recipient may not exceed $15,000." Undergraduate tuition waived; "The waiver in this section does not apply to fees." https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title53H/Chapter11/C53H-11-S303_2026050620260506.pdf
- Veterans Tuition Gap Program 53H-11-413; Lundell 53H-11-308 ("killed while serving on state or federal active duty ... on or after September 11, 2001"; "does not apply to fees, books, or housing expenses"); resident status 53H-11-202(8)(c)-(d). https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title53H/Chapter11/C53H-11_2025101420251206.pdf
- UDVMA 2025 Legislative Wrap-up: H.B. 142 "removes the residency requirement for in-state tuition at a state institution of higher education." https://veterans.utah.gov/news/2025-utah-legislative-wrap-up/

## Income tax
- Utah has a state income tax. 2025 TC-40 Instructions (PDF modified 1/5/2026), https://files.tax.utah.gov/tax/forms/current/tc-40inst.pdf: "(AJ) Military Retirement Credit (UCA 59-10-1043) ... You may qualify for this credit if you ... received taxable military retirement pay." Worksheet: "Multiply line 1 by 0.045." Cannot claim with Retirement Credit (code 18). Statute text (version eff. 3/23/2022 shown on le.utah.gov): credit = rate in 59-10-104(2) times military retirement pay included in federal AGI; no carryover. UDVMA booklet calls it an "exclude" - the official form shows a credit.
- VA compensation: TC-40 line 4 starts from "federal adjusted gross income." No Utah page found naming VA compensation. Flagged needs_verification.

## Employment
- Utah Code 71A-2-102 (eff. 5/3/2023): "(a) 5% of the total possible score, if the preference eligible is a veteran or service member; (b) 10% ... if ... a veteran or service member with a disability or a purple heart recipient." https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title71A/Chapter2/C71A-2-S102_2023050320230503.pdf. DHRM, https://dhrm.utah.gov/hiring-pathways/veterans/ (no date): "A veteran or service member with a disability, regardless of the percentage of disability"; "Ten points or ten percent of the total possible score (whichever is greater)."

## Other
- State Veterans Homes, https://veterans.utah.gov/veterans-homes: "There is no cost for veterans with a 70% or higher service-connected disability (SCD)."
