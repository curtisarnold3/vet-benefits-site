## Property tax credit (Wis. Stat. 71.07(6e))
- Source: https://dva.wi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/WDVA_B0106_WI_Veterans_Property_Tax_Credit.pdf (WDVA B0106, 10/25). Statute: https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/document/statutes/71.07(6e) (2023-24 statutes through 2025 Act 247, published 10-1-26). WDVA page: https://dva.wi.gov/services/housing-and-financial-services/property-tax-credit/
- Quote: eligible veteran "Has either a service-connected disability rating of 100 percent under 38 USC 1114 or 1134 or a 100 percent disability rating based on individual unemployability." Credit is "a refundable property tax credit for the primary residence (in-state) and up to one acre of land via the state income tax form."
- Rating must be held "for the first year the property tax credit will be claimed, AND in each year in which credit is claimed." Not required to be P&T. Prestabilization/hospitalization/convalescent and 38 USC 1151 ratings do not count.
- Surviving spouse: unremarried; veteran died on active duty, or had 100%/IU and was WI resident at death, or Guard/reserve line-of-duty death, or spouse receives DIC. Remarriage ends it.
- Apply: WDVA certification (CVSO, or WDVA 2097 to WDVA, Attn VBRC-PTC Application, PO Box 7843, Madison WI 53707), then claim with income tax return to Dept. of Revenue; claim must be within 71.75(2) time. Certification not repeated yearly unless status changes.
- Statute 71.07(6e)(c)2: no credit if you also claim the homestead credit or farmland/other claim for the same year (subch. VIII/IX, sub. (9)).
- 2025 Act 118 appears in statute notes only as repealing other unrelated credits; no change to 6e found. The 2023 bills (AB 102/SB 102) to lower to 70% were not found enacted.

## DNR recreation benefits
- Source: https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/permits/veterans.html ; https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/permits/disabled.html ; https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/outdoor-rec-and-sports/park-pass/ ; Wis. Stat. 29.236 and 27.01(7)(c)1m, (8)(bn)1.
- Recreation card: 50% or TDIU, $7 annual; small game + fishing + park/trail admission. Statute 29.236: "disability rating that is 50 percent or greater ... or ... individually unemployable."
- Fishing license: DNR "veterans who are receiving Veteran's Disability Compensation for a disability of 70 percent or more ... or ... individually unemployable"; $3.
- Free park/trail pass: 70% or TDIU (statute) or former POW; WDVA page: "Free Park Admission & Trail Pass: ... 70% or greater." CVSO certifies form 2300-123.
- Spring turkey authorization 50%, $10.
- DNR resident license price list (https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/GoWild/resident.html) shows Fishing, Veteran/Disabled 3.00.

## Wisconsin GI Bill
- Source: https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/education/wi-gi-bill/ ; brochure B0104 (07/26) https://dva.wi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/WI_GI_Bill_Brochure_2026.pdf
- Quote: spouse/child may use it "if the veteran has been awarded a service connected disability rating of at least 30% by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or died in the line of duty or as a result of a service connected disability." Child at least 17, under 26. 128 credits or 8 semesters, whichever is longer.
