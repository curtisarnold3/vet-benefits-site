# Wyoming (WY) narrative, verified 2026-10-18

Statute text read from official wyoleg.gov title PDFs (no page date): https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title19.pdf, title23.pdf, title31.pdf, title39.pdf

1. Property tax, W.S. 39-13-105 (title39.pdf). Quotes: "A disabled veteran with a compensable service connected disability certified by the veterans administration or a branch of the armed forces"; "bona fide Wyoming residents for at least three (3) years at the time of claiming"; "limited to an annual exemption of six thousand dollars ($6,000.00) of assessed value"; "file a sworn claim on or before the fourth Monday in May with the county assessor"; "the exemption for real property shall only apply to the principal residence"; "not more than one (1) county". Subsequent years: claimant "contacts the assessor's office by telephone, mail or other communication method on or before the fourth Monday in May". Surviving spouse: 39-13-105(a)(v),(h). County practice (Laramie County, https://www.laramiecountywy.gov/Resident-Services/Property-Information/Veterans-Exemptions, no update date): in-person first claim, about $424 savings, vehicle fees reduced by 6000 assessed dollars (about $90), cap $180, leased vehicles do not qualify. Flat amount, no tier for 100%/P&T/TDIU.
2. 2026 HB0068 (full exemption of home plus up to 10 acres for 100% P&T veterans, first applying tax year 2027): digest https://www.wyoleg.gov/2026/Digest/HB0068.pdf shows committee "Do Pass" 2/18/2026, then "2/23/2026 H COW:H Did not consider for COW". Tracker (wyotax.org 3/9/26) lists it as monitor with no later action. Not law. HB0067 (1-year residency, more categories) failed COW 24-25 on 2/19/2026 per same tracker.
3. Vehicle: W.S. 31-3-101(b)(xv): "A disabled veteran who receives fifty percent (50%) or more service connected disability compensation ... is exempt from the fees imposed under subsection (a) ... for one (1) vehicle". 31-2-215 plates; application annually to county treasurer, 30 days before registration month. WYDOT page https://dot.state.wy.us/home/titles_plates_registration/specialty_plates/Disabled_Vet.html: "cannot be used to park in handicapped parking spaces in Wyoming"; no update date.
4. Game and Fish form (REV 09/26) https://wgfd.wyo.gov/media/34070/download: 23-2-201(f) lifetime fishing license free at 50%+ compensation; 23-1-705(d) "lifetime game bird, small game and fish licenses to any disabled veteran who is one hundred percent (100%) disabled as determined by the United States Department of Veteran Affairs ... valid so long as the licensee remains a Wyoming resident"; Reg Ch. 44 s.14(d) "One hundred percent (100%) disabled does not refer to disability compensation"; Disabled Hunter Permit at least 70% (Reg Ch. 35 s.5(f)); Season Extension Permit 100% (Ch. 35 s.10(a)(ii)).
5. State parks https://wyoparks.wyo.gov/index.php/permits-reservations/permits-fees: "The Lifetime Veteran's Pass allows free day use and camping for life"; "available to any Wyoming resident veteran who is 50% or more service-connected disabled". No update date (2025 application).
6. Tuition W.S. 19-14-106(f): "free tuition for up to a total of eight (8) semesters ... University of Wyoming or any Wyoming community college"; eight academic years limit; 2.0 GPA (k). Overseas combat veteran defined (a)(v). Community College Commission page https://communitycolleges.wy.edu/veteran-tuition-benefit says post-2018 participants get tuition only; no update date.
7. Employment W.S. 19-14-102(c): "five percent (5%) advantage over any nonveteran. A veteran that has a service connected disability of ten percent (10%) or more shall be given a ten percent (10%) advantage"; applicant honorably discharged, 1 year Wyoming resident; not for veterans currently employed by a public department. UW page https://www.uwyo.edu/hr/talent-acquisition/veteran-preference-info.html confirms interview preference.
8. Income tax: https://www.wyo.gov/about-wyoming "Unlike other states, Wyoming does not possess an individual or corporate income tax." Page is dated/undated; no official page found specifically about military retirement.
9. Veterans' Home: https://health.wyo.gov/aging/veteranshome/admissions/ (no update date, footer 2026).
