# Washington (WA) - verified 2026-10-18

Agency: Washington State Department of Veterans Affairs, https://dva.wa.gov

Key correction to hint: property tax threshold is NOT simply 80%. RCW 84.36.381 (amended 2025 c 200 and 2026 c 163, applying to taxes levied for collection in 2027+) reads "A combined service-connected evaluation rating of 40 percent or higher" or "A total disability rating for a service-connected disability without regard to evaluation percent". DOR program page still says "a service-connected evaluation of at least 80%" or compensation "at the 100% rate". DOR notice (Issue Date July 18, 2025, EHB 1106): rating "decreased from 80% or higher to 40% or higher" beginning with taxes due in 2027. Surviving spouse/partner qualifies at 57+. Own and occupy as principal residence. Apply via county assessor; eligibility by Dec. 31 of assessment year. No last-updated dates on DOR/WDVA pages.

- https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/property-tax/property-tax-exemption-seniors-people-retired-due-disability-and-veterans-disabilities
- https://dor.wa.gov/forms-publications/publications-subject/special-notices/legislative-changes-property-tax-administration-3
- Income: RCW 84.36.383 thresholds = greater of prior or 60/70/80% of county median household income; disability compensation (38 CFR 3.4) excluded from disposable income. https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=84.36.383
- Exemption amounts: threshold 1: "greater of $80,000 or 80 percent of the valuation"; threshold 2: "greater of $70,000 or 45 percent of the valuation" (capped $200,000 valuation); threshold 3 exempt from excess levies and state levy.

Plates: RCW 46.18.235 "receiving service-connected compensation at the one hundred percent rate"; one vehicle; no license/plate fees or excise taxes. DOL: "exempt from license fees on 1 vehicle per year"; "This plate doesn't give you disabled parking privileges." https://dol.wa.gov/vehicles-and-boats/license-plates/get-custom-plates/special-design-plates/disabled-american-veteran
Parking permit: "70 percent disability rating or higher" and "You use a service animal" (unclear if both required). Free metered street parking. https://dol.wa.gov/driver-licenses-and-permits/get-or-renew-disabled-parking-permits/disabled-veteran-parking

Parks: RCW 79A.05.065(3) "veteran and has a service-connected disability of at least 30 percent"; lifetime pass at no cost, free campsite and admission, not valid at concessionaire facilities. WDVA: Oct. 1 passholders pay reservation/change fees. https://dva.wa.gov/veterans-service-members-and-their-families/veterans-benefits/free-or-reduced-rate-passes-and-tax-exemptions
Hunting/fishing: RCW 77.32.480 "30 percent or more service-connected disability"; combination fishing at "$6.90" (statute text; fee may have changed since); hunting at youth fee. WDVA notes eligibility "will change as a result of HB 2014" (qualifying discharge wording). WDFW page says honorable discharge, 30%+ or 65+. https://wdfw.wa.gov/accessibility/eligibility-requirements-veterans-reduced-fees

Education: RCW 28B.15.621 "determined to be one hundred percent disabled by the federal department of veterans affairs"; children 17-26; 250 quarter credits (WDVA page says 200); $500 stipend. Veteran waiver: school-by-school, no threshold on WDVA page. https://dva.wa.gov/veterans-their-families/veterans-benefits/education-and-training/washington-tuition-waiver

Income tax: "Washington does not currently have an individual income tax." 9.9% over $1M from Jan. 1, 2028 (SB 6346). https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/income-tax. Capital gains excise tax exists (DOR; not fetched directly).

Employment: RCW 41.04.010 10% (war/armed conflict, not retired) / 5% (others or retirees); no disability-based points in text fetched. https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=41.04.010
