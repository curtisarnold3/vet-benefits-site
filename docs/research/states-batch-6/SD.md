# South Dakota (SD) verified 2026-10-18

Agency: SD Department of Veterans Affairs, https://vetaffairs.sd.gov/ (its benefits sub-pages loaded; /benefits/state.aspx shows "Coming Soon").

## Property tax
- SDCL 10-4-40 (https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/10-4-40; source note lists SL 2026 ch 40): "Two hundred thousand dollars of the full and true value of the total amount of a dwelling ... classified as owner-occupied ... which is owned and occupied by a veteran who is rated as permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability, is exempt from property taxation." Late filers may petition county commissioners; pending P&T claim: refund back to claim effective date, up to 4 previous years. Retained until transfer, non-occupancy, or change in use. Hint said $150,000/"full" for paraplegic and confusion: $200,000 is current.
- DOR brochure (Dec 2025) https://dor.sd.gov/media/jzbhdtzr/disabled-veteran-property-tax-exemption-brochure.pdf: eligibility = P&T service-connected, owned and occupied, owner-occupied classification; "Application must be submitted to your county director of equalization on or before November 1." Proof from VA (Sioux Falls RO). "Dwelling is defined as the home, garage, and the lot." No annual reapplication.
- SDCL 10-4-41 surviving spouse: $200,000; spouse of P&T veteran, or spouse receiving DIC; ends on remarriage.
- SDCL 10-4-24.10 paraplegic veteran / loss of use of both lower extremities: dwelling "is exempt from taxation" (full); DOR overview: Nov 1 to director of equalization, injury need not be service related. Separate income-based paraplegic reduction (10-4-24.11 to .13) goes to county treasurer by April 1.
- SDCL 10-13-39 owner-occupied classification: one principal residence only (amended SL 2026 ch 46).

## Vehicle
- SDCL 32-5-157(2): disabled veteran plate requires rated "in receipt of a statutory benefit for loss or loss of use of one or more extremities, ... receives a veteran's allotment for a total service-connected disability, or ... K Award. The disability must have been incurred while serving on active duty during a time of war or while participating in a military mission involving armed conflict."
- SDCL 32-5-160: $10 initial, $10 annual renewal, "not subject to the annual registration fees"; maximum two sets; DOR page (https://dor.sd.gov/individuals/motor-vehicle/cars-trucks-vans/specialty-military-plates/) adds $12 mailing fee, form MV-4000. SDCL 32-5-157.1 (2024): may use standard county plates instead and still pay 32-5-160 fees. Parking placard not researched (DOR disabled parking page not read).

## Recreation
- SDCL 41-6-10.2: reduced fee if resident "Receives a veterans allotment for a forty percent or more disability ... or has received the ... K Award"; 4-year validity; $10 per GFP license page https://gfp.sd.gov/license-types/ and application revised Nov 2025 (https://gfp.sd.gov/UserDocs/nav/Disabled_Vet_reduced_fee_app.pdf). Replaces resident small game and fishing licenses only (big game still purchased).
- Lifetime park entrance: https://gfp.sd.gov/vet-pow/ "100% total service-connected disability" or K award, POW, Purple Heart; "50% discount on camping fees"; apply online at Go Outdoors SD; cites SDCL 41-17-13.4 (text not read). SDDVA park page says "100 percent total and permanent disabled or is a K Award recipient" (slight wording difference; used 100%).
- Special hunts: pheasant hunt page https://gfp.sd.gov/special-pheasant-hunt/ (20% or more); 2025 deer hunt application (Aug 2025) 20% or more, K award, or Purple Heart; sponsored by Mule Deer Foundation, deadline July 31, 2025; 2026 details not confirmed.

## Education
- SDCL 13-55-2: quoted limits above; "No tuition benefit may be provided by the state under this section until the applicant has applied all federal tuition benefits." Disability-only veterans need "ten percent or greater". 13-55-3 timing.
- SDCL 13-55-6 (child of resident who died in service, under 25), 13-55-10 (National Guard death or total and permanent disability, child under 25 or spouse).

## Employment
- SDCL 3-3-1: "A veteran who has a service-connected disability shall be given a preference over a nondisabled veteran." SDDVA preference page confirms; spouse provisions listed there (unmarried spouse of veteran who died in service or of service-connected cause; spouse of disabled veteran unable to use preference). No points system found.

## Income tax
- DOR sales/use tax guide July 2026: "South Dakota does not have a corporate, unitary, or personal income tax". DOR individual tax page lists no income tax filing.
