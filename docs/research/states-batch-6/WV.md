# West Virginia - verified 2026-10-18 (official sources only)

Agency: WV Department of Veterans Assistance (https://veterans.wv.gov/). The handout hint pages (veterans.wv.gov/benefits/Pages/...) 404; current pages are under /benefits-assistance/benefits/.

## Property tax
- DV Real Property Tax Credit. TSD 455 (Rev. January 2026) https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/TSD/tsd455.pdf : "Veteran taxpayers who are 90-100% permanently and totally disabled and owners of a homestead ... may be eligible"; "first half ... paid before October 1 and the second half before April 1"; claimed with DV-1 on the IT-140; need VA rating letter and tax receipts; widow(er) "has not remarried ... not been sold ... not been reclassified"; "must be taken alone" (not with SCTC/HEPTC); homestead "used or occupied by the owner for at least 6 months". DV-1 Rev. 07/2025: "at least ninety percent totally and permanently disabled due solely to service-connected disabilities". WVDVA: credit passed 2023. No TDIU statement found.
- Homestead exemption. W. Va. Code 11-6B-3 https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-6B-3/ : "first $20,000 of assessed value ... owner is sixty-five years of age or older or is certified as being permanently and totally disabled", two consecutive years residency. 11-6B-4: claim "on or before December 1, following the July first assessment day", filed with county assessor; accepted proof includes "a written certification signed by the veterans administration certifying that a person is totally and permanently disabled". WVDVA says "Veterans rated 90-100 percent permanent and disabled ... contact County Assessors' Office". No constitutional amendment/statute for a veteran-specific full exemption confirmed (hint unconfirmed).

## Vehicle
- W. Va. Code 17A-10-8 https://code.wvlegislature.gov/17A-10-8/ : exempt "Any vehicle owned by a disabled veteran as defined by ... Public Law 663 ... 187 ... 77"; "Not more than one vehicle owned by a veteran with a hundred percent total and permanent service-connected disability as certified by the Director of ... Veterans' ... and not used for commercial purposes."
- Plates: WVDVA state benefits page https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-assistance/benefits/state-benefits : "provided without fee to veterans who qualify for VA auto grant benefits or who have a 100 percent permanent and total service-connected disability rating"; other veterans $10 one-time. DMV pages blocked/404 for me; DMV-48-D/DI forms (search summary only) mention $0.50 + $1 fees and physician certification for mobility-impaired parking plates. Unverified.

## Recreation
- Hunting: 2026-27 Hunting and Trapping Regulations Summary (July 2026) https://wvdnr.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Pub_Regs_HuntTrap_202627_DNR_WILD_20260724.pdf : residents "receiving total permanent service connected disability benefits or former prisoners of war ... or resident disabled veterans who qualify under West Virginia Code 17A-10-8 ... may apply for a Disabled Veterans and Former Prisoner of War License (Class V)" (= AB-O-L and DS privileges). Code 20-2-28(g),(h) exempts them from license.
- Fishing: 2025 Fishing Regulations https://wvdnr.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Pub_Regs_Fishing2025_DNR_WILD_Interactive_20250107.pdf : "disabled veterans receiving 100% permanent service-connected disability" exempt; "must carry a card issued by the DNR". 2026 edition not checked.
- State parks: WVDVA page: "10 percent discount on all standard lodge rooms, cabins, and campsites". wvstateparks.com blocked by Cloudflare; not confirmed there.

## Education
- WVDVA education page https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-assistance/benefits/education-benefits : War Orphan Program (tuition/fees waived, up to $1,000/semester, $2,000/yr); in-state rates for nonresident GI Bill users within 3 years; Medal of Honor / Purple Heart tuition waiver; Re-education stipend up to $500/semester. W. Va. Code 18-19-2 and 18-19-3 show "[Repealed]" (2025 HB 3192 repealed art. 19), so War Orphan status needs confirmation. 18B-10-7 waives tuition only for children/spouses of members killed in line of duty (not disabled veterans).

## Income tax
- TSD 443 (Rev. April 2024) https://tax.wv.gov/Documents/TSD/tsd443.pdf : "For tax years beginning on and after January 1, 2019, all military retirement income ... is exempt from WV income tax." IT-140 2025 instructions Line 32 confirm. VA compensation: no explicit official statement found.

## Employment
- W. Va. Code 6-13-2 https://code.wvlegislature.gov/6-13-2/ : 5-Point Preference and 10-Point Compensable Disability Preference (disabled veteran per 6-13-1(e): "has established the present existence of a service-connected disability or is receiving compensation..." or Purple Heart); highest category only; points only on passing score.
