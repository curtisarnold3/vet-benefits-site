# Vermont (VT) research, verified 2026-10-18

Agency: Vermont Office of Veterans Affairs, https://veterans.vermont.gov (no last-updated dates shown on its pages; footers show copyright 2026).

## Property tax
- Statute 32 V.S.A. 3802(11), https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/32/125/03802: "$10,000.00 of appraisal value ... occupied as the established residence of and owned in fee simple by a veteran ... if one or more of them are receiving disability compensation for at least 50 percent disability ... before May 1 of each year, there is filed with the Office of Veterans Affairs". Permanent disability: "only required to be filed ... before May 1 of the first year ... exemption shall remain on the grand list until title to the property is transferred". Surviving spouse: "An unremarried widow or widower of a previously qualified veteran shall be entitled to the exemption ... whether or not the individual is receiving government compensation or pension." Towns may vote to raise to $40,000.
- OVA page https://veterans.vermont.gov/benefits-and-services/veteran-benefits/tax-exemptions-veterans (says 2026 application closed May 1): eligible = 50%+ compensation, non-service-connected pension, permanent medical retirement pay; "owned by the veteran or survivor; and is the primary residence"; "Veterans who are determined to be totally and permanently disabled only have to provide proof of eligibility the first year". Fact sheet FS-1003 (Revised November 2024) says must be requested each year; this conflicts with the statute/OVA on permanent disability, so the statute and OVA page were followed. No official page found for TDIU treatment; TDIU is not 100% schedular and is not named, so unclear.

## Vehicle
- OVA tax exemptions page: waiver of vehicle sales tax, registration and license fees only for disabled veterans whose vehicle was modified and paid for by VA: "All other veterans are not exempt, including veterans who may be 100% disabled." Could not retrieve 23 V.S.A. text (legislature site returned no section text).
- DMV https://dmv.vermont.gov/military/military-license-plates: Disabled Veteran plate requires VG-168 and VS-113 (unless medical filed in last four years). No minimum rating stated. DMV https://dmv.vermont.gov/registrations/license-plates/disabled-plates-placards: one set of plates without additional fees for a person who is blind or has an ambulatory disability; VD-120 placard.

## Recreation
- Fish and Wildlife https://vtfishandwildlife.com/node/340: "is, or ever has been, 60% disabled due to a service connected disability ... may receive a free permanent license, if qualified, upon presentation of a specific letter from the Veterans Administration." Reciprocal non-residents: NH and ME. Exact license types covered not stated on page.
- State Parks https://www.vtstateparks.com/fees: "Green Mountain Passport: For Vermont residents who are 62+ or honorably discharged veterans. Lifetime free day-use entry. Available for $2 at your local Town Clerk's Office." No disability-specific park pass found on official pages.

## Income tax (https://tax.vermont.gov/node/604, no last-updated date)
- Act 71 signed June 25, 2025, effective tax year 2025: military retirement and survivor benefit income exempt at AGI <= $125,000; phases out to $175,000; "No exemption is available" at $175,000+. OVA adds: can elect either the Social Security exemption or this one, not both.
- Veteran Tax Credit: $250 refundable, AGI <= $25,000; "No credit is available for veterans with more than $30,000 of adjusted gross income." Residents and part-year residents.
- VA compensation: no official Vermont page found stating it is not taxed (inference only).

## Employment
- 3 V.S.A. 310(g),(h) (https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/03/013): five points; "10 points" for service-connected disabled veterans, widows/widowers, spouses of totally disabled; "subject to ... 20 V.S.A. 1543". DHR page https://humanresources.vermont.gov/node/279: veteran = at least 90 days, honorable or other acceptable conditions; promotional-only access.
