Chapter 31: Veteran Readiness & Employment

VR&E can pay for a degree, a certification, tools for a trade, or help starting a business, when your service-connected disability gets in the way of work. It can pay more than the GI Bill in some cases, and it does not use up your GI Bill months.

At a glance

WhoVeterans with a service-connected rating who have an "employment handicap" (your disability limits your ability to get or keep suitable work)
Rating needed20% or more with an employment handicap, or 10% with a serious employment handicap (38 CFR 21.40)
Time limitNone if you were discharged on or after Jan 1, 2013. Before that: 12 years from separation or first rating, extendable for a serious employment handicap
FormVA Form 28-1900, online
Official pageva.gov VR&E eligibility

The five tracks

  • Reemployment: return to your old job with accommodations.
  • Rapid Access to Employment: job search help, resume, interviews.
  • Self-Employment: business plan and start-up help.
  • Employment Through Long-Term Services: degree, certificate, apprenticeship, with tuition, books, fees, and a monthly allowance.
  • Independent Living: for veterans who cannot work yet; services to live more independently.

Monthly subsistence allowance (effective Oct 1, 2026)

Full-time school, no dependents: $837.23 a month. With one dependent $1,038.49; two dependents $1,223.79; each additional $89.18. Or, if you are eligible for the Post-9/11 GI Bill, you can choose the GI Bill housing allowance instead, which is usually much higher.

Source: benefits.va.gov subsistence rates (updated Sep 23, 2026).

Traps

  • A counselor decides. Eligibility is just the door. A Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor must agree that your plan is needed and feasible. Come prepared to explain how your disability limits your work.
  • The old 12-year rule still shows up in the regulation (38 CFR 21.41) but the statute (38 U.S.C. 3103(g), 2021) removed it for post-2013 discharges. The statute wins.
  • Keep your GI Bill. Using VR&E does not use up GI Bill months in most cases, but ask the counselor how your plan is charged.

How to apply

  1. Submit VA Form 28-1900 online.
  2. Attend the orientation and the counselor meeting (often by video).
  3. Build the rehabilitation plan together and sign it.
