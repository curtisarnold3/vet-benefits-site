CHAMPVA: health coverage for your spouse and kids

If you are rated permanent and total (P&T), your spouse and dependent children can get VA-paid health coverage. It also covers survivors of veterans who died of a service-connected condition.

At a glance

WhoSpouse or dependent child of a veteran rated P&T for a service-connected disability; or surviving spouse/child of a veteran who died from a service-connected disability or who was P&T at death
Rating needed100% P&T. A 100% rating that is not marked permanent does not qualify. TDIU marked P&T is a total rating; ask VA to confirm your P&T status in writing
Not eligibleAnyone eligible for TRICARE. If the family member has Medicare, they must have Parts A and B; CHAMPVA pays second
FormVA Form 10-10d (plus 10-7959c for other insurance)
Official pageva.gov CHAMPVA

What it costs

  • Deductible: $50 per person, $100 per family, each calendar year (outpatient only).
  • After that, the family pays 25% of the CHAMPVA allowable amount.
  • Catastrophic cap: $3,000 per family per year. After that CHAMPVA pays 100%.
  • Prescriptions through Meds by Mail are free for beneficiaries with no other drug coverage.

Traps

  • P&T is the whole game. Look for the words "permanent and total" or "Dependents' Educational Assistance established" on your rating decision or benefits letter. If they are not there, you are not P&T yet, even at 100%.
  • Children 18 to 23 must send proof of full-time school enrollment to keep coverage.
  • Remarriage of a surviving spouse can end CHAMPVA, with exceptions by age. Needs verification Confirm the current rule with the CHAMPVA office before you count on it.
  • TRICARE wins. If your family qualifies for TRICARE (for example, you are a military retiree), they use TRICARE, not CHAMPVA.

How to apply

  1. Get your P&T letter from VA.gov (download your benefits letter).
  2. Complete VA Form 10-10d online or on paper, one application per family.
  3. Mail to VHA Office of Community Care, CHAMPVA Eligibility, PO Box 137, Spring City, PA 19475, or apply online.

2026 note: VA no longer mails Form 1095-B automatically, and GLP-1 drugs are covered only for FDA-approved uses.
