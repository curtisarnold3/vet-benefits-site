VA home loan funding fee waiver, and property tax breaks

Two of the biggest dollar benefits most rated veterans never add up: the funding fee waiver on a VA loan (often thousands of dollars) and your state property tax exemption.

At a glance

Funding fee waiverYou pay no funding fee on a VA loan if you receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability (any rating 10%+), or you would but you get retired pay instead, or you are a surviving spouse on DIC, or you have a pre-discharge memorandum rating, or an active-duty Purple Heart
What it savesFirst-use purchase with under 5% down: 2.15% of the loan. On a $400,000 loan that is $8,600. Repeat use: 3.3%. Refinance (IRRRL): 0.5%
HowYour Certificate of Eligibility (COE) shows "exempt." Request it online, through your lender, or with VA Form 26-1880. If your rating came through after closing, you can apply for a refund
Property taxSet by each state, usually at 100% P&T, sometimes at 50% or 70%, sometimes any rating. Find your state
Official pageva.gov funding fee

Traps

  • A pending claim at closing: if your rating is granted after you close, ask the lender and VA for a funding fee refund. It is not automatic.
  • Tax deduction (new in 2026): VA announced that funding fees paid in 2026 can be deducted on federal taxes. The legal basis and which tax years it covers should be confirmed with a tax professional. Needs verification
  • Property tax exemptions have deadlines (often mid-year) and residency rules, and some require re-filing each year. Your county assessor is the office that matters.
  • "100% P&T" vs "TDIU": some states count TDIU as 100%, some do not. Colorado does, since 2025. Check your state page.
