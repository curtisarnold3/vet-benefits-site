What "Permanent and Total" means, and how to tell if you have it

P&T is not a rating. It is a finding VA adds to a total rating when it decides your condition will not improve. It unlocks a separate set of benefits for your family and your state.

How to check

  1. Log in to VA.gov and download your Benefit Summary Letter (also called the VA benefits letter).
  2. Look for the line "You are considered to be totally and permanently disabled due solely to your service-connected disabilities" or the words "permanent and total."
  3. On your rating decision, look for "Basic eligibility to Dependents' Educational Assistance is established". That line only appears when P&T is granted.
  4. No "future exam" date listed is a good sign but not proof.

What P&T unlocks

  • CHAMPVA health coverage for spouse and children.
  • Chapter 35 education money for spouse and children.
  • Most state property tax exemptions, plates, and tuition programs at the "100%" tier.
  • No routine re-examinations.
  • Faster Social Security disability processing.
  • Not required for: VA dental (100% or TDIU is enough), commissary access, SMC, or the funding fee waiver.

Traps

  • You can ask. If your conditions are not going to improve (by their nature or by age), you or your representative can ask VA to make a P&T finding. There is no special form; it is a request on a 21-4138 with medical support, or it comes up at the next decision.
  • Mental health ratings are rarely marked permanent early. Let the record build.
  • Asking for an increase after 100% for anything except SMC can reopen the file. Usually not worth it.
