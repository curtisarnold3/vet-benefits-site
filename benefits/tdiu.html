TDIU: paid at 100% when you cannot work

Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability pays the 100% rate to veterans whose service-connected conditions keep them from holding a steady job that supports them, even if the math only reaches 70%.

At a glance

Rating neededOne condition at 60% or more; or a combined 70% with at least one condition at 40% (38 CFR 4.16(a)). Below that, VA can still grant it on an "extra-schedular" basis, but rarely
The real testYou cannot get or keep "substantially gainful" work because of service-connected conditions. Earning under the federal poverty line for one person is "marginal" and does not count against you
PaysSame as 100%: $3,938.58 a month, veteran alone, effective Dec 1, 2025
FormsVA Form 21-8940 (you) and VA Form 21-4192 (your last employer fills it out)
Official pageva.gov individual unemployability

What counts as "one condition" for the 60% / 40% test

The regulation lets you group: both arms or both legs together (with the bilateral factor), conditions from a common cause or a single accident, conditions in one body system, multiple injuries in action, or POW injuries. So two 30% knees can count as a single 51% condition. Check the math in the calculator.

Evidence that moves the needle

  • Your Social Security earnings record (shows the income drop).
  • A doctor's opinion that ties your service-connected conditions, not your age or other problems, to being unable to work.
  • A vocational expert report. VA has no rebuttal expert for these.
  • Statements from former employers or coworkers.

Traps

  • TDIU and SSDI are different programs with different rules and words. Winning one does not win the other. Do not send the SSDI file to VA as-is.
  • Working after TDIU: VA now matches wages with Social Security. If your income goes over the poverty line, VA sends a notice and you have 65 days to return VA Form 21-4140. Ignoring it stops the benefit.
  • TDIU can be marked P&T. When it is, it unlocks CHAMPVA, Chapter 35, and most state 100% benefits. Ask for the P&T finding if your conditions are not expected to improve.
  • You still have to be rated first. TDIU is added to an existing compensation award.
