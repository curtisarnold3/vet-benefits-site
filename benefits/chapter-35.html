Chapter 35: education money for your spouse and kids

Dependents' Educational Assistance (DEA) pays your spouse or child a monthly check for school or training. The trigger is P&T status, not just a 100% rating.

At a glance

WhoSpouse or child of a veteran who is permanently and totally disabled from a service-connected disability, or who died of a service-connected disability, or died on active duty, or is MIA/POW
Rating needed100% P&T. The law says "total disability permanent in nature": rated total for compensation, and expected to last your whole life (38 U.S.C. 3501(a)(8), 38 CFR 3.340(b))
How muchUp to 36 months (45 if the student started before Aug 1, 2018). Full-time rate $1,621 a month starting Oct 1, 2026 ($1,574 for Oct 2025 to Sept 2026)
FormVA Form 22-5490, online
Official pageva.gov Chapter 35

What "100% P&T" actually means

Three different things get called "100%." Only one of them turns on Chapter 35:

  • 100% schedular, temporary or subject to re-exam. VA thinks you might improve. Not P&T. No Chapter 35.
  • 100% schedular, permanent. VA has decided the condition will not improve. P&T. Chapter 35 yes.
  • TDIU (unemployability), paid at the 100% rate. This is a "total" rating. If VA also marks it permanent, it is P&T and Chapter 35 applies. VA's own pages do not spell out the TDIU case in plain words, so get your P&T status confirmed in your benefits letter. Needs verification

How to check: download your VA benefits letter on VA.gov. Look for "permanent and total" or the line "Dependents' Educational Assistance established."

Time limits and ages

  • Children, eligibility on or after Aug 1, 2023: any age, no time limit.
  • Children, eligibility before Aug 1, 2023: generally ages 18 to 26, with extensions for military service and some other cases.
  • Spouses, qualifying event on or after Aug 1, 2023: no time limit.
  • Spouses, before Aug 1, 2023: usually 10 years from the eligibility date (20 years if the service member died on active duty).

2026 to 2027 monthly rates (effective Oct 1, 2026)

Full time$1,621.00
Three-quarter time$1,281.00
Half time$939.00

Apprenticeship/OJT, months 1 to 6$1,029.00

Chapter 35 rates change every October 1. Paid to the student, not the school. The student still pays tuition.

Traps

  • Chapter 35 is not transferred GI Bill. You do not have to give up any of your own benefits. Your family gets it on top of yours.
  • Pick one program at a time. A dependent eligible for both Chapter 35 and the Fry Scholarship must choose for each period of use.
  • High school programs: starting Aug 1, 2026, Chapter 35 no longer pays for secondary (high school) programs. Needs verification

How to apply

  1. Student creates a VA.gov account.
  2. Submit VA Form 22-5490 online.
  3. Give the Certificate of Eligibility to the school's certifying official.
