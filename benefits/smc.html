Special Monthly Compensation (SMC)

SMC is extra pay for losses the normal rating schedule does not cover well: loss of use of a hand or foot, being housebound, or needing another person's help every day. It is the one thing still worth filing for at 100%.

At a glance

WhoVeterans with service-connected disabilities that meet one of the SMC definitions in 38 CFR 3.350
Rating neededSMC-K: any rating, 0% to 100%, as an add-on. SMC-S: a single 100% rating plus more. Aid & Attendance levels: based on need, not percentage
FormsClaimed on the 21-526EZ (or increase). For housebound or aid & attendance, a doctor completes VA Form 21-2680; nursing home residents also use 21-0779
Official pageva.gov SMC rates

Traps

  • "Housebound" has two doors. Most SMC-S awards come from the numbers door: a single 100% plus a separate 60%. You do not have to be stuck at home. Many veterans at 100% qualify and never ask.
  • SMC-K for erectile dysfunction is common as a secondary to diabetes, prostate treatment, or medication side effects. It is paid at 0% for the condition plus the K add-on.
  • A&A needs the 21-2680 filled out by a physician. Bring a plain list of what you cannot do without help.
  • The regulation says exactly "60 percent" for the S add-on, and the extra conditions must be independently ratable at 60 (combined among themselves), separate from the 100% one.
