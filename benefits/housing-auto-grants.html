Adaptive housing grants and the automobile allowance

For veterans with the most serious losses: money to buy or change a home, and a one-time payment toward a vehicle plus adaptive equipment. These key off specific disabilities, not a percentage.

At a glance

SAH grantUp to $126,526 (FY2026). Loss of use of more than one limb, blindness plus a limb, severe burns, or post-9/11 loss of one lower leg with mobility limits. You own or will own the home
SHA grantUp to $25,350 (FY2026). Loss of use of both hands, certain burns, certain respiratory injuries. You or a family member owns the home
TRA grantUp to $50,961 (SAH-eligible) or $9,100 (SHA-eligible) to adapt a family member's home you live in temporarily
HISAHome Improvements and Structural Alterations, a health care benefit: $6,800 lifetime for a service-connected need (or any need if you have a single 50%+ rating), $2,000 for others
Auto allowanceOne-time $27,908.90 (effective Oct 1, 2026) toward a vehicle, paid to the seller, plus VA-paid adaptive equipment. Loss of use of a hand or foot, vision 20/200 or worse, severe burns, or ALS. Ankylosis of a knee or hip gets equipment only
FormsSAH/SHA/TRA: VA Form 26-4555. HISA: VA Form 10-0103 through your VA medical center. Auto: VA Form 21-4502; equipment: VA Form 10-1394

Details that matter

  • Grant maximums reset every October 1. The FY2027 SAH/SHA numbers had not been posted as of Oct 16, 2026. Needs verification
  • You can use SAH/SHA money up to six times over your life, up to the current maximum.
  • TRA grants do not count against your SAH/SHA lifetime maximum.
  • The auto allowance can be paid a second time if 30 years have passed since the first (since Jan 5, 2023).
  • The FY2026 auto allowance (Oct 1, 2025 to Sept 30, 2026) was $27,074.99 per VA's 2026 Federal Benefits guide; VA's rates page now shows only the FY2027 figure. Needs verification
