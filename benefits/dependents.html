Adding dependents to your compensation (30% and up)

At a combined rating of 30% or higher, VA pays extra for your spouse, children, and dependent parents. It is not automatic. You have to tell VA.

At a glance

Rating neededCombined 30% or more
Who countsSpouse (including common-law and same-sex marriages); unmarried children under 18; children 18 to 23 in school full time; children of any age who became permanently disabled before 18; parents who depend on you financially
FormsVA Form 21-686c (spouse, children under 18); add VA Form 21-674 for each child 18 to 23 in school. Both online at VA.gov
Official pageva.gov add or remove a dependent

Amounts in between (40% to 90%) scale up. The calculator applies them for you. These "+" figures are subtracted from VA's published table rows.

The one-year rule

Tell VA within one year of the marriage, birth, adoption, or the rating decision that got you to 30%, and the extra pay goes back to that date. Tell VA later, and it starts only when you tell them (38 CFR 3.401(b)).

  • School-age kids: file the 21-674 before each school year. It lapses when the term ends.
  • Divorce, a child turning 18, a death: you must remove the dependent or VA will create an overpayment and take it back.
  • The first child 18 to 23 in school is priced as the "first child" on VA's table; only additional school-age children get the higher add-on. Needs verification
