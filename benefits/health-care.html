VA health care: priority groups and copays by rating

Almost every veteran with a rating can enroll. Your rating decides your priority group, and your priority group decides what you pay.

At a glance

WhoServed on active duty, not dishonorably discharged. Most who enlisted after Sept 7, 1980 need 24 continuous months or the full call-up period (exceptions for service-connected discharge).
Rating neededNone to apply. 10%+ removes most copays. 50%+ removes all copays.
FormVA Form 10-10EZ, online, by phone (877-222-8387), by mail, or in person
Official pageva.gov/health-care/eligibility

"0% compensable" is rare (VA pays you at 0%, for example for inactive TB). Most 0% ratings are "non-compensable" and land in group 5, 7, or 8 by income. Source: 38 CFR 17.36.

2026 copay amounts (effective January 1, 2026)

  • Outpatient (non-service-connected care, rating under 10%): primary care $15 per visit, specialty care $50, imaging like MRI or CT $50. Labs, x-rays, and preventive tests $0.
  • Urgent care: groups 1 to 5, $0 for the first 3 visits per year then $30; groups 7 and 8, $30 per visit.
  • Medications (30-day supply): tier 1 $5, tier 2 $8, tier 3 $11. Annual cap $700. Free for any service-connected condition at any rating, and free for everything at 50%+.
  • Inpatient (non-service-connected): group 7, $347.20 plus $2 a day for the first 90 days; group 8, $1,736 plus $10 a day.

Copays reset every January 1. Full table: va.gov/health-care/copay-rates.

Traps and tips

  • Combat veterans get a 10-year window. If you served in a combat theater after Sept 11, 2001, you can enroll in group 6 for 10 years from discharge even with no rating (PACT Act). Do not wait.
  • PACT Act toxic exposure lets many veterans enroll without filing a disability claim first (since March 5, 2024).
  • You do not have to report income on the application if you have a compensable rating, but reporting it can only help you.
  • Your priority group updates automatically when your rating changes. Check your group on VA.gov after any new decision.
  • Enrolling also gets you a VHIC (Veteran Health ID Card), which is the easiest proof for commissary and exchange access.

How to apply

  1. Fill out the 10-10EZ online (about 30 minutes).
  2. Or call 877-222-8387, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET.
  3. Or mail the paper form to the Health Eligibility Center, PO Box 5207, Janesville, WI 53547-5207, or bring it to any VA medical center.
