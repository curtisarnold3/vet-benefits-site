Federal hiring preference: 5-point, 10-point, and the 30% rule

A service-connected rating, even 0%, gives you 10-point preference for federal jobs. At 30% or more, agencies can hire you without competition.

At a glance

5-point (TP)Served during a war period, in a campaign with an authorized medal, or specific date windows. No rating needed
10-point XPAny present service-connected disability (including 0%), receipt of compensation, disability retirement, or pension; or a Purple Heart. Also spouses, widows, and mothers in some cases (derived preference)
10-point CPCompensable rating of 10% to 29%
10-point CPSCompensable rating of 30% or more. Highest category; floats to the top of the list
30% or More Disabled Veteran authorityAgencies can appoint you noncompetitively to any grade, then convert to permanent. Bring your VA rating letter
Other authoritiesVRA (noncompetitive up to GS-11), VEOA (compete for internal jobs), Schedule A (for a documented disability)
FormSF-15 plus your VA letter (dated 1991 or later) and DD-214, attached to the USAJOBS application

Traps

  • Preference applies to hiring, not promotions.
  • Attach the paperwork every time. Missing SF-15 or VA letter means you get no points, and HR will not chase you.
  • Retired officers at O-4 and above generally do not get preference unless disabled.
  • fedshirevets.gov moved in 2026 to opm.gov/fedshirevets. Old bookmarks 404.
